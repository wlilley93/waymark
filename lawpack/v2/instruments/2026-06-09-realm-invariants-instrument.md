## The instrument text

> The kernel machinery of the realm, namely its invariants, obligations, agent rules, and specifications, is consolidated under this single instrument. The machinery is maintained as the working schedules of this instrument: the machine-checkable records under `lawpack/v2/invariants`, `lawpack/v2/obligations`, `lawpack/v2/rules`, and `lawpack/v2/specs`. The schedules carry force as part of this instrument and are loaded and enforced by the kernel directly. They may be amended from time to time by the established lawmaking route (route, permit, decision log, validation) without separate registration in the Gazette: the Gazette registers this instrument alone, and the schedules are read within it. This consolidation is registration practice only; it does not alter the force, severity, or effect of any scheduled record, each of which continues to trace its authority to the Act or assent event that enacted it.

**Kernel effect:** the Gazette registers legislation and case law only; the machinery (42 records today: 28 invariants, 5 obligations, 4 rules, 5 specs) is scheduled under this instrument and read within it; schedule amendments travel the route-permit-log-validate path; consolidation never alters force or severity.

## Effects on the register (on commencement, not before)

- This instrument's Gazette entry carries the schedules in full as its readable text, so nothing is curated out of the record.
