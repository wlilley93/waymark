**Forum:** Privy Council, constitutional first instance (odd bench of 3), the jurisdiction /

**Referring subscriber:** A subscriber. **Canonical / apex jurisdiction:** vjs.
**Result:** UNANIMOUS (3-0) for Construction B (the Court of Appeal sits at the canonical level).
**Bench:** a constituted odd bench of three; each seat's opinion follows in order. Permission to

## Opinion of Reid J. (lead judgment)

### Jurisdiction

This forum is properly seated. The matter is *routing* (where the Court of Appeal sits),
*jurisdiction* (which seat may convene and record), and *public/private boundary* (the
anonymisation of an up-referral) at the realm level. That is the exact grant in **[2026] VJS-SC 2
D2** (Privy Council, odd bench of 3, constitutional first instance). Nothing has been recorded to
the citator; the packet is staged only. No breach is before us; we declare the law prospectively.

### Q1 - Where the Court of Appeal sits. HELD: Construction B - the Court of Appeal sits at the canonical jurisdiction.

For the matter before us, and the class it represents, everything above First Instance refers up.
A subscriber seats only its First Instance (County Court) line for any question that (i) turns on
the *meaning of canonical VJS law* or (ii) is *generalisable / federation-wide* in effect. The
Court of Appeal (bench of 3) and the apex tier for such questions are convened and recorded by the
canonical jurisdiction (vjs) alone.

Reasons, grounded in canon. First, the drafters made the County Court, and only the County Court,
the repo-local tier: **[2026] VJS-SC 2 D1** designates the County Court for "repo-local operational
matters"; D2 places the Privy Council "at the realm level", D3 makes the Supreme Court "the single
central apex", and D4 speaks of the Court of Appeal without any repo-local qualifier. Where the
canon wanted a local tier it said so (D1). Its silence as to the Court of Appeal, set against the
express local designation of the County Court, is meaningful: the local floor is the County Court
alone, and the appellate ladder is realm/canonical. Second, a binding ruling on canon meaning must
resolve to the one series and single apex: **[2026] VJS-PC 10** binds every "binding question on the
MEANING of canonical VJS law" to "the Realm's one citation series and single apex; ... no parallel
series, local apex, or per-domain citator on canon meaning." A subscriber-seated Court of Appeal
recording a CA-SUBSCRIBER line on a canonical question is precisely a parallel series and a per-domain
citator. Third, the federation's design is one jurisprudence above the local floor:
**REG-FEDERATION-COORDINATION-001** ("one continued apex ... Every other (subscribing) jurisdiction
must refer up", and it prohibits "a_root_asserting_an_apex_or_final_court_function"). Fourth,
**[2026] VJS-SC 4** supplies the controlling principle and its direction: it re-characterised a
subscriber's self-record as a referral and, at D3, hardened the kernel so the mis-filing is
structurally impossible. The principle is *refer up*; the enforcement grows to meet the rule.

Why Construction A loses. Construction A is textually accurate about the *kernel proxy* and nothing
more. REG-FEDERATION-COORDINATION-001 and the gate name only "supreme/privy" because those were the
apex mis-filings that prompted VJS-SC 4; that enumeration is the worst case the gate was built to
catch, not an exhaustive grant that everything below it is permitted. *Expressio unius* cannot run
against D1's express (and exclusive) designation of the County Court as the local tier. The
constitutional rule is broader than its mechanical proxy. An under-inclusive gate is a gap to
close, not a jurisdiction to occupy.

Reserved (not decided): a subscriber appeal genuinely confined to repo-local operational matter,
touching no canon meaning, generating no federation effect, binding no peer. That hypothetical is
not before us and is strongly disfavoured by the no-parallel-series rule; I reserve it and note
that, on the present kernel (which cannot distinguish such a case), the safe default remains
referral-up.

### Q2 - Benefit-of-all rationale. CONFIRMED as design intent.

An appellate ruling on a generalisable question binds and benefits the whole federation: one
consistent jurisprudence, not N divergent local appeal lines. This is the affirmative ground of Q1,
sourced in **[2026] VJS-PC 10** ("unity of citator and apex with binding referral-up") and
**REG-FEDERATION-COORDINATION-001** ("one continued apex"). N parallel CA-<REPO> lines would
fragment the single jurisprudence those provisions exist to protect.

### Q3 - The First Instance / appeal boundary. CONFIRMED at the County Court.

The subscriber's repo-local First Instance (the CC-SUBSCRIBER County Court line, carried as binding *repo-local*
precedent) is correct and lawful. **[2026] VJS-SC 2 D1** (County = single judge, repo-local
operational). **REG-FEDERATION-COORDINATION-001** preserves peer local law (default subscription
lawful because revocable; it prohibits foreclosing a peer amend/pin/fork/exit). One caveat already
implicit in the canon: that repo-local precedent binds *the subscriber*, and may not bind or gate a peer
local law without adoption, nor settle canon meaning for the federation. First Instance stays local
at the County Court; appeal-and-above on canon-meaning / federation questions refers up.

### Q4 - Anonymisation of the up-referral. CONFIRMED: fully anonymised is the correct and required shape.

The packet that crosses into the canonical (shared/public) record must be the abstract legal
question + redacted facts + a pointer, with no subscriber-specific facts (no repo paths, client
facts, or concrete schema/verb identifiers). **ACT-005 s1** (must not publish client_facts /
private_repo_paths; redaction is the agent's duty), **s4** (local evidence referenced by pointer
only; the pointer must not expose the private detail), **s5** ("community contributions to the
public record are limited to anonymised summaries and system data; private facts from contributor
repos must not enter the public record"). The subscriber's staged anonymised packet is the right altitude.

### Q5 - Form of the returned ruling. CONFIRMED: abstract precedent, cited and mirrored; never a local CA-SUBSCRIBER.

The canonical appellate ruling returns as an abstract precedent the subscriber CITES (the central
series) and applies locally. It is never recorded as a repo-local CA-SUBSCRIBER. **[2026] VJS-PC 10**
(one series; no per-domain citator on canon meaning); **[2026] VJS-SC 4** (the subscriber holds a
referral and a citation, not a local apex order). The subscriber lawfully mirrors the canon order
read-only into its lawpack and cites it; minting a local appellate citation is the prohibited act.

### Q6 - Mechanism.

There is no dedicated up-referral verb in server_of_law. The conforming path on present canon: (1)
The subscriber files the anonymised packet (Q4) as a referral record carrying a non-apex,
non-above-County court field (e.g. court: county / a referral type), and transmits it to the
canonical forum's intake; its role ends there. (2) The canonical jurisdiction (vjs) drives the
verbs: `allocate` mints the next citation in the central appellate (CA) or supreme (SC) series from
the live register, kernel-minted, never hand-picked (**ACT-004 s8**); `convene` opens the court,
verifies bench size against **[2026] VJS-SC 2** (Court of Appeal = odd bench of 3), pins the
case-file digest, and refuses an under-strength bench; `record` enters the order and verifies
bench-integrity. (3) Who convenes: the canonical jurisdiction (vjs), as the one continued apex seat,
not the subscriber. A first-class "refer up" verb would make the path ergonomic; it is not required
to dispose of this matter.

### Q7 - Kernel enforcement. The kernel today UNDER-ENFORCES the rule; conformance is mandated.

Present state: `enum Court { County, PrivyCouncil, SupremeCourt }` has no CourtOfAppeal variant -
tolerable only while the tier is administratively non-convened (**[2026] VJS-SC 2 D4**); an appeal
has now arisen. `apex_routing_decision` blocks a subscriber record only when the court field
contains "supreme" or "privy"; a subscriber staging a court-of-appeal order would pass today. That
is the gap, and it does not license the conduct. The mandate (ratio): (a) generalise the predicate
so a subscribing jurisdiction may locally record only court: county; any tier above First Instance
refers up (add court_of_appeal / appeal to the blocked set), preserving the lawpack-mirror exclusion
so read-only mirroring of canon CA/SC orders stays lawful; (b) add the CourtOfAppeal variant to
`enum Court` so vjs can convene and record CA orders. The block operates as a refer-up /
route-for-correction, never a void - it respects the assented-record floor. Until the kernel is
conformed, the rule is held by this ruling and the agent's duty of care.

### Disposition (Reid J.)

Q1 Construction B. Q2 Confirmed. Q3 Confirmed (CC-SUBSCRIBER First Instance correct). Q4 Confirmed (full
anonymisation required). Q5 Confirmed (abstract cited precedent; no CA-SUBSCRIBER). Q6 As set out
(subscriber files anonymised referral; vjs convenes and records via allocate/convene/record). Q7
Kernel under-enforces; conformance mandated. Permission to appeal to the Supreme Court GRANTED on
R1, R2, R7.

## Opinion of Wilberforce J. (concurring)

I concur in full with Reid J. and add the framing that, to my mind, is decisive.

Construction A is not frivolous, and I record it honestly: REG-FEDERATION-COORDINATION-001, [2026]
VJS-SC 4 and the kernel gate each name only the apex, inviting an *expressio unius* inference that a
subscriber Court of Appeal is left unbarred. But that inference fails against the canon's structure
because subscriber authority is a positive-authorisation scheme, not a residual-liberty one. A
subscriber holds exactly the powers subscription law grants: run a County Court applying canon to
local facts, and refer up. **[2026] VJS-PC 10** grants "unity of citator and apex with binding
referral-up" and imposes the synthesiser bar; it grants no local appellate or interpretive bench.
The absence of a prohibition is not a grant. Within a subscriber that cannot hold supreme/privy, a
local Court of Appeal would be that jurisdiction's highest and final court - the prohibited "final
court function" of REG-FEDERATION-COORDINATION-001, even though it is not the apex.

I add a refinement on Q3: "First Instance" is bifurcated. Operational first instance (County, D1) is
repo-local and the subscriber's own. Constitutional first instance (Privy Council, D2 - this forum)
is realm-level and not repo-local. The subscriber's local ceiling is therefore the top of its County
line; every appeal, and every constitutional-first-instance matter, refers up.

On Q7 I stress the constitutional point: the Court of Appeal's absence from the `enum Court` has not
abolished it. **[2026] VJS-SC 2 D4** and **ACT-COMPUTER-FIRST-REALM** (s29) keep the tier alive in
law, "NOT abolished", with abolition requiring "express amendment of s.10 by number". Kernel code
cannot amend a statute; the enum omission is an implementation lacuna, not a repeal. Enforcement
scope is not the measure of substantive lawfulness; the gate is a backstop and its narrowness cannot
enlarge subscriber authority.

I would GRANT permission to appeal on two tracks: the routing holding (this ruling), as constitutional
architecture of the whole federation apt for apex confirmation; and the underlying merits, which are
not for this routing Council and which I direct up for substantive disposition in the central series.

## Opinion of Hoffmann J. (concurring; charged to test Construction A adversarially)

I was charged to test Construction A adversarially before settling. I have done so. Construction A
fails. The correct reading is B.

The case turns on one distinction the canon draws everywhere: application of canon to repo-local
facts (lawful locally) versus a deciding bench on the MEANING of canonical law (reserved to the one
apex). A County Court does the former; a Court of Appeal deciding an abstract, generalisable,
first-impression constitutional question does the latter.

Construction A rests on three footholds, each of which I have tested and rejected. (A1) "REG and the
kernel name only apex, so a sub-apex Court of Appeal is unblocked" is a residual-liberty inference;
federation law for subscribers is positive-authorisation, and **REG-FEDERATION-COORDINATION-001**'s
operative command is affirmative ("must refer up"). Naming the apex tier states the floor of what
must go up; it does not license seating every tier beneath it. (A2) "The kernel gate is silent on
court_of_appeal, so it is lawful": **[2026] VJS-SC 4**'s own ratio is decisive against reading
permission from kernel silence - capability is not authority; the bright-line is a floor of
enforcement, never the measure of lawfulness. The silence is an enforcement gap (Q7), not a
permission. (A3) "[2026] VJS-SC 2 D4 / ACT s29 say the Court of Appeal may be convened on a bench of
3 when an appeal arises, so the subscriber convenes its own": read in full, D4 fixes the tier's
*shape* (3) and *occasion* (an appeal arises), not that the *convening jurisdiction is the
subscriber*. Read with the entrenchment ("the singleness of the central apex may never be relaxed")
and with **[2026] VJS-PC 6** - which I located and which is squarely on point - the position is
settled: an appeal "creates no new tier, seat, apex, or citation series; the single apex is
undisturbed." A subscriber standing up its own Court of Appeal would create exactly the new seat and
citation series PC 6 says taking an appeal does not create.

The structural objection that defeats A on its own: a subscriber-level Court of Appeal deciding the
abstract question would be a deciding bench of its own on canon meaning (forbidden by the synthesiser
bar) and would mint a parallel interpretive series (forbidden by unity-of-citator, **[2026] VJS-PC
10**). N subscribers each running their own appeal line is the precise fragmentation the single apex
exists to prevent.

I concur on Q1-Q7 as Reid J. has set them out. On Q6 I add that the choice of which canonical tier
hears a referred-up merits matter (Court of Appeal vs Privy Council vs Supreme) is the allocating
jurisdiction's / Principal's call (**BOOT-003**), not the subscriber's. On Q7 I note the conforming
change is machinery giving effect to enacted law (**[2026] VJS-SC 2 D4**; **ACT-COMPUTER-FIRST-REALM**
s29 already fix the Court of Appeal at bench-of-3), operating as a Block / route-for-correction and
never a void, so it respects the assented-record floor; no Sovereign assent is engaged.

I emphasise the scope of what we have decided: this ruling determines ROUTING ONLY. The merits -
whether the real-world erasure right is satisfied by live-data nullification plus write-time
pseudonymisation plus access-restricted retention-under-exemption, or whether per-subject
crypto-shred is build-blocking - are not before us and must be decided by the canonical bench on
referral up. Because that question touches the non-derogable external-law floor and is foundational
and federation-wide, I direct that, on referral up, it be allocated to a tier no lower than required
to bind the whole federation. I would GRANT permission to appeal to the Supreme Court on the routing
ratio.
