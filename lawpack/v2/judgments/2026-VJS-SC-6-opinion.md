# [2026] VJS-SC 6 — Reference re Two Recorded Contradictions in the Canon

Supreme Court of the Vibe Justice System, constitutional sitting of five.
**Unanimous (5-0), Construction A on both questions.** Each seat's opinion is delivered in full below
under its own heading (s.18: judgment authored by counted members, no synthesiser-on-top).

On a reference by the Principal following a whole-repo audit (2026-06-26) that surfaced two
unreconciled conflicts among binding orders. These are questions of the System's own law
(reconciling its recorded orders), squarely within the court's competence.

---

## Bingham J.

**Method.** Two recorded binding orders that appear to collide are not in conflict if a reading
gives operative effect to each (harmonious construction), or if the collision dissolves on lex
superior / lex posterior, or if they govern different objects. Voiding is the last resort.

**Q1 (Court of Appeal).** There is a facial/citator conflict but no live substantive one.
BOOT-003's "No CoA in V2 MVP" means *administratively non-convened* — exactly what
COURTS-CONSTITUTION-001 (= [2026] VJS-SC 2) D4 records ("remains administratively non-convened in
the V2 MVP"). The only tension is BOOT-003's flat `create/route` prohibition against D4's reserved
power to convene "an odd bench of 3 if an appeal arises." The entrenchment clause forecloses any
reading of BOOT-003 as *abolishing* the tier (it is no express s.10 amendment by number); on the
narrow convening margin, lex superior (entrenched, sovereign-assented constitution) reinforced by
lex posterior subordinates BOOT-003's prohibition pro tanto. PC-19 operationalises D4 and is **not
per incuriam**. **Remedy:** an express supersession/qualification annotation in the citator; no s.10
amendment, no per incuriam.

**Q2 (empty bench field).** PC-13/PC-16/SC-5 make the *substantive constitution of the deciding
bench* constitutive (a genuinely benchless order is void ab initio, never softened); PC-8 makes the
*structured `bench:` field* validity-neutral and prospective. These govern different objects. The
test for "benchless" is substantive: an order whose opinion names a fully constituted bench is **not
benchless**. The nine orders, including the constitution, each name a full bench in their opinions →
**valid**. The constitution is additionally insulated: a rule enacted under the constitutional order
cannot annul its own founding norm. **Remedy:** declared **non-constitutive** back-fill of `bench:`
from the opinions (record hygiene, not a validity cure); the prospective/staged-diff gate is
affirmed and is not to be re-pointed at committed legacy orders.

## Hale J.

A constitutional court does not reach for demolition where construction will serve, and is slowest
to adopt a reading whose terminus is the destruction of the instrument from which its own authority
descends.

**Q1.** Construction A. "No CoA in MVP" is the constitution's own non-convened position; on the
residual convening margin the entrenched, sovereign-assented, later and more specific constitution
controls; BOOT-003's create/route limb is pro tanto spent; PC-19 is good law, not per incuriam. The
mischief is in the **record**, not the law: BOOT-003 carries `supersedes: []` and presents a
live-looking ban. **Remedy:** a recorded supersession annotation + a partially-superseded citator
flag on BOOT-003, with reciprocal cross-references from SC 2 and PC-19. Adjudication plus
annotation, deliberately not amendment.

**Q2.** The substance/field distinction is decisive. PC-13/SC-5 condemn a substantively
unconstituted tribunal; PC-8 makes the record field validity-neutral. PC-8 D3 reserved the power to
make a field constitutive to "primary law or the Supreme Court"; SC-5 (a Supreme ruling) honoured
that reservation **in form** but exercised it over *bench-integrity (substance)*, **not** over the
empty *field* — so the field-constitutiveness power remains unexercised, and the constitutive rule
does not reach legacy orders' empty fields. The nine are valid; the constitution validates itself
through its opinion-recorded bench and PC-8's grandfather, not by bootstrap. **Remedy:** a one-time,
expressly **non-constitutive, non-revalidating** back-fill from the opinions; a guard that any
future field-constitutiveness come only by SC order or primary law naming PC-8.

## Reed J. (charged to press the strict / constitutive reading)

**Construction B at full strength.** "Constitutive" is a word of existence; "void ab initio... never
softened" is expressly backward-reaching and anti-mitigation; the diff-time gate's silence is a fact
about the policeman, not the law. Pressed to its limit, B voids COURTS-CONSTITUTION-001 itself —
whence the courts it constitutes never existed, PC-13/SC-5 is itself a nullity, and the rule that
condemns the constitution annihilates its own source of authority. A liar's paradox; B saws off the
branch on which it sits.

**Why B does not compel.** It fails on independent grounds: (a) it **equivocates on "bench"** — the
*constituted bench* (a proper panel actually sat) versus the *`bench:` field* (a serde slot); PC-13/
SC-5 make bench-**integrity** constitutive (substance), PC-8 makes the **field** validity-neutral;
the nine have full named benches in their opinions and are bench-constituted, only under-recorded;
(b) PC-8's **express manner-and-form reservation** over the field was never exercised for it; (c) the
**reductio** — a construction that voids its own authorising source is wrong by that fact; (d) the
**assented-record floor** — a sovereign-assented entrenched instrument is route-for-correction, never
machine-voided. **The dividing line is substance, not vintage:** a *substantively* benchless order
(no panel, even bench, synthesiser-on-top) IS void ab initio whatever its date, and no field
back-fill could cure a panel that never sat. The nine survive solely because their opinions show
real benches. **Remedy:** validity-neutral back-fill from the named benches.

## Sumption J.

**Q1.** No operative conflict at the present state (both instruments agree the CoA does not now sit).
The latent, contingent tension ("if an appeal arises") resolves because the later law and the higher
law are the same law (SC 2: later + Supreme + sovereign-assented + entrenched). The entrenchment is
decisive against Construction B: reading BOOT-003 as a permanent routing ban permits **abolition by
starvation** of a tier the constitution lets be abolished only by express numbered amendment. PC-19
reaches the correct result under the controlling instrument and is not per incuriam. **Remedy:** a
declaratory supersession annotation; the law already exists in higher and later form — only the
record must be made to tell the truth.

**Q2.** Four independent grounds, any one sufficient: (i) the retroactive reading is self-refuting
(it destroys the source of its own authority); (ii) an order with a named bench is not "benchless" —
the predicate addresses panel integrity, not field population; (iii) the presumption against
retrospectivity + PC-8's grandfather (legacy orders took a settled validity; "void ab initio"
describes the *character* of an in-scope defect and "never softened" denies *curing*, neither
announces retroactive reach); (iv) entrenchment (an entrenched sovereign-assented instrument is
undone only by its prescribed manner). **PC-8 and PC-13/SC-5 are reconciled on different axes:** PC-8
governs temporal scope + reserved manner; PC-13/SC-5 govern the character of the defect *within
scope*. SC-5 exercised PC-8's reserved power **prospectively**; it could not, and on the
self-refutation argument logically cannot, reach back across the grandfather line. **Remedy:** a
ministerial back-fill (transcription of a recorded fact, confirming the bench, not creating it).

## Carnwath J.

**Q1.** Construction A, refined for the kernel. The `forbidden` mechanism is a flat diff-time
denylist (token present ⇒ block); it cannot evaluate "has an appeal arisen," so left intact it would
block every future constitutionally-authorised convening. Higher law must prevail at the level the
kernel reads. **Remedy:** annotate BOOT-003 `superseded_in_part_by: [SC 2, PC 19]` **and strike**
`create_court_of_appeal` / `route_to_court_of_appeal` from its `forbidden` list; the precondition
("an appeal has arisen") and locus ("the canon convenes," PC-19) are adjudicated at routing time,
not as a flat denylist. PC-19 affirmed.

**Q2.** The diff-time-only architecture **is** the prospective boundary and makes the holding
self-executing: the gate already checks every new staged order and never re-touches committed legacy
orders (the committed/staged line is the legacy boundary — no date lookup, no whole-canon scan).
Construction B would need a whole-canon re-verification pass the kernel does not have and which would
void the constitution — unenforceable and self-defeating. **The Constituted-Bench Invariant** (bench
non-empty; odd-length; **bijection** between `bench:` and the seats authoring the opinion; tier-size
per S-10/S-13) is enforced diff-time on staged orders. **Remedy:** route-for-correction back-fill of
`bench:` from the named opinions (Warning-level, registered in `ROUTE_FOR_CORRECTION_CODES`, never
Error/Fatal — it cures, never voids); the constitution, sovereign-assented, is route-for-correction
only.

## Unanimous disposition

**Q1:** Construction A. BOOT-003's CoA-prohibition limb is **spent / pro tanto superseded** by
[2026] VJS-SC 2 (COURTS-CONSTITUTION-001) D4 and [2026] VJS-PC 19, by lex superior + lex posterior,
reinforced by entrenchment; BOOT-003 otherwise binding as the MVP non-convened default; **PC-19 is
good law, not per incuriam.** Remedy: annotate BOOT-003 `superseded_in_part_by: [SC 2, PC 19]` and
strike `create_court_of_appeal` / `route_to_court_of_appeal` from its `forbidden:` list. No s.10
amendment.

**Q2:** The nine legacy empty-bench orders, **including the constitution, are VALID.** PC-13/PC-16/
SC-5 (substantive bench-integrity constitutive) and PC-8 (the `bench:` field validity-neutral +
prospective) are reconciled on different objects/axes; "benchless" condemns an unconstituted
tribunal, not an empty field where the opinion names the bench; the constitutive rule binds
**prospectively** on staged orders. Remedy: a **ministerial, non-constitutive** back-fill of `bench:`
on all nine from the benches named in their opinions, as a route-for-correction (never a void); the
diff-time/staged gate is affirmed as the prospective boundary.
