## Ratio (binding)

1. **One Gazette implies one canonical line (T1).** The canon consolidates onto the **established public repository** (the Gazette repo - reusing its README, public Pages Gazette site, `REALM-*` citator, and public presence), with the canon on **`main`**. T2 (consolidate onto the runtime repo) is rejected: it forfeits the public Gazette presence and drags the spent "v2" ordinal into the name of the canon, inverting Q6. T3 (keep separate) is rejected: it leaves two canonical-ish lines and splits the single-Gazette citation graph across two repositories, which Bill 32 s.16 ("one VJS Gazette continuing across V1 and V2") forbids. The repository name drops the spent "v2" ordinal per Q6, with an inbound redirect preserving existing links.
2. **An estate cannot rest on a bare branch.** The V1 Archive estate is anchored by an **immutable annotated/signed tag** pinning the V1 tree (the estate proper), exposed via a **protected, read-only `v1` branch** (reading-room only) and rendered as a **distinct published Archive directory** on the canonical Gazette build. A movable branch is too weak a boundary for an estate; a tag alone is too inert to publish from; the two together, plus branch protection, are the boundary.
3. **One Gazette of two estates, graph intact, V1 archival-source-only.** The single Gazette is built on the canonical line and carries both estates, including V1 law, so every edge from a transitioning V2 instrument to its `REALM-*` source resolves in one repository. V1/REALM records bear explicit, machine-readable estate markers (`estate: v1_archive`, `status: archival_source_only`, `live_authority: false`) and render in a visibly distinct Archive section. A citation to a V1 source is a **source edge, not an authority edge**: it keeps the graph whole; it confers no live force. V1 binds V2 only by express incorporation (s.8), never by silence (s.9; INV-NO-V1-GAP-FILLER); the estate boundary is operative by substance, not caption (s.16).
4. **The destination is settled now; the migration is STAGED (relief iii).** The correct repository and the safe sequence are not the same act. The physical byte-move is deferred to the governed Canonicalisation/Migration instrument and proceeds only on a **lock-preserving procedure**: no mass `sed` or blind move (fail-open un-scoping of fatal invariants is the worst failure mode); the `lawpack/v2/` path prefix is preserved, or a compatibility/redirect installed, so every fatal-invariant `scope.paths` glob still resolves; **each fatal invariant is proven to bind a non-empty set before and after**; the assented digest `8e1d3f51...`, the lawpack lock `4d2639cc...` and `COMMENCEMENT-V2-0001` stay pinned (a fresh lock is recorded under a **commencement addendum** if the tree changes; the old lock is never overwritten); the `[YEAR] VJS-ACT N` / `REALM-*` series and record IDs are carried verbatim. **If path-continuity and invariant-scoping cannot be proven at execution, the migration halts and the matter returns** - better two repos than a fail-open lawpack.
5. **Reserved to others.** The actual move, push, branch protection, tag signing, and any re-licence are the Principal's warranted acts; the Council settles the principle only. The AGPL adoption is the Principal's prerogative as copyright holder (the public repo is presently **unlicensed**) and is recorded by the Act, not decided here.

## The opinions, in full

### Adair J (institutional / public-boundary)
Verified the facts (public Pages Gazette live; repo unlicensed; 12 lawpack files embed `lawpack/v2/` paths; lock and assented digest pinned). Adopts **T1**: the Gazette is a public-boundary institution whose authority depends on the continuity and reachability of its public presence; stranding it on the runtime repo (T2) forfeits that and embeds the spent ordinal; T3 leaves two lines and splits the graph contrary to s.16. Holds an **estate cannot rest on a mere branch** - canon on `main`, V1 estate on an immutable signed tag exposed via a protected read-only `v1` branch and an Archive directory. Affirms one Gazette including V1 archival-source-only with the graph intact. Migration governed, non-disturbing; old lock stays pinned in the immutable COMMENCEMENT record. Notes the repo is unlicensed (bears on AGPL). **Vote: T1 variant; immutable-tag estate; Gazette-on-canon; governed migration; direct the Framework Act + raft.**

### Calloway J (operability / risk; the staging holding)
Verified the lock is a tree digest and that `INV-LAWMAKING-001/002`, `INV-005`, `INV-NO-V1-GAP-FILLER` carry literal `lawpack/v2/` globs. Agrees **T1 is the correct end state** (one canonical line; s.16 maps to one repo; the established repo holds the public Gazette) but holds that **destination is not licence to move now**: bare relief (i) is too thin and T3 abandons the right end state, so the operable answer is **relief (iii) - adopt T1 in principle and stage the physical migration** behind a lock-preserving procedure. Specifies the conditions concretely (no mass sed; preserve the path prefix or a redirect; prove each fatal invariant still binds a non-empty set before/after; fresh lock under a commencement addendum, old lock never overwritten; halt and re-refer if continuity/scoping cannot be proven - "better two repos than a fail-open lawpack"). **Vote: T1 end-state via relief (iii); protected read-only estate; staged lock-preserving migration; direct the legislature.**

### Devereux J (the Gazette and the citation graph)
One Gazette implies one canonical line: two repos make every cross-estate edge cross a repository boundary - "two registers stapled together," not one Gazette - so **T3 fails on its face** and **T2 inverts canonicalisation**; **T1 is right**. Holds the estate-vehicle must be a **protected, read-only archival estate** (protected branch + immutable annotated tag + distinct published Archive directory), not a bare branch. Gives the operative labelling rule that stops V1 reading as live: each V1/REALM record carries `estate: v1_archive` / `status: archival_source_only` / `live_authority: false` and renders in a distinct Archive section; citations to V1 are **source edges, not authority edges**; V1 binds only by s.8 incorporation. Non-disturbance per Harlan J/SC-1. **Vote: T1 governed migration; immutable-tag protected estate; one Gazette two estates with estate-markers; direct the Framework Act + raft.**

## Disposal

Reference ALLOWED, unanimous (3-0). **T1**: the canon consolidates onto the established public repository as the one canonical line (canon on `main`; repo name drops the spent "v2" with a redirect). The V1 Archive estate is an **immutable-tag-anchored, protected, read-only estate**, rendered as a distinct Archive directory. **One Gazette** on the canonical line carries both estates including V1 archival-source-only law, the graph intact, V1 citations being source edges not authority edges, with machine-readable estate markers. The physical migration is **STAGED** behind a governed, lock-preserving instrument and **halts and returns** if path-continuity and invariant-scoping cannot be proven. The Standing Committee is directed to codify in the Consolidation & Reconciliation Framework Act + raft (Migration-and-Incorporation carrying the estate-marker / source-edge / graph-continuity rule and the lock-preservation procedure; Gazette-Continuity mandating the two-estate build, distinct Archive rendering, and the immutable-tag pin; Canonicalisation carrying the governed rename + redirect). AGPL adoption is the Principal's prerogative, recorded by the Act. Nothing is moved, pushed, tagged, or re-licensed by this judgment. Recorded by Lexby as registrar.
