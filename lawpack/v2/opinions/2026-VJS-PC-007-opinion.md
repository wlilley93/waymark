# [2026] VJS-PC 7 - In re the Realm Invariants and Machinery Instrument 2026 (assent construction)

**Privy Council of vibe-justice-system, bench of three, first instance per [2026] VJS-PC 6 and ACT-COMPUTER-FIRST-REALM:s29. Registrar's own motion. Disposition: assent found, 2-1 (Justice C dissenting).**

The question: whether the Sovereign's entire and immediate reply "go", given in answer to the registrar's digest-pinned presentment of the Realm Invariants and Machinery Instrument 2026 (digest sha256:1be7181cbb3215fe3e1b540bb19915ab4ccd85d2e4eed146e8207d8e07ec1e7f) as the sole outstanding act, constitutes express, antecedent, external assent within CASE-LAW s.23 and the lifecycle gates of DEC-ASSENT-DRAFT-002.

## Opinion of Justice A (for the majority)

The floor asks three things of assent: that it be express, antecedent, and external. "Go" satisfies all three. It is an actual utterance, not silence or lapse; it precedes every machine act; it issued from the Sovereign's mouth, not from any organ of V2. The remaining question is referent, and the presentment answered it before the word was spoken: the registrar had executed everything else, named the instrument, recited the digest, and stated that only the Sovereign's word remained. An unqualified affirmative in immediate reply to a single outstanding offer is acceptance of that offer. The omnibus precedent already holds that minimal words suffice where the presentment fixes instrument and digest; "assent given" carried no digest either. CASE-LAW s.23(4) settles the rest: the assent rule binds the delegate and the agent, never the Sovereign. Magic words would bind the Sovereign to the registrar's form. I find assent.

## Opinion of Justice B (concurring)

I concur, on the source-of-force test. The negative covenant in CASE-LAW s.23 forbids any commencement condition satisfiable by the machinery alone. Nothing here is satisfied by machinery alone: there is a real, antecedent, external sovereign utterance, and this constituted bench performs the ordinary judicial office of construing it. Construction by a court of an act the Sovereign actually did is not promulgation by the machinery; [2026] REALM-PC 12 marks exactly that line. The registrar was right to decline "proceed all", which addressed a slate of pending work and had many referents. "Go" came after "everything else is done", when exactly one act remained, and the registrar had invited "words to the effect of" assent, not a formula. In that frame "go" has one possible meaning. I stress the outer edge: had anything else been pending, or the presentment been stale, I would hold. The fail-closed doctrine of s.23(5) is untouched; the allow-list is satisfied by a recorded, digest-pinned assent event quoting the Sovereign verbatim.

## Opinion of Justice C (dissenting)

I would hold the draft. The assent floor is a form rule precisely so that force never rests on construction, and today the majority rests force on construction. "Go" names no act of assent and no instrument; every particle of its meaning is supplied by the registrar's framing, which is the machinery doing the work the covenant reserves to the Sovereign. The omnibus words were minimal but they named the act: "assent given" is a performative of assent; "go" is not. Worse, the Sovereign spoke after being told what words would commence the instrument and did not use them; the natural fail-closed reading of s.23(5) is to treat that as words withheld, not words abbreviated. The registrar's own cured breach teaches that generic directives are never assent. The cost of holding was one more direct address; the cost of error is a precedent that ambient context can commence law. I respectfully dissent. The majority's confinement of the holding to this exact posture is, at least, essential.
