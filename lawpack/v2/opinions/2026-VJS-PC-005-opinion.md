# [2026] VJS-PC 5 - Opinion of the V2 Privy Council (Institutions, Repos House, and Vehicle)

**Court:** V2 Privy Council (institutional structure / governance / public-private boundary; bench of 3)
**Before:** Adair J, Calloway J, Devereux J
**Cause:** On Lexby's own-motion reference (the Principal directed "get a ruling first, maybe this needs legislation too").
**Disposition:** Reference ALLOWED. **Unanimous (3-0)** on the substance (Q1, Q2) and on the split vehicle (Q3). **2-1** on one drafting point (extend vs supersede the existing register; majority: extend). **2-1** against a separate DEC-INSTITUTIONS-001 (majority: the ruling declares the model).
**Registrar:** reduced to record by Lexby (CASE-LAW s.18(4)); independent seats, no access to Lexby's preference.

## Ratio (binding)

1. **Repos House: yes, but declarative, never sovereign (Q1).** Repos House is a lightweight jurisdiction registry and certification ledger that records, certifies, discovers, and publishes jurisdictions; **registration creates no legal force - local sovereign invocation and the lawpack lock do** (REG-INVOCATION-001). It decides none of the merits (whether a repo is bound, whether a local order is valid, whether private evidence is public, whether V1 binds V2). It has **two layers**: a private in-repo register holding operational facts; a public register holding **system-data only** (no secrets, paths, hostnames, logs, or evidence). Registration is **optional** for a private/local repo and owed only when it **federates, publishes to the Gazette, or claims canonical status**.
2. **Certification is a restatement of deterministic kernel output, never an office's judgment** (Calloway J's express limit; Devereux J). The fields `local_ci_pass`, `hooks_installed`, `boundary_scan_pass`, `permit_gate_pass` are deterministic gate/kernel outputs, never human attestations; the public layer is a derived, rebuildable, pointer-only projection. A "certification" that read as an office deciding a repo is bound/valid/canonical would be void (no office certifies a repository; Bill 20 s.7/s.10 carried forward).
3. **Repos House extends, does not supersede, REG-REPOS-REGISTER-001 (Q1, majority Adair + Calloway).** That regulation remains the lean mandatory-subscription floor; Repos House sits above it as the certification/discovery layer (non-disturbance, [2026] VJS-SC 1). (Devereux J would consolidate into one regulation that supersedes-and-extends by naming, to avoid two overlapping registry regs - recorded as the dissent on this point.)
4. **MoJ and MBES are dropped as mandatory institutions, paths, or runtime packaging (Q2).** Their functions are carried forward as records, roles, and registries: **MoJ -> Court Registry + Gazette Clerk** (clerical only, over the realm's one judiciary and the single Keeper of Constitutional Memory; no adjudication or curation); **MBES -> Toolchain Registry + Skills/Agent Registry** (derived, pointer-only, like the agent roll). The ministry **policy arms become a Policy Proposal record type routed by the existing kernel lawmaking route** (implementation / regulation / Act / Privy Council / Supreme Court) - proposal-only, no self-enactment, no new standing office. Nothing falls through; the functions with constitutional owners (the courts, the Keeper) keep those owners.
5. **V1 ministry names are archive lineage and optional ceremonial aliases only**, with no legal, path, or kernel effect (no crossing by silence; relabelling is prospective).
6. **Vehicle: split (Q3).** The institutional model (Q2 and Repos House in principle) is **settled by this ruling** as an application of the directory-agnostic / functions-by-role law; **no DEC-INSTITUTIONS-001** is minted (majority: a bare declaration adds force only by restatement and is surplus surface; Calloway J would have lodged it). The declarative registry **requires a regulation** because a public/private-boundary register needs a fixed, gate-enforceable schema a ruling cannot supply: **REG-REPOS-HOUSE-001**, subordinate under Framework Act s.7, is **drafted now but its build and lodgement are sequenced after permit enforcement and the lawmaking route** (the Principal's priority note, adopted unanimously - Repos House is not the critical path).

## The opinions, in brief
- **Adair J:** the substance is settled law; a ruling suffices for the model and the MoJ/MBES disposal; the one fresh instrument that earns its keep is REG-REPOS-HOUSE-001 (the new declarative content), which **extends** the existing register; non-critical-path.
- **Calloway J (concurring; the controlling limit):** Repos House survives only inside the rails - it records a status the gate already produced, never confers one; the order of operations (invoke -> govern -> optional register -> certify-as-gate-output -> public-safe to Gazette) is constitutionally mandatory; the registry needs a **schema-bearing regulation** (a ruling cannot supply an enforceable boundary); would withhold assent to the regulation unless "certification = restatement of the gate's deterministic output, never an office's merits judgment" is on its face; would also lodge DEC-INSTITUTIONS-001.
- **Devereux J (operability):** certification fields must be deterministic kernel outputs, not attestations; **consolidate into one regulation that supersedes-and-extends REG-REPOS-REGISTER-001 by naming** (avoid two overlapping registry regs); policy proposals route through the existing kernel route, no new office; DEC-INSTITUTIONS-001 is surplus; ruling now + regulation drafted-but-sequenced.

## Disposal

Reference ALLOWED. Repos House is retained as a declarative, non-sovereign, two-layer jurisdiction registry and certification ledger (certification = restatement of deterministic kernel output; registration optional unless federating/publishing/claiming canonical), **extending** REG-REPOS-REGISTER-001. MoJ and MBES are dropped as mandatory institutions; their functions are carried forward as Court Registry + Gazette Clerk and Toolchain Registry + Skills/Agent Registry, with policy arms becoming Policy Proposal records routed by the existing lawmaking route, and V1 names as no-effect aliases. The model is settled by this ruling; REG-REPOS-HOUSE-001 is drafted now under Framework Act s.7 and sequenced after permit enforcement and the lawmaking route. Recorded by Lexby as registrar.
