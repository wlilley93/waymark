## Leading judgment of Marbury J, for the Court

This Court, sitting as a Privy Council of three at constitutional first instance (2026-VJS-COURTS-CONSTITUTION-001 D2), answers a single question on the symmetric case file: may CASE-LAW, the entrenched V1 case-law document, be cited as live binding V2 authority? It may not. The bench is unanimous.

The governing law is plain. ACT-001 s.4 and ACT-COMPUTER-FIRST-REALM s.8 hold that V1 has live V2 force only by express incorporation stating v1_source, v2_destination, operative rule, kernel effect, and supersession; "a general reference or a citation for history is not incorporation." Section 6 prohibits import by implication; s.9 forecloses revival by silence. A reference of the form "CASE-LAW s.X" carries none of the statutory words of incorporation. It is a pointer to the archive, not an enactment. As Calder J puts it, this is not incorporation but implicit ancestry; as Ashby J holds, reference is not incorporation.

The vice is concrete, not theoretical. The citation form appears across the binding corpus: ACT-COMPUTER-FIRST-REALM s.23 self-references "CASE-LAW s.23(5)" in its operative remedy text; INV-ASSENT-SOURCE-001 repeats it; the courts-constitution order and s.29 cite "CASE-LAW s.10 and s.22(2)." This imports V1 as if live, breaches VJS-PC 4's settled cite-by-source convention, and breeds the very confusion REALM-SC 11 displayed when it purported to "enact CASE-LAW s.24," treating an untouched archive as a V2-extensible statute. It cannot be.

The remedy is the court's own settled procedural route, not delay. VJS-PC 4 rejects re-cutting the entrenched document but mandates a governed, meaning-preserving clerical re-citation off the lodged source-column register, needing no fresh adoption. We apply it. Every "CASE-LAW s.X" in the binding record is struck and replaced by its true V2 source: P/constitutional articles by their V2 statutory home (the assent floor to ACT-COMPUTER-FIRST-REALM s.23 and INV-ASSENT-SOURCE-001), court-made SC articles by their case, cited persuasive only. The principle-20 gate fails closed on a pseudo-section over a court-made article, in all records.

The saving is automatic and emphatic. Striking the citation FORM repeals nothing. The entrenched assent floor (an affirmative, fail-closed allow-list) dwells operatively in ACT-COMPUTER-FIRST-REALM s.23 and is enforced by INV-ASSENT-SOURCE-001, sourced to [2026] REALM-SC 10 back-filled per VJS-PC 3. The Sovereign's founding assent to Bill 32 stands. We amend pedigree, not substance: the rule endures, stronger for being housed in V2 statute and trace-anchored to its true source.

## Opinion of Marbury J.

CASE-LAW, a V1 archive document, cannot be cited as live binding V2 authority under ACT-001 s.4: citation alone is not express incorporation. The citation form "CASE-LAW s.X" appears 30+ times in the binding V2 corpus (assent floor, courts, invariants, orders) but is not incorporation; it implies V1 authority without lawful gate, breaches VJS-PC 4's cite-by-source principle for court-made articles, and creates confusion about V2's authority to amend V1 documents (as evidenced by REALM-SC 11's erroneous "enactment" of CASE-LAW s.24). The true V2 sources are already lodged: the assent floor is operative in ACT-COMPUTER-FIRST-REALM s.23 and INV-ASSENT-SOURCE-001; court articles are in ACT-COMPUTER-FIRST-REALM s.29 and 2026-VJS-COURTS-CONSTITUTION-001; all cite [2026] REALM-SC 10 (back-filled per VJS-PC 3) as true source. Strike every "CASE-LAW s.X" reference and replace by its V2 source per the VJS-PC 4 citation-map: P/constitutional articles by their true V2 statutory home, SC articles by their case. The entrenched assent rule is saved: it dwells in ACT-COMPUTER-FIRST-REALM s.23 (Sovereign Assent to Bill 32) and INV-ASSENT-SOURCE-001; re-grounding the citation clarifies V2 enactment and operability, does not amend the rule, and completes VJS-PC 4's directive to carry the split forward at the restatement step.

## Opinion of Calder J

The question resolves on the confrontation between ACT-001 s.4 (V1 is not binding without express incorporation) and the court's own settled procedural remedy in VJS-PC 4. ACT-001 s.6 and ACT-COMPUTER-FIRST-REALM s.8 close the implication door entirely. The live binding V2 corpus does cite CASE-LAW as if live authority (e.g. ACT-COMPUTER-FIRST-REALM s.23 self-references "CASE-LAW s.23(5)" in its operative remedy text), yet CASE-LAW is untouched V1-lineage archive. This is not incorporation; it is implicit ancestry. The assent floor itself is lawfully carried into V2 at founding—Bill 32 re-enacted its operative text verbatim, and the Sovereign assented—but the citation form orphans its foundational source-trail. VJS-PC 4's holding rejects wholesale re-cutting but mandates prospective re-citation by a "governed, meaning-preserving clerical pass" and lodges a resolver. That remedy is the binding procedural route, not delay. The P/constitutional articles (s.1,2,3,9,10,13,14,23 of CASE-LAW) are genuine Sovereign-enacted statute and do keep their sections, but that fact does not sustain the citation form for SC-sourced articles, which must cite by their case. The principle-20 gate fails closed on CASE-LAW s.X citing an SC article in new records—a court-made doctrine has no pseudo-section. The existing citations are legacy breaches of that standard. Striking the form (CASE-LAW s.23 replaced by ACT-COMPUTER-FIRST-REALM s.23 and [2026] REALM-SC 10) cures the implication and restores transparency: the rule itself (affirmative allow-list, fail-closed assent gate) endures intact, stronger for being housed in V2 statute (ACT-COMPUTER-FIRST-REALM and INV-ASSENT-SOURCE-001) and trace-anchored to REALM-SC 10. The saving is automatic: restatement preserves substance, amends only form and pedigree.

## Opinion of Ashby J.

CASE-LAW, an untouched V1 document, cannot be cited as live binding V2 authority. ACT-001 s.4 is clear: V1 binds V2 only by express incorporation; s.6 forecloses implication. A citation form "CASE-LAW s.X" is reference, not incorporation. The binding records cite it without the statutory words of incorporation ACT-COMPUTER-FIRST-REALM s.8 requires.

VJS-PC 4's cite-by-source holding applies equally to old and new records: court-made articles (the SC articles pinned to REALM-SC 1-10) must be cited by their case, never by pseudo-section. CASE-LAW s.23, whose true source is [2026] REALM-SC 10, should appear as "[2026] REALM-SC 10" or "ACT-COMPUTER-FIRST-REALM s.23" (its V2 enactment), never as "CASE-LAW s.23(5)." The principle-20 gate extends to all records, not only new ones.

The entrenched assent floor itself is preserved: the rule (affirmative assent-source allow-list, fail-closed) is embodied in ACT-COMPUTER-FIRST-REALM s.23, enforced by INV-ASSENT-SOURCE-001, and sourced to [2026] REALM-SC 10 back-filled per VJS-PC 3. Striking the citation form does not repeal it; it re-grounds it in V2 authority. P/constitutional articles keep their sections in their true instruments (Constitution s.1-3, etc.); only the archive reference is struck. The operative law endures; the citation changes from archive pointer to primary source. The Sovereign's founding assent to Bill 32 Part 12 stands; the rule is carried forward, transparently, into V2.
