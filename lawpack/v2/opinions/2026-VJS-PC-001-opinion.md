# [2026] VJS-PC 1 - Full Opinion of the V2 Privy Council

**Court:** V2 Privy Council (constitutional/jurisdiction/routing first instance, bench of 3; constituted by 2026-VJS-COURTS-CONSTITUTION-001)
**Before:** Adair J, Calloway J, Devereux J (three independent seats)
**Cause:** In the matter of V1->V2 Transitional Continuity, Project Onboarding, and the Council's own Jurisdiction
**Seised by:** the Principal's reference of 2026-06-09, through Lexby as officer
**Disposition:** Jurisdiction taken over Q1 and Q2-mechanism; Q2-policy declined; Q3 (constructive limb) leapfrogged to the V2 Supreme Court. Reference ALLOWED in part; the Transitional and Continuity Instrument directed through the lawmaking route as a void first draft. **Unanimous (3-0).**
**Registrar:** authored by the three counted seats on a symmetric file with no access to Lexby's preference; reduced to the filed record by Lexby as registrar, the decision pre-existing the prose (ACT-COMPUTER-FIRST-REALM s.15; CASE-LAW s.18(4)).

## Ratio (binding)

1. **Kompetenz-kompetenz; standing.** A constituted V2 court may determine the bounds of its own competence. Standing lies in the Principal's reference through Lexby as officer (real party, present stakes in live projects; not a bare advisory). The "no phantom forum" limit is satisfied: the bench is constituted.
2. **Jurisdiction allocated.** The PC is competent over the transitional-continuity doctrine (Q1) and the onboarding *mechanism* (Q2). The *policy* question whether any given project should migrate is the local Principal's reversible, low-blast call under ACT-007 and is **non-justiciable** - declined. Vesting and supersession are **fast-path/declaratory** (already effected by ACT-COMPUTER-FIRST-REALM s.6 and the courts-constitution order). The constructive **apex/federation reconciliation (Q3) is foundational** (entrenched s.15) and is **leapfrogged to the V2 Supreme Court**; a 3-judge bench will not gloss an entrenched apex rule.
3. **Transitional continuity (Q1) - the 1873 model, adopted as varied.** Transfer carries the estate forward; it does not restart it (concordant with REALM-SC 7: status found entry-by-entry, nothing voided wholesale by provenance). Perfected V1 orders and accrued appeal/review rights are preserved. The **"perfect by old machinery" limb is lawful only as bounded by the s.9 guardrail**: confined to matters already fully heard in V1 before commencement; effect limited to perfection in V1 form and publication to the Gazette (which creates no runtime force and is not enactment or incorporation, s.16); creating no new V1 doctrine; the V1 machinery used as a spent procedural shell, never as live law. Pending matters are **classified by a V2 transition court and then continued at their current procedural point** where a V2 analogue exists, given **fresh V2 framing** where continuation is genuinely unworkable for a computer-first successor, or recorded **moot/closed** where commencement itself resolved them - the default being continue-at-point, with reframing and mootness as reasoned findings on the record, not licences to restart. V2 procedure governs going forward; the V2 court may direct old-style procedure "as nearly as may be" as a transitional bridge. **No matter is void, restarted, or relitigated by reason only of the transfer.**
4. **Onboarding (Q2).** A project is onboarded to V2 by ordinary local installation under the local Principal's authority (ACT-007 s.1-s.2); no central court order is required (one is needed only to vary the canonical lawpack, s.3, or to fork with divergent lineage, s.6). A migrating project's V1 local precedent carries into V2 **by express incorporation by default** (s.8; barred from silent revival by s.9). A **recorded, revocable continuity election** is available to the local Principal - a wholesale carry-forward of settled local precedent at its current state, lodged in `.vjs/config.toml` and the local lawpack, **local-scope-only** (ACT-007 s.4): it binds no peer repo and cannot vary the canonical lawpack without the s.3 route. (Concordant with the persuasive archive REALM-PC 17 and REALM-PC 15.)
5. **Apex line (settled, pending the Supreme Court).** The V1 super-repo survives into V2 as an ACT-007 federation of peer local jurisdictions under one canonical lawpack and one continued apex. A federation/super-repo root may coordinate but may **never** stand up a second or parallel apex, and may **never** bind a peer repo's law beyond ACT-007 s.4 (s.15 entrenched; REALM-SC 6; REALM-PC 15). The residual constructive question - how far coordination may go before it hardens into sovereignty - is reserved to the Supreme Court.
6. **Instrument.** `REG-TRANSITION-CONTINUITY-001` is admitted as a **void first draft** (REALM-SC 8) and **directed through the V2 lawmaking route** (Standing Committee + assent), with mandatory amendments: (a) the s.9 guardrail written onto the perfection limb as a hard `must_not`; (b) the continuation limb rewritten as classify -> {continue | reframe | moot}; (c) the apex clause left as a placeholder reserving Q3 to the Supreme Court and asserting nothing of its own. It carries no force until it clears the s.14 constitutive gate and an `assent_source` resolving under s.23.

## Opinion of Adair J

A constituted court may rule on the limits of its own competence; that power is ordinary and not an arrogation. Standing: there is a real party with real stakes - the Principal, by reference through Lexby as officer, brings live projects awaiting onboarding and pending V1 matters whose forum is presently uncertain; that is not a moot academic question dressed as litigation, and the "no phantom forum" rule is satisfied because the bench is constituted. Competence is not uniform. Q1 is the paradigm jurisdiction/routing question, save that vesting is already effected (s.6 + the constitution order) and is fast-path/declaratory. Q2 is competent only as to the lawful mechanism; the antecedent policy - whether a given project should migrate - is a reversible, low-blast executive choice under ACT-007, non-justiciable, and I decline it. Q3 construes apex-singleness, which s.15 makes an entrenched constitutional rule; a 3-judge bench must not entrench, qualify, or gloss it, and I leapfrog it to the Supreme Court of 5/9.

On Q1 I adopt the 1873 model: preserve perfected orders and accrued rights; permit already-heard-but-unperfected matters to be perfected; continue pending matters in the competent V2 court at their current point; permit a procedural bridge; forbid relitigation by reason only of transfer - the just, tested rule, coherent with REALM-SC 7. The "perfect by old machinery" limb is the live danger and I harden it: lawful only if confined to matters already fully heard before commencement, limited to perfection and Gazette publication (s.16: no force, not enactment), creating no new V1 doctrine, the V1 machinery borrowed as a spent shell, never as live law. Where a pending matter has no clean V2 analogue, "continue at current point" yields to fresh V2 framing; matters mooted by the migration are closed, not continued. On Q2, onboarding is the local Principal's act (ACT-007 s.1-s.2); V1 local precedent carries by express incorporation by default (s.8/s.9), with an optional recorded, revocable, local-scope-only continuity election (s.4). I direct the Instrument through the lawmaking route with the two mandatory amendments.

**Vote (Adair J):** Standing YES. Q1 PC-competent (vesting declaratory). Q2 competent as to mechanism; migration-policy declined. Q3 leapfrogged. Q1 adopted as varied with the s.9 guardrail. Q2 onboarding + continuity election. Instrument directed.

## Opinion of Calloway J

Continuity is a doctrine of carriage, not of resurrection, and a computer-first successor diverges from its predecessor more sharply than the 1873 courts diverged from the old ones; that divergence is the pressure point, and I address it directly. Kompetenz-kompetenz is ordinary; standing lies in the Principal's reference; the reference is competent in form. But competence is not uniform: vesting and supersession are settled (s.6 + constitution order), fast-path; Q2-mechanism is competent; Q2-policy I decline as the local Principal's reversible call; Q3 construes entrenched s.15 and I leapfrog it, holding the line only that no second apex may stand up pendente lite.

I adopt the 1873 model but vary two limbs. The "perfect by old machinery" limb I bound on three faces - already-heard matters only; publication-to-Gazette only (s.16, no force); no new V1 doctrine - so it is a closing-out of the old estate, not a re-opening of the old engine; anything wider is the silent revival s.9 forbids. The "continue at the current point" limb I will not read flat: V2 is not a relabelled V1, and a long-prose case-law matter may have no compact V2 analogue. The just rule is classify-then-continue: a V2 transition court classifies each pending matter and either continues it at its current state, gives it fresh V2 framing where continuation is unworkable, or records it moot/closed where commencement resolved it. These are not relitigation - they are found dispositions on the record, which is exactly REALM-SC 7's discipline that status is found, not presumed from provenance. On Q2 I hold onboarding the local Principal's act, express incorporation the default, and a recorded revocable local-scope-only continuity election available, since forcing entry-by-entry re-incorporation of settled local precedent would be the relitigation the model condemns.

**Vote (Calloway J):** Standing established. Q1 PC-competent (vesting fast-path). Q2 mechanism competent; policy declined. Q3 leapfrogged (no second apex pendente lite). Q1 adopted, varied: limb-3 guardrailed, limb-4 classify->{continue|reframe|moot}, limb-6 anti-relitigation the keystone. Q2 onboarding + local-scope continuity election. Instrument directed with the two amendments.

## Opinion of Devereux J

Onboarding is the local Principal's ordinary act and this is not close: ACT-007 s.1 provides that installing VJS creates a local jurisdiction anchored at `.vjs/config.toml`, and s.2 sets default subscription; nothing requires a central court's leave. REALM-PC 17 settled the parent point in V1 (install/fork creates a local jurisdiction; default subscription is not perpetual subjection) and survives as persuasive archive concordant with ACT-007. A central order is required only to vary the canonical lawpack (s.3) or to fork with divergent lineage (s.6). On local precedent, Bill 32 s.8 governs: V1 material has live V2 force only by express incorporation, and s.9 forbids revival by silence; so express incorporation is the default. But to force every project to re-incorporate its settled precedent entry-by-entry would impose the relitigation the 1873 model condemns; the just accommodation is a recorded, revocable continuity election, with two mandatory guardrails - local-scope-only (s.4: it can never bind a peer or vary canonical law without s.3) and express-recorded (so it is not silent revival within s.9).

On Q3, the super-repo of REALM-SC 6 survives as ACT-007 federation: a coordinating root over peer jurisdictions under one canonical lawpack and one continued apex. REALM-PC 15 already held the super-repo to be realm-as-state, adding no new tier, seat, or apex. The single condition is the structural guarantor s.15 entrenches: the root may coordinate but may never assert its own apex or bind a peer beyond s.4. That anti-second-apex condition is settled law and fast-path; but the constructive question - how far coordination may go before it hardens into sovereignty - construes entrenched s.15, and a three-judge bench should not gloss it, so I leapfrog the residual limb to the Supreme Court while recording the settled condition.

**Vote (Devereux J):** Q0 - jurisdiction over Q1 and Q2-mechanism; standing established; Q2-policy declined; Q3 partly fast-path, constructive limb leapfrogged. Q1 adopt the 1873 model with the s.9 guardrail. Q2 onboarding the local Principal's ACT-007 act; express incorporation default + recorded revocable local-scope-only continuity election. Q3 super-repo survives as federation; anti-second-apex/no-binding-peers settled; constructive limb leapfrogged. Instrument directed as void first draft with the s.9 and s.4 guardrails.
