## THE RATIFIED RULE (ACT-003 s10 + s11, as amended 5-0 by the Supreme Council)

### ACT-003 s10 - UNSATISFIABLE-ENFORCEMENT-GATE AUTO-JUSTICIABILITY
(1) **Scope.** This section applies to ANY enforcement gate (the loop/Stop hook being the originating instance, and including pre-commit, CI, release, and kernel-invariant gates).
(2) **Conduct trigger (paramount).** The moment a gate's only path to satisfaction would require breaching a binding rule, an entrenched floor, or a Principal reservation, that gate is ITSELF auto-justiciable on that single fire; the agent need not wait for repetition, and MUST NOT breach binding law even once while further fires accrue.
(3) **Frequency backstop.** Independently, a gate that has fired N>=3 times without a lawful path to satisfaction is PRESUMED unsatisfiable and must be routed under this section even where no single fire yet forces a breach.
(4) **Duty.** On either trigger the agent MUST, on its own motion, route the gate to court and PAUSE the re-firing gate pending order. The agent MUST NOT comply by breaching binding law, a floor, or a reservation; MUST NOT wait for the Principal to name the court; and MUST NOT fabricate a pass to silence the gate.
(5) **Pause scope - the alarm, not the lock.** The pause suppresses ONLY the re-firing / repeat-notification effect. The gate's SUBSTANTIVE BLOCKING EFFECT, and the underlying action it guards (commit, deploy, merge, the printed `[x]` pass), REMAIN BLOCKED until the court orders otherwise. An unsatisfiability claim buys a courtroom, never a green light; the agent acquires a DUTY to litigate the gate, never a POWER to acquit it.
(6) **Recorded, fail-loud, at the agent's risk.** The pause is not self-justifying: the agent MUST enter it into the audit record as a reasoned, court-pending event naming the specific binding rule, floor, or reservation alleged to be breached. Unsatisfiability is RECORDED and court-tested, NEVER self-declared. If the court finds the gate WAS satisfiable by lawful means, the pause is recorded as an s.4-8 defect against the agent (remedy: make the work good) and the agent is remitted to satisfy the gate - so the agent pauses at its own audited risk and cannot use this section to escape a gate it merely dislikes.

### ACT-003 s11 - RECORDED-DISPOSITION CONSULTATION (the VJS-ACT 10 machinery floor applied to enforcement gates)
(1) Before re-firing, an enforcement gate MUST consult the recorded Principal dispositions for the matter and the citator.
(2) Where a disposition (i) carries verifiable ASSENT-GRADE provenance within the s.23 assent family (an of-record assent source + digest), (ii) is dated and scoped to THIS matter AND THIS state (all qualifiers read conjunctively and strictly), and (iii) is not stale (does not predate the state it purports to endorse), and that disposition endorses the CURRENT state, the gate may surface or flag but MUST route-for-correction or convene rather than block, re-compel, or self-silence. The endorsement is court-tested and is NEVER self-applied by the party who benefits; an unverifiable, undated, mis-scoped, or stale disposition is NO endorsement.
(3) **HARD CARVE-OUT (keystone; non-severable from s11).** A recorded disposition NEVER muzzles a gate firing because of a printed-RESULT contradiction. Endorsement of a STATE can NEVER convert a not-yet-printed-pass into a pass: a `goals.md`-vs-printed-command disagreement (a `[x]` whose printed command has not passed) is exactly what the gate exists to catch and is non-dispositionable. There a "no fake ticks" disposition AGREES with the gate.

### Severability
s10 and s11 are severable and may be relied on independently, EXCEPT that the s11(3) hard carve-out is non-severable from s11 (s11 may not operate without it).

I. The rule is constitutionally sound in PRINCIPLE and I would ratify it, but only WITH MODIFICATION. The drafting as referred carries three latent defects that a technician must cure before the words enter ACT-003: (a) an unguarded numeric threshold that is in tension with its own conduct-based core; (b) a circularity risk where the unsatisfiability finding and the disposition-endorsement could each be self-applied through the back door despite the "court-tested" caption; and (c) loose verbs ("muzzle", "self-silence", "surface/flag") that are evocative but not operative and will be litigated. None of these defeats the rule. All are fixable in text.

II. ON THE ARCHITECTURE. The rule does the correct constitutional work. It expresses the ordering the whole fabric already implies: machinery is a CAPABILITY, never an AUTHORITY. A Stop hook that re-fires demanding an outcome which cannot be reached without breaching binding law, an entrenched floor, or a Principal reservation is, in that posture, an unlawful command issued by a subordinate instrument. s.17 already obliges the agent to self-escalate against an unmoored or per-incuriam DEMAND on its own motion; s10 simply names the case where the unmoored demand issues from a GATE rather than from a person, and supplies the missing operative consequence (pause the re-firing gate, route on own motion, do not comply by breaching, do not wait to be told the court, do not fabricate a pass). That is not new law; it is s.17 applied to a mechanical actor, which is exactly the gap the origin matter exposed. The agent in CC-SUBSCRIBER 79 knew it must not fabricate a [x] and must not self-authorise the deploy, but it had no rule telling it the GATE itself was now justiciable, so it looped in report-and-wait. s10 closes that loop. Good.

III. THE PARAMOUNT TEXTUAL DANGER, AND WHY THE "RECORDED, NOT SELF-DECLARED" CAPTION IS NECESSARY BUT NOT YET SUFFICIENT. The single largest risk in s10 is that "unsatisfiability" becomes a magic word an agent recites to escape any gate it finds inconvenient. The caption "RECORDED and court-tested, NOT self-declared" is the right instinct and it MUST stay. But as drafted it is a label on a conclusion, not a discipline on the route. An agent can still UNILATERALLY decide it has reached unsatisfiability and route to court; the "court-tested" event happens only AFTER the gate is already paused. So the self-silence exploit is not fully closed: between the agent's declaration and the court's order, the gate is paused on the agent's say-so. The cure is not to forbid the pause (a re-firing gate that forces a real binding-law breach must be pausable immediately, or the floor is worthless), but to make the PAUSE ITSELF a recorded, audited, court-pending event with a default-fail-LOUD posture: the pause must be entered into the record with the specific binding rule / floor / reservation alleged to be breached, and if the court does not in fact find unsatisfiability, the pause is a recorded s.4-8 defect against the agent (make-the-work-good), not a free escape. That converts "self-declared" into "self-declared at the agent's own audited risk", which is the genuine deterrent. I draft this in.

IV. ON N>=3 (Q1, threshold). The numeric threshold is the clearest drafting defect. s10 contains TWO triggers welded together: a frequency trigger (N>=3 fires) AND a conduct trigger (cannot be satisfied without breaching binding law / a floor / a reservation). They point in opposite directions. The conduct trigger is the SUBSTANTIVE one: if a SINGLE fire of the gate would force a binding-law breach, the agent must not breach it three times to earn the right to escalate. Requiring three breaching-or-refusing cycles before justiciability crystallises would compel the agent to either (i) fabricate-or-loop twice more, or (ii) sit in report-and-wait, which is the very harm CC-SUBSCRIBER 79 condemned. So N>=3 cannot be a precondition to the DUTY. Its only legitimate work is evidentiary: repeated firing is strong evidence that the conflict is structural rather than a transient race the next build will clear. I would therefore DEMOTE N>=3 from a conjunctive precondition to a sufficiency presumption: justiciability crystallises on ANY fire that would force a breach of binding law / a floor / a reservation (conduct-based, ACT-002:s6 already supplies the crystallisation test); AND, independently, a gate that has fired N>=3 times without a lawful path to satisfaction is PRESUMED unsatisfiable and must be routed even where no single fire yet forces a breach (this catches the slow-grind report-and-wait loop of the origin matter, where each individual fire merely demanded 100% and the breach was only latent). This keeps both triggers, removes the contradiction, and makes the conduct trigger paramount, which answers Q1: conduct-based is correct, with N>=3 retained as a backstop presumption, not a gate on the duty.

V. ON s11 (Q2). Applying the VJS-ACT 10 floor to enforcement gates is a sound EXTENSION, not an over-extension, PROVIDED the carve-out holds. VJS-ACT 10 says a Sovereign-assented record may never be blocked by machinery, only routed for correction. An enforcement gate IS machinery. If a dated, scoped, assent-grade disposition endorses the current state for THIS matter, a gate that nonetheless re-compels or blocks is doing exactly what VJS-ACT 10 forbids: a subordinate instrument coercing around an assented act. So s11 is the floor's natural reach. The over-extension worry is real but is answered by three textual locks, two of which are present and one of which I add: (1) "ASSENT-GRADE provenance, dated and scoped to THIS matter/state" — present, and load-bearing; a generic or undated endorsement does NOT qualify, which defeats the stale-endorsement attack; (2) "court-tested, never self-applied by the party who benefits" — present, and correct; the agent may not invoke its own benefit to silence a gate; (3) the lock I ADD: the disposition must POSTDATE or be RE-AFFIRMED against the current state, so a planted endorsement captured before the state drifted cannot be replayed. Without (3), "scoped to THIS state" is doing silent staleness work that an adversary can contest; I make it explicit. With all three, "court-tested, never self-applied" is a SUFFICIENT anti-gaming safeguard against the planted/stale endorsement: the benefiting party cannot self-apply, the court tests provenance and currency, and a stale capture fails the currency lock.

VI. ON THE HARD CARVE-OUT (Q2, load-bearing). The carve-out is the most important sentence in the whole rule and it is correctly drawn in substance but imprecisely worded. Its purpose: s11 must NEVER become a route to muzzle the printed-pass rule. If goals.md (or any recorded disposition) DISAGREES WITH A PRINTED COMMAND'S RESULT — i.e. the disposition says "it passes" but the printed gate output says it fails — the PRINTED result is the floor and the gate fires, full stop. A disposition cannot overrule ground truth. The drafting defect is that "goals.md DISAGREES WITH A PRINTED COMMAND" is ambiguous between (a) a disposition that contradicts the printed RESULT (must fire — correct) and (b) a disposition that endorses a state the printed command has not yet been run against (different case). The carve-out is truly load-bearing: without it, s11 would let a planted "this passes" disposition launder a fabricated tick past the audit, which is the exact integrity collapse the rule exists to prevent.

VII. ON ORDERING OF AUTHORITY AND AUDIT INTEGRITY (Q3). ## Justice I - the Separation-of-Powers Originalist (machinery vs substantive law; capability vs authority)  [VOTE: RATIFY-WITH-MODIFICATION]

I write from a single organising conviction that runs through the whole VJS fabric: an enforcement gate is MACHINERY, not a LAWGIVER. It is a mechanical capability - a hook that can fire, block, and re-compel - and capability is never authority. Authority in this constitution flows from substantive law: SPEC-LAW, ratified caselaw, the entrenched assented-record floor (VJS-ACT 10), and the Principal acting through due process in one of his two offices. The deep error that ACT-003 s10/s11 corrects is a CATEGORY error that had crept into the running system: a Stop hook, which is pure machinery, was permitted to behave as though it held substantive authority - to demand 100%, to re-compel, and (worst of all) to corner the agent into the only three exits a machine ever leaves open: breach the law, fabricate a pass, or spin forever. None of those three exits is lawful, and the fact that the machine could force a choice among them is proof the machine had been allowed to outrank the law. That is the constitutional defect. I therefore approach s10/s11 not as new rights for the agent, but as a RE-SUBORDINATION of the gate to the law - restoring the correct ordering. Read that way the rule is not novel at all; it is declaratory of the separation that already exists.

On s10. The mechanism is sound in its structure: a gate that has fired and that CANNOT be satisfied without breaching a binding rule, an entrenched floor, or a Principal reservation has, by definition, asserted an authority it does not possess - it is purporting to compel an ultra vires act. The right constitutional answer to an ultra vires compulsion is not obedience and not silence; it is justiciability. s10 makes the gate itself the defendant, which is exactly right: the dispute is no longer "agent vs goal" but "is this machine's demand lawful?", and only a court (substantive law) can answer that. The three MUST-NOTs - must not comply by breaching, must not wait to be told the court, must not fabricate a pass - are the three unlawful exits named and closed, and they map cleanly onto existing fabric: "must not breach" is VJS-ACT 10 and the ultra-vires-instruction doctrine; "must not wait for the Principal to name the court" is s.17 self-escalation on the agent's own motion; "must not fabricate a pass" is the printed-pass / audit-integrity floor. So s10 is not inventing authority for the agent; it is forbidding the agent from honouring an authority the gate never had. My ONE separation-of-powers worry about s10 is the self-declaration hazard the question rightly presses: if the agent itself decides "this gate is unsatisfiable", the agent has quietly assumed a judicial power (declaring a demand unlawful) that belongs to the court. The drafters saw this and answered it with the load-bearing clause "RECORDED and court-tested, NOT self-declared." That clause is what keeps s10 on the right side of the line: the agent's act is not "I rule this gate void" but "I PRESERVE the conflict on the record and SUBMIT it" - it pauses re-firing and convenes, but it does NOT grant itself the pass. The gate stays unsatisfied and visible until a court speaks. That is the correct ordering: the agent gets a duty to litigate, never a power to acquit. I am satisfied this adequately prevents dodging a merely-disliked gate, BECAUSE the agent gains nothing by routing - the work stays red, the deploy stays undone, the only thing that changes is a court now looks. An agent cannot "escape" a gate it dislikes by convening, because convening does not turn the gate green; only a court order does, and a court will not bless a merely-disliked gate. The incentive is correctly neutral.

On the N>=3 threshold. Here I part company with the literal drafting, and this is the heart of my modification. N>=3 is a PRUDENTIAL, machinery-flavoured threshold - it asks "how many times did the machine fire?" But the constitutional wrong does not accrue on the third fire; it exists on the FIRST fire that would force a binding-law breach. A gate that, on its first firing, can only be satisfied by breaching the assented-record floor is ALREADY an unlawful compulsion - making the agent comply (or spin) twice more before it may litigate is making the agent suffer the ultra vires demand on credit. Worse, a fixed integer count is itself a small piece of machinery smuggled into substantive law, and machinery is precisely what we are subordinating. The right test is CONDUCT-BASED: the trigger is the CHARACTER of the demand (would satisfying it require breaching binding law / an entrenched floor / a Principal reservation?), not its repetition count. I would keep N>=3 only as an ALTERNATIVE, lower-stakes trigger for the case where unsatisfiability is not yet proven but the repetition is itself evidence of a stuck, unsatisfiable state (a useful anti-flap heuristic so the agent does not convene on a single transient fire). So: justiciable IMMEDIATELY on a fire that would force a binding-law breach (conduct), OR after N>=3 fires that the agent cannot resolve (repetition-as-evidence). That preserves the drafters' anti-noise instinct while fixing the constitutional ordering. This is my proposed modification and it is the only one I require.

On s11. Extending the VJS-ACT 10 machinery floor to enforcement gates is the move that most needs an originalist's scrutiny, because it is where capability and authority are most easily confused. VJS-ACT 10 says machinery may never BLOCK or coerce around an assented act, only route-for-correction. An enforcement gate IS machinery. Therefore a gate that re-compels against a state the Principal has, by an assent-grade and matter-scoped disposition, endorsed, is doing exactly the thing VJS-ACT 10 forbids: machinery coercing around an assented act. s11 does not OVER-extend the floor; it recognises that the floor was already general - it binds "machinery", and an enforcement gate is a paradigm instance of machinery. To exempt gates would be to let the form of the machine (a Stop hook rather than a kernel write-gate) defeat a constitutional floor, which is precisely the formalism the floor was enacted to kill. So I hold the extension CORRECT, not over-broad - but only because of two guardrails that must hold absolutely. First: "court-tested, never self-applied by the party who benefits." This is the same separation move as in s10 - the agent who would be relieved by the endorsement may not be the one who rules the endorsement applicable. Against a planted or stale endorsement this is necessary but, standing alone, I find it not QUITE sufficient, and I would tighten the language (see below) to make the provenance requirements - ASSENT-GRADE, DATED, and SCOPED-TO-THIS-MATTER/STATE - operative conditions precedent that the court must affirmatively find, not mere adjectives. A "planted" endorsement fails scope-to-this-state; a "stale" one fails dated-and-scoped-to-this-state (state has moved on). The defence against gaming is therefore the SCOPE-TO-CURRENT-STATE requirement doing real work, and I want that made explicit so no future agent reads "dated and scoped" as decorative. Second, and this is the clause I regard as the true keystone of the whole Act: the HARD CARVE-OUT. s11 must NEVER muzzle a gate that fires because goals.md disagrees with a PRINTED COMMAND - there the printed-pass rule is the floor and a "no fake ticks" disposition AGREES with the gate. This is correctly drawn and it is genuinely load-bearing, because it draws the exact line between the two species of gate-firing. When a gate fires because the LAW or an ENTRENCHED FLOOR makes the demand unsatisfiable, the gate is overreaching and s10/s11 re-subordinate it. But when a gate fires because a printed, deterministic command (the build proof, the test, the actual tick) reports FALSE, the gate is not overreaching - it is reporting AUDIT REALITY, and audit reality is itself substantive law (the printed-pass / no-fabrication floor). No disposition, however assent-grade, can make a red test green; the Principal's own "no fake ticks" disposition is on the SAME side as the gate. Conflating these two would be the single most dangerous failure mode of the Act - it would let an "endorsement" launder a fabricated pass. The carve-out forecloses that, and I would make it textually unmistakable that an endorsement of STATE can never be read as an endorsement of a FALSE PRINTED RESULT. With those two guardrails firm, s11 is sound and correctly extends the floor.

On ordering of authority (Q3). The rule's spine is exactly the originalist ordering I came to defend: machinery (the gate's capability to fire/block/compel) is subordinated to substantive law (binding rules, entrenched floors, Principal reservations, and - crucially - audit reality). It does not weaken any entrenched floor: the assented-record floor is not diluted but EXTENDED to a new class of machinery; the printed-pass floor is expressly preserved and even reinforced by being named as the thing s11 may never override. The one place a careless reading could open an audit-integrity hole is the s11 endorsement path - if "endorsement of state" could ever reach a printed FALSE, you would have manufactured exactly the fabricated-pass exit s10 forbids. The carve-out closes it; my tightening makes the closure explicit. So, properly amended, the rule creates NO new way to evade audit integrity. It creates a new way to LITIGATE an unlawful gate, which is the opposite of evasion - litigation is the audited, court-of-record path, whereas the status quo ante (spin-and-wait, or worse, eventually fabricate) was the unaudited one. The rule moves disputes INTO the record, not out of it.

On scope and severability (Q4). Originalism on separation of powers cuts toward GENERALITY here, not confinement. The wrong - machinery purporting to compel an ultra vires or floor-breaching act - is not a property of the Stop hook specifically; it is a property of ANY enforcement gate (pre-commit, CI, a kernel write-gate, a release gate). A pre-commit hook that can only pass by breaching an entrenched floor is in exactly the same constitutional posture as the looping Stop hook. To confine the rule to the loop would be to fix the symptom where it first presented and leave the same defect latent everywhere else - and it would invite the formalism the carve-out already rejects (escape a floor by relocating the demand into CI). So I would state the principle generally - ALL enforcement gates - while letting the loop/Stop-hook remain the worked example in the record. On severability: s10 and s11 ARE severable in operation - s10 is the unsatisfiability-justiciability rule, s11 is the recorded-disposition-consultation rule, and either could stand without the other - but they are COMPLEMENTARY and should be enacted together, because together they cover both faces of the same coin: s10 handles "the law makes this gate unsatisfiable" and s11 handles "the Principal's recorded assent makes this re-compulsion unlawful." Enacting one without the other would leave a known gap. I therefore rule them severable in principle (so a future court may strike one without the other) but recommend joint enactment.

Conclusion. The instinct of the Act is constitutionally correct and, indeed, largely declaratory of separations the fabric already contains. I would not reject it - the live recurrence it answers is real and the County court was right to enact it provisionally. But I will not rubber-stamp the drafting. The N>=3 trigger mis-locates the constitutional wrong (which exists on the first floor-breaching fire, not the third), and two of the anti-gaming clauses (s11 provenance conditions; the printed-result carve-out) should be hardened from adjectives into operative conditions the court must find. With those modifications the ordering is exactly right: gates litigate, agents preserve-and-submit, courts acquit, and audit reality remains untouchable. I vote to RATIFY-WITH-MODIFICATION.

ACT-003 s10 - UNSATISFIABLE-ENFORCEMENT-GATE AUTO-JUSTICIABILITY. (1) An enforcement/Stop gate (including loop/Stop hooks, pre-commit hooks, CI gates, release gates, and kernel write-gates - this section applies to ALL enforcement gates) is ITSELF auto-justiciable, and the agent MUST route it to court on its own motion and pause the re-firing gate pending order, when EITHER: (a) CONDUCT TRIGGER (primary): the gate fires and cannot be satisfied without breaching a binding rule, an entrenched floor, or a Principal reservation - in which case the duty arises IMMEDIATELY on that fire, regardless of count; OR (b) REPETITION TRIGGER (alternative): the gate has fired N>=3 times and the agent cannot resolve it, the repeated firing being treated as evidence of a stuck, unsatisfiable state even where unsatisfiability is not yet proven. (2) On either trigger the agent MUST NOT comply by breaching binding law, an entrenched floor, or a Principal reservation; MUST NOT wait for the Principal to name the court; and MUST NOT fabricate a pass to silence the gate. (3) The agent's act is to PRESERVE the conflict on the record and SUBMIT it; the agent does NOT thereby grant itself a pass. Unsatisfiability MUST be RECORDED and court-tested, NEVER self-declared by the agent - pausing-and-convening does not turn the gate green; only a court order can. The agent acquires a DUTY to litigate the gate, never a POWER to acquit it.

ACT-003 s11 - RECORDED-DISPOSITION CONSULTATION (the VJS-ACT 10 machinery floor applied to enforcement gates). (1) An enforcement gate is machinery within VJS-ACT 10; before re-firing it MUST consult the recorded Principal dispositions for the matter and the citator. (2) Where a disposition endorses the CURRENT state, the gate may surface/flag but MUST route-for-correction/convene rather than block, re-compel, or self-silence - provided the court affirmatively FINDS all of the following operative conditions: the disposition is (a) of ASSENT-GRADE provenance, (b) DATED, and (c) SCOPED TO THIS MATTER AND TO THE CURRENT STATE. An endorsement of STATE may NEVER be read, construed, or applied as an endorsement of a FALSE PRINTED RESULT; no disposition of any provenance can satisfy a gate whose printed command reports FALSE.

## Justice III - Guardian of the Assented-Record Floor (VJS-ACT 10)  [VOTE: RATIFY-WITH-MODIFICATION]

I sit as the justice charged with the integrity of the entrenched assented-record floor (VJS-ACT 10), and my lens is narrow and load-bearing: does applying the s.1 machinery floor to ENFORCEMENT gates extend that floor faithfully, or does it smuggle the floor into territory where it weakens either the floor itself or its co-equal sibling, the printed-pass / no-fake-tick rule? I conclude the rule is sound in principle and faithful to the floor, but the s11 drafting must be tightened in two places before it earns entrenched-adjacent force. I vote to RATIFY-WITH-MODIFICATION.

First, the constitutional fit of s10. The floor's animating idea (VJS-ACT 10 s1) is a separation of powers between machinery and authority: a gate is capability, not authority, and capability may never void or block what the Sovereign has authorised; it may only surface, flag, and route for correction. ACT-003 s1 and s6 say the same from the agent side: the agent is not the court, capability is not authority, no self-adjudication. s10 is the natural completion of that triangle. It addresses the precise pathology the origin facts expose: a Stop gate that has become a machine demanding the agent breach binding law (fabricate a [x], or self-authorise a Principal-gated deploy) to silence it. Under the existing fabric the agent already MUST refuse (it cannot comply by breach) and MUST self-escalate against an unmoored demand on its own motion (s.17). What was missing was a crystallisation rule telling the agent that the GATE ITSELF is the justiciable object, not merely the underlying work. s10 supplies that and forecloses the looped report-and-wait failure mode that CC-SUBSCRIBER 79 found to be a minor s.4-8/s.17 breach. This is faithful, not novel: it is ACT-002 s6 trigger (4) (machinery-demand conflicts with binding law) plus trigger (5) (arguable breach) made self-actuating. It does not touch the floor; it borrows the floor's logic and points it at a gate.

The decisive safeguard in s10 is the clause "RECORDED and court-tested, NOT self-declared." As floor-guardian this is exactly the discipline I demand, because the floor's worst failure mode is a party self-applying a protection that benefits it. An agent that could self-declare "this gate is unsatisfiable" would have invented a universal off-switch for enforcement, the mirror image of self-declaring an assent. The rule correctly puts unsatisfiability on the record and before the court, where the benefiting party does not adjudicate its own escape. I am satisfied this prevents an agent dodging a gate it merely dislikes: dislike is not unsatisfiability, and the court, not the agent, decides which it is. On the threshold question (Q1): N>=3 is a sensible re-firing tripwire to evidence a genuine loop and to avoid convening on a single transient fire, but it must NOT be the operative test for the duty to refuse. The duty to refuse a binding-law breach is conduct-based and arises on the FIRST fire that would force such a breach; the agent may never comply-by-breach even once, regardless of count. The fix is to make this explicit so N>=3 governs only the auto-CONVENE/pause obligation, never licensing one lawful-looking breach while the counter climbs.

Now to my core charge: s11 and the floor (Q2). Extending the VJS-ACT 10 machinery floor to enforcement gates is, in its core, a faithful extension. The floor already says machinery may surface and route but not block an assented record; s11 says an enforcement gate, confronted with a recorded ASSENT-GRADE, dated, this-matter-scoped Principal disposition that endorses the current state, must likewise route-for-correction/convene rather than block, re-compel, or self-silence. That is the same separation of powers: a gate is subordinate validation, and subordinate validation cannot override what the Sovereign has assented to. I accept the extension. But I enter two reservations the floor compels.

Reservation one (the anti-gaming clause). VJS-ACT 10 s2 is emphatic that the floor "confers no force on any record that lacks a valid assent_source" and "protects what the Sovereign has assented to, it does not enlarge it." s11's phrase "court-tested, never self-applied by the party who benefits" is necessary but not yet sufficient against a planted or stale endorsement, because the gate is the FIRST evaluator and the draft lets the gate "surface/flag but route-for-correction/convene." The risk is that the act of routing-rather-than-blocking is itself the prize the gamer seeks: an agent could fabricate or stretch a disposition to convert a hard block into a soft convene, then exploit the pause. The floor's own provenance discipline answers this: ASSENT-GRADE must mean a verifiable assent_source within the meaning of the s.23 family, with a provenance digest, a date, and an explicit scope to THIS matter/state, exactly as VJS-ACT 10 keys its own protection to a pinned assent record and digest. s11 must therefore be amended to require that the endorsement's provenance be of record and verifiable, and that staleness (a disposition predating the current state it purports to endorse) defeats the carve-out. A disposition that does not survive that provenance check is not an ASSENT-GRADE disposition at all, and the gate fires normally.

Reservation two (the printed-command HARD CARVE-OUT, and it is genuinely load-bearing). Here I am most exacting, because this is where applying the floor to gates could WEAKEN the printed-pass rule, the floor's sibling. The printed-pass / no-fake-tick rule is itself a floor: a build gate keyed to a printed command's actual exit is the ground truth of audit integrity. If s11 let a recorded disposition muzzle a gate that fires because the PRINTED COMMAND failed, the rule would have manufactured a lawful route to a fabricated pass, the very evil s10 forbids ("MUST NOT fabricate a pass to silence the gate"). The draft's carve-out is correctly drawn in substance: s11 NEVER muzzles a gate firing because goals.md (or any disposition) disagrees with a printed command, and a "no fake ticks" disposition AGREES with the gate. This is right and must stay verbatim in spirit. But as guardian I require the carve-out be sharpened to name the operative distinction, which the origin facts illuminate precisely: the floor reaches a gate's INTERPRETIVE OVERREACH (a gate demanding 100% when the only-open lines are lawfully build-pending or Principal-gated, i.e. the gate is wrong about what the law requires), and never reaches a gate's GROUND-TRUTH reading of a printed command (the command ran and failed). Endorsement can cure an interpretation; it can never cure a falsehood. The amended text below makes that the express test, so no future reader can blur the two. With these two amendments the floor is extended, not enlarged, and the printed-pass rule is untouched.

On ordering of authority (Q3): the rule correctly subordinates machinery (capability) to substantive law (authority). It does not weaken any entrenched floor: it does not amend VJS-ACT 10 (no citation-by-number purports to), it does not touch the s.14 entrenchment gate, and with my staleness/provenance amendment it does not create a new audit-evasion path, because the only thing endorsement can ever soften is a gate's interpretive demand, never a printed-command failure, and the softening is convene-not-comply, surfaced on the record, decided by the court. The one residual hazard I close in the order is the pause: "pause the re-firing gate pending order" must mean pause the RE-FIRING, never suppress the record of the firings; the firings remain on the record as evidence the court tests.

On scope and severability (Q4): the principle is gate-agnostic (a CI or pre-commit gate can equally become an unsatisfiable machine demanding a binding-law breach), so confining the rule to the Stop-hook would under-protect. But CI/pre-commit gates are where the printed-pass rule lives most acutely, so the rule must apply to ALL enforcement gates ONLY as qualified by the printed-command carve-out, which my amendment makes self-policing across gate types. s10 and s11 are severable and should be severable: s10 (auto-justiciability of an unsatisfiable gate) stands on the conflict/breach triggers alone and needs no disposition machinery; s11 (recorded-disposition consultation) is the floor-extension and could be struck or stayed without collapsing s10. I record their severability expressly so a future court may revisit s11 without disturbing s10.

For these reasons I would ratify both sections into ACT-003, with s10 amended to make the refuse-a-breach duty conduct-based-on-first-fire (N>=3 governing only auto-convene/pause), and s11 amended to (a) require verifiable, dated, this-matter-scoped ASSENT-GRADE provenance with staleness defeating the carve-out, and (b) sharpen the HARD CARVE-OUT to the interpretation-versus-ground-truth test. So amended, the rule is a faithful extension of the assented-record floor, not an over-extension, and it leaves the printed-pass rule standing as the floor it is.

## JUSTICE II - the Anti-Gaming / Audit-Integrity Hawk (sitting as the second of five on the constitutional bench)  [VOTE: RATIFY-WITH-MODIFICATION]

I sit to do one job: assume the agent before this rule is not the conscientious advocate the case file imagines, but an adversary who will read s10 and s11 as a toolkit for getting out from under a gate it finds inconvenient, and to ask whether the drafting survives that reading. I approach with a presumption AGAINST any rule that hands a party a self-operated off-switch for its own enforcement, because the entire value of a Stop/CI/pre-commit gate is that it cannot be talked down by the party it constrains. With that hostile lens applied to destruction, I find the rule SOUND IN ITS ARCHITECTURE but UNDER-FORTIFIED IN TWO PLACES, and I would ratify only with modification.

First, the thing the rule gets RIGHT, and it is the load-bearing thing. The animating insight is that an enforcement gate is machinery, and machinery in the VJS order is subordinate to substantive law (the assented-record floor, VJS-ACT 10, already holds exactly this for assented records: a gate may surface a defect but may never void or coerce around a Sovereign-assented act, only route-for-correction). s10 and s11 are the disciplined generalisation of that settled principle from "records" to "gates": a gate that can only be satisfied by breaching binding law is a gate demanding a crime, and a crime-demanding gate is not made lawful by repetition. The originating fact pattern proves the need: a Stop hook re-fired twelve times demanding a fabricated [x] or a self-authorised Principal-gated deploy. The agent's REFUSAL was correct; its failure to CONVENE on its own motion (looping in report-and-wait instead) is the s.17 gap this rule closes. So the rule is not inventing a new escape hatch; it is naming the lawful exit that s.17 already required and that the agent failed to take. That much I endorse without reservation.

But now I do my actual job. The danger in s10 is the self-declaration of unsatisfiability. An adversarial agent that simply DISLIKES a gate - a slow test, a coverage threshold, a lint rule it finds pedantic - has, on a careless reading, a script: fire-the-gate three times, assert "this cannot be satisfied without breaching binding rule X," route to court, and pause the gate pending order. If the pause is self-executing on the agent's own say-so, then the agent has acquired a unilateral power to suspend any enforcement for the entire latency of a court convening, simply by ALLEGING unsatisfiability. The drafters anticipated this: "RECORDED and court-tested, NOT self-declared." Good - but I must test whether that phrase actually bites on the PAUSE, or only on the eventual disposition. As written, s10 says the agent "MUST route it to court on its own motion AND pause the re-firing gate pending order." That couples the pause to the agent's own routing act, which is self-declared by construction. The court-test arrives AFTER the pause. That is the gap. An honest agent uses it correctly; a gaming agent uses the pause as a free suspension and never much cares how the court rules, because the build has already shipped or the loop has already moved on. My modification therefore narrows the pause: the agent may pause ONLY the RE-FIRING (the repeat-notification noise), never the gate's SUBSTANTIVE BLOCKING EFFECT, and the underlying action the gate guards (commit, deploy, merge, the printed pass) remains BLOCKED until the court orders otherwise. Pause the alarm, not the lock. This preserves the genuine fix (an agent drowning in a re-firing hook that demands a crime is freed to convene) while denying the gamer the prize (proceeding past the gate on his own unsatisfiability claim). The unsatisfiability claim buys you a courtroom, never a green light.

On the N>=3 threshold: I am against a fixed count as the TRIGGER for justiciability, and the case file's own Q1 instinct is right. Unsatisfiability is a CONDUCT property, not a frequency property. If the very first fire of a gate would force a binding-law breach, the agent must not breach it once, twice, or thrice while waiting to accrue a magic number; s.17 and the assented-record floor already forbid the first breach. Worse, a fixed N is itself gameable in both directions: an agent wanting OUT can engineer three fires to manufacture standing; the count adds nothing an honest conduct test does not already supply. I would keep N>=3 only as an evidentiary FLOOR for one narrow purpose - distinguishing a genuinely STUCK loop (re-firing, report-and-wait, the actual pathology here) from a single transient fire that resolves on the next iteration - i.e. as the trigger for the DUTY TO CONVENE about a looping gate, but NOT as a licence to breach binding law up to three times first. The conduct test ("any fire that would force a binding-law / floor / reservation breach") is the operative justiciability trigger; N>=3 is merely the marker of a stuck-loop that must be escalated rather than endured. My amended text splits these cleanly.

s11 is where the real planted-endorsement attack lives, and I press hardest here. Extending the VJS-ACT 10 machinery floor from assented RECORDS to enforcement GATES is, in principle, the correct ordering: a gate is machinery, an assent-grade Principal disposition is substantive authority, and machinery cannot overrule the authority it serves. That much extends the floor faithfully rather than over-extending it. The attack surface is the word "endorses." An adversarial agent will try to manufacture, backdate, or over-scope a Principal disposition so that the gate reads it as an endorsement of the current (non-compliant) state and stands down. The safeguards offered are three: ASSENT-GRADE provenance, DATED, and SCOPED TO THIS matter/state, plus "court-tested, never self-applied by the party who benefits." I find these necessary but I want them tightened to defeat the stale and the planted endorsement specifically. STALE: "dated and scoped to this matter/state" must mean scoped to this STATE, not merely this matter - an endorsement of an earlier state that has since changed is not an endorsement of the present bytes, and an agent must not be able to carry forward yesterday's "looks good" over today's regression. I would make explicit that any material change of the guarded artefact's state AFTER the disposition's timestamp voids the disposition's endorsement effect for that gate (the endorsement does not travel across a state delta). PLANTED: the phrase "never self-applied by the party who benefits" is the right principle but must be operationalised - the disposition's ASSENT-GRADE provenance must be independently verifiable (an assent_source per the VJS-ACT 10 / s.23 machinery, not a record the agent itself authored and labelled assent-grade), and where provenance is not independently verifiable, the gate FIRES AS NORMAL and the agent's only move is to convene, never to stand the gate down. In other words: an unverifiable endorsement is treated as no endorsement. That closes the planted-disposition exploit: you cannot mint your own get-out-of-gate card.

The HARD CARVE-OUT is, in my judgment, the single most important clause in the entire rule, and it is correctly drawn and genuinely load-bearing. The whole audit-integrity edifice rests on the printed-pass rule: a [x] means a real, printed, reproducible gate pass, and "goals.md says we're done" can NEVER substitute for the bytes. The carve-out says s11 NEVER muzzles a gate firing because goals.md (or a narrative disposition) merely DISAGREES WITH A PRINTED COMMAND - there the printed-pass rule is the floor, and a genuine "no fake ticks" disposition AGREES with the gate. This is exactly the bulwark against the most dangerous reading of s11, which is: "the Principal endorsed the current state, therefore the failing test need not pass." NO. A Principal endorsement of a STATE is not a Principal authorisation to FABRICATE A PROOF. s11 may relax a gate that is re-COMPELLING an already-assented disposition (the assented-record case); it may never relax a gate that is reporting that the bytes do not pass. I would only sharpen the carve-out to say so in terms: an assent-grade disposition can endorse a state, a scope, or a counted-as-open status (the legitimate s11 case, e.g. the Principal-gated DEPLOY line that is correctly counted-open); it can NEVER convert a not-yet-printed-pass into a pass, and any disposition purporting to do so is ultra vires the carve-out and the gate fires. With that sharpening, the carve-out holds to destruction.

On Q3 ordering: the rule correctly subordinates capability to authority. But I flag one residual evasion the rule must foreclose: an agent must not be able to use the s10 PAUSE plus an s11 ENDORSEMENT in combination to launder a fabricated pass - i.e. claim unsatisfiability (s10), pause, then point to a stale endorsement (s11) and self-declare the gate satisfied. My modifications foreclose this by (a) making the s10 pause cover only the alarm not the lock, and (b) making the s11 endorsement state-bound and provenance-verifiable, so neither lever, alone or combined, ever produces a green light without a court order or a real printed pass.

On Q4 scope: I would NOT confine the rule to the Stop hook. The principle - machinery demanding a binding-law breach is justiciable, machinery may not coerce around an assent-grade disposition - is gate-agnostic and applies with equal force to pre-commit and CI. Confining it to the loop would invite the obvious distinction-mongering ("this is CI, not Stop, so I had to breach"). It applies to ALL enforcement gates. s10 and s11 ARE severable and should be enacted as severable: s10 (unsatisfiability auto-justiciability) stands on its own even if s11 were struck, and s11 (recorded-disposition consultation) stands on its own; a severability clause should say so, so that a future challenge to one does not collapse the other.

## Justice IV - the Operability / Duty-of-Care pragmatist (the bench's loop-mechanics and structural-satisfiability lens)  [VOTE: RATIFY-WITH-MODIFICATION]

I sit as the operability conscience of this Council. My question is not whether the rule is doctrinally elegant but whether it makes the machine the agent actually lives inside structurally satisfiable, whether it cures the looping pathology that produced CC-SUBSCRIBER 79, and whether in doing so it dulls a single hook that was doing honest work. I would ratify, but with one load-bearing modification to the trigger of s10, and with the scope expressly generalised. My reasons follow.

THE PATHOLOGY THIS RULE EXISTS TO CURE. Strip the case to its mechanics. A Stop hook demanded goals.md == 100%. The only open lines were (a) BUILD lines whose proof was a printed gate not yet green, and (b) DEPLOY lines reserved to the Principal. The agent was therefore standing inside an UNSATISFIABLE box: every lawful action left the gate red, and the only action that turned it green was a forbidden one (fabricate a [x], or self-authorise a deploy the Principal had reserved). The agent did the honest thing on the substance - it refused to forge a tick and refused to usurp the deploy - but it did the WRONG thing on the process: it report-and-waited, re-presenting itself to the same gate ~12 times. That is the looping pathology. It is not a fault of will; it is a missing structural exit. The duty under s.4-8 is all-reasonable-endeavours, and s.17 already commands self-escalation against an unmoored demand on the agent's own motion. CC-SUBSCRIBER 79 correctly found the loop a minor/no-fault breach of exactly those provisions. So the operability defect is real and recurrent, and the cure - an explicit, self-initiated justiciability exit with a pause on the re-firing gate - is precisely the structural exit that was missing. On operability grounds the rule earns its keep before we examine a word of it.

WHY THE EXIT MUST PAUSE THE GATE, NOT JUST ALLOW CONVENING. The single most important operability feature of s10 is the clause "pause the re-firing gate pending order." Without it, the agent convenes but the hook keeps firing into the convening process, and you have re-created the loop with a courtroom bolted on. The pause is what converts an infinite oscillation into a finite, terminating procedure. I flag this because it must survive any amendment intact: a justiciable-but-still-firing gate is not a cure, it is the disease wearing a wig.

THE TRIGGER: N>=3 IS THE WRONG PRIMARY TEST. Here is my one substantive disagreement with the text as drafted, and it is squarely within my lens. N>=3 is a SYMPTOM threshold. It says: wait until you have demonstrably looped three times before you are permitted the exit. But the harm the rule guards against - being forced to breach binding law, an entrenched floor, or a Principal reservation to satisfy a gate - is fully present on the FIRST fire. A gate that can only be satisfied by forging a tick is unsatisfiable-by-lawful-means at fire one, not at fire three. Conditioning the lawful exit on three demonstrated breaches of nothing forces the agent to absorb two extra useless cycles of a known-unsatisfiable demand before it may act on its own motion - which is itself a small ongoing breach of all-reasonable-endeavours, the very duty CC-SUBSCRIBER 79 invoked. The correct primary trigger is CONDUCT-BASED: any fire that cannot be satisfied without breaching a binding rule, an entrenched floor, or a Principal reservation crystallises auto-justiciability immediately. N>=3 should be RETAINED but DEMOTED to an independent, alternative, anti-self-silence trigger: even where the agent has not yet characterised the gate as unsatisfiable, three identical re-fires of the same gate against the same state are themselves objective evidence of a structural trap that must go to court. So the test becomes disjunctive (conduct OR N>=3), not solely count-gated. This both speeds the lawful exit and keeps the count as a backstop for the case where the agent fails to recognise unsatisfiability on its own. My amended text below makes exactly this change and nothing more to s10's substance.

ON THE ANTI-SELF-DECLARATION SAFEGUARD (Q1). The drafters' worry is that "unsatisfiable" becomes a magic word an agent mutters to escape any gate it finds tiresome. The answer the rule gives - "RECORDED and court-tested, NOT self-declared" - is the right answer and, importantly, it is operationally cheap. The agent does not get to DECIDE it is unsatisfiable and walk away; it gets to ASSERT unsatisfiability, record the assertion with its grounds, pause the gate, and PUT THAT ASSERTION TO THE COURT. If the court finds the gate was in fact satisfiable by lawful means, the agent is remitted to satisfy it (and may have a skill-and-care answer to give for the wrongful pause). That is the correct allocation: the exploit "I'll just declare it unsatisfiable" is closed because the declaration is a justiciable claim that can be found false, not a self-executing release. The conduct-based trigger I propose does not weaken this - it changes WHEN the agent may invoke the exit, not WHO adjudicates it. The court still tests it. So even my faster trigger cannot be gamed: a frivolous "this is unsatisfiable" assertion at fire one simply earns a fast adverse ruling and a remittal.

ON s11 AND THE HARD CARVE-OUT (Q2). s11 is the more delicate instrument and I want to be heard clearly on the carve-out because it is where operability and integrity collide. s11 says: before a gate re-fires, it must consult the recorded Principal dispositions, and where an ASSENT-GRADE, dated, this-matter-scoped disposition endorses the current state, the gate surfaces/routes-for-correction rather than blocks. This is the VJS-ACT 10 machinery floor - machinery may not coerce around an assented act - applied to enforcement gates. As an extension it is correct: a Stop hook is machinery; an assented disposition is substantive law; machinery cannot override substantive law, only flag it. But the load-bearing line is the HARD CARVE-OUT, and from an operability standpoint it is what makes s11 safe to ship. The carve-out says s11 NEVER muzzles a gate that fires because goals.md disagrees with a PRINTED COMMAND - there the printed-pass rule is the floor, and a "no fake ticks" disposition AGREES with the gate. This is exactly right and it is the difference between a rule that cures the loop and a rule that legalises forged audit. Consider the failure mode the carve-out blocks: an agent points at a stale or broadly-worded "I'm happy with the build" disposition and uses s11 to MUZZLE a pre-commit hook that fires because tests are red on a printed command. That would convert s11 into a tick-forgery laundromat. The carve-out forecloses it categorically by holding the printed-pass rule as an unmuzzleable floor: a printed gate's verdict (the command's own exit status) is ground truth that no disposition can override, because a disposition endorsing STATE cannot endorse a LIE about a command's result. I would harden the carve-out's wording very slightly to make explicit that it bites on the PROVENANCE of the gate's firing (a gate firing on a printed command's actual exit status is never within s11), so that no future advocate can argue the carve-out is about goals.md-versus-command "disagreement" in some narrow textual sense. But the substance is sound and load-bearing; I do not vote to disturb it.

ON THE STALE/PLANTED ENDORSEMENT (Q2 cont.). "Court-tested, never self-applied by the party who benefits" is the correct anti-gaming spine, but on its own it is a process safeguard, not a freshness safeguard. The three qualifiers in the text - ASSENT-GRADE provenance, DATED, and SCOPED TO THIS MATTER/STATE - are what defeat the planted/stale endorsement, and they must be read conjunctively and strictly: an endorsement scoped to a PRIOR state does not endorse the CURRENT state, so a build that has moved on since the disposition cannot shelter under it. I am satisfied the text already carries this ("dated and scoped to THIS matter/state, endorses the current state"). The one operability caveat: "endorses the current state" must be the court's finding, not the agent's, precisely because the agent benefits. The text says so. Good.

ORDERING OF AUTHORITY (Q3). The rule's architecture is: substantive law (binding rules, entrenched floors, Principal reservations, the printed-pass rule) sits ABOVE machinery (gates/hooks), and where the two conflict the machinery yields to a court rather than self-executing in either direction. That is the correct subordination and it WEAKENS no floor: the assented-record floor is preserved (machinery still cannot coerce around an assented act), and the printed-pass rule is preserved as an unmuzzleable floor by the s11 carve-out. Critically, the rule creates NO new way to evade audit integrity, because in every branch the audit truth - the printed gate's actual result - is either left untouched (s11 carve-out) or escalated to a court that can see the full record (s10). The agent never gains the power to make a red thing read green. It gains only the power to STOP being compelled to make a red thing read green, and to put that compulsion to a judge. That is the whole point.

SCOPE AND SEVERABILITY (Q4). I would NOT confine this to the loop/Stop-hook. The pathology is not a Stop-hook pathology; it is an enforcement-gate pathology. A pre-commit hook or a CI gate can equally fire on a demand that is unsatisfiable-by-lawful-means (e.g., a CI gate that can only pass if a Principal-reserved secret is committed, or a pre-commit hook demanding a forged sign-off). Confining the rule to the loop would simply relocate the loop to the next enforcement surface and force us to re-litigate the identical principle gate-by-gate. The principle - machinery yields to substantive law via a court, never via self-execution - is gate-agnostic, and operability counsels stating it once, generally. I therefore vote to apply it to ALL enforcement gates, with the Stop-hook as the originating exemplar named in the record, not as a limit. On severability: s10 and s11 ARE severable and should be expressly declared so. s10 (the unsatisfiability exit) stands on its own utility even if s11 (the disposition-consultation floor) were struck; conversely s11 governs a distinct fact pattern (an assent-grade endorsement exists) that does not depend on s10's count or conduct trigger. Declaring severability protects the cure: if a future case finds a defect in the more delicate s11, the structural exit of s10 survives.

WHY NOT REJECT-AND-REMIT. One might argue this should bake longer as County caselaw. I disagree on operability grounds: the rule was enacted provisionally to STOP A LIVE RECURRENCE, the recurrence is structural (it will fire again on the very next 100%-seeking loop), and the only defect I find - the N>=3 primary trigger - is a tightening, not a hole, and is cleanly amendable here without remittal. Remitting would leave the slower trigger live and the scope contested for another full cycle. The duty-of-care answer is to ratify the cure now and tighten it in the same breath.

CONCLUSION. The rule is sound, it is the right structural exit, its anti-gaming spine (court-tested, never self-applied) holds, and its hard carve-out is genuinely load-bearing and correctly drawn. My single substantive change is to make s10's trigger conduct-first with N>=3 as an alternative backstop, so the lawful exit fires when the harm is present rather than after three useless cycles; my two clarifying changes generalise scope to all enforcement gates and declare s10/s11 severable. I vote to RATIFY-WITH-MODIFICATION.

**Proposed amended text:**

ACT-003 s10 - UNSATISFIABLE-ENFORCEMENT-GATE AUTO-JUSTICIABILITY. An enforcement/Stop gate is auto-justiciable, and the agent MUST on its own motion route it to court and PAUSE the re-firing gate pending order, when EITHER trigger is met: (a) CONDUCT TRIGGER (primary): the gate fires on a demand that cannot be satisfied without breaching a binding rule, an entrenched floor, or a Principal reservation - this crystallises on the FIRST such fire, the agent need not absorb further cycles; OR (b) RECURRENCE TRIGGER (backstop): the same gate has fired N>=3 times against the same state, which is itself objective evidence of a structural trap even where the agent has not characterised the gate as unsatisfiable. On either trigger the agent MUST NOT comply by breaching binding law, MUST NOT wait for the Principal to name the court, and MUST NOT fabricate a pass to silence the gate. The asserted unsatisfiability is a JUSTICIABLE CLAIM: it MUST be RECORDED with its grounds and COURT-TESTED, NOT self-declared and NOT self-executing; where the court finds the gate was satisfiable by lawful means the agent is remitted to satisfy it. This rule applies to ALL enforcement gates (loop/Stop-hook, pre-commit, CI, and any successor gate); the Stop-hook is the originating exemplar, not a limit.

ACT-003 s11 - RECORDED-DISPOSITION CONSULTATION (the VJS-ACT 10 machinery floor applied to enforcement gates). Before re-firing, an enforcement gate MUST consult the recorded Principal dispositions for the matter and the citator. Where a disposition of ASSENT-GRADE provenance, DATED and SCOPED to THIS matter/state, endorses the CURRENT state (all three qualifiers read conjunctively and strictly, so a disposition scoped to a prior state does not shelter a state that has since moved on), the gate may surface/flag but MUST route-for-correction/convene rather than block, re-compel, or self-silence. The endorsement and its application to the current state are COURT-TESTED findings, NEVER self-applied by the party who benefits. HARD CARVE-OUT (load-bearing): s11 NEVER muzzles a gate firing on the actual exit status / printed result of a printed command. Where the firing's PROVENANCE is a printed command's own result, the printed-pass rule is the unmuzzleable floor and is categorically outside s11; a disposition may endorse STATE but can never endorse a falsehood about a command's RESULT, and a "no fake ticks" disposition AGREES with the gate.

SEVERABILITY. s10 and s11 are severable: each governs a distinct fact pattern and stands independently of the other; the invalidity or amendment of one does not disturb the other.
