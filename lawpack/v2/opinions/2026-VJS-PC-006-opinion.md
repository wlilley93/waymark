# [2026] VJS-PC 6 - Opinion of the V2 Privy Council (Self-Invocation of the Canon Super-Repo; First-Instance Court)

**Court:** V2 Privy Council (court structure / realm-as-state; bench of 3)
**Before:** Adair J, Calloway J, Devereux J
**Cause:** On Lexby's own-motion reference (the Principal raised the point). Independent seats; no access to Lexby's preference.
**Disposition:** Reference ALLOWED. **Unanimous (3-0).**

## Ratio (binding)

1. **The canon super-repo must self-invoke; authorship is no exemption (Q1).** Binding flows from the kernel-readable invocation (the `.vjs/config.toml` anchor + the locked lawpack digest + the recorded `local_sovereign_invocation`), **never from authoring the law** (REG-INVOCATION-001; the rule-of-law / no-immunity principle - public/official status excuses nothing). A "bound because it is the source" theory is the directory-ancestry shortcut the regulation abolishes and is operably empty (no code path confers jurisdiction by authorship). The canon's **self-subscription** (it pins a digest of its own lawpack) is coherent, not circular: the lock is a snapshot at lock-time; later edits simply require re-locking.
2. **The lodged invocation is good but incomplete - hooks must be installed to perfect it.** `vjs invoke` ran without `--install-hooks`; REG-INVOCATION-001 lists `install_enforcement_hooks` as a `must`. The invocation is substantively good (config + lawpack.lock + invocation record present) but mechanically incomplete; the **hook install must be completed** to perfect it.
3. **The Privy Council is the realm-as-state's first-instance / County-Court analogue (Q2), by incorporation.** The super-repo is the realm-as-state with no County Court of its own; the PC is the distinct central first-instance court for realm-level matters. **REALM-PC 15 is V1 archival and does not bind V2 of its own force (Calloway J's controlling point); it binds because this order incorporates its ratio with a mandatory kernel effect** (Bill 32 s.11; no revival by silence). The kernel already routes realm-level `jurisdiction.`/`routing.`/`public_private.`/`local_sovereignty.`/`federation.` matters to the PC (`choose_court`), so this is largely declaratory of the operating default and of 2026-VJS-COURTS-CONSTITUTION-001 D2.
4. **First-instance is not apex.** A realm-level PC first-instance decision **remains appealable upward to the Court of Appeal and the Supreme Court by permission to appeal** (leapfrog certificate aside); the PC adds no new tier, seat, apex, or citation series; the single apex (CASE-LAW s.22(2)) is undisturbed.
5. **Vehicle: ruling + a short order, not legislation (Q3).** No kernel change is needed (the route engine already implements both holdings). The order incorporates the PC-first-instance ratio (appealable upward), records the canon's self-invocation as good, and directs completion of the hook install; no ministry, tier, or series is created.

## The opinions, in brief
- **Adair J** (rule of law): nemo est supra leges - the author of the law is bound by the same constitutive act as everyone else; self-invocation is a meaningful consistency act (lock, record, route, hooks), and the hooks must be installed to complete it.
- **Calloway J** (incorporation; controlling on Q2): REALM-PC 15 is archival and inert in V2 until **expressly incorporated with a mandatory effect** (Bill 32 s.11; the V1-silence rule); this order is that incorporation; the self-referential digest pin is a coherent snapshot; first-instance remains appealable to the single apex.
- **Devereux J** (operability): inherent binding is operably empty (no authorship code path); `choose_court` already routes realm-level matters to the PC, so Q2 is declaratory; a ruling + short order, no legislation or kernel change.

## Disposal
Unanimous (3-0). The canon super-repo must self-invoke under REG-INVOCATION-001 (binding by invocation, not authorship), and its lodged invocation must be **perfected by installing the enforcement hooks**. The Privy Council is the realm-as-state's first-instance court for realm-level matters - incorporated from [2026] REALM-PC 15 with a mandatory effect, appealable upward to the single apex, adding no new tier or apex. Settled by this ruling and a short order; no legislation. Recorded by Lexby as registrar.
