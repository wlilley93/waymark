# [2026] VJS-PC 8 - The court machinery versus court constitution dividing line (appeal)

**Privy Council of vibe-justice-system, bench of three at constitutional first instance per 2026-VJS-COURTS-CONSTITUTION-001 D2. Appeal from [2026] VJS-CC-AGENT-UNIVERSE-V2 14 (Ledgerward CCJ), permission granted. Before: Marchmont, Tindale, Rowanne. Disposition: appeal dismissed; County Court AFFIRMED, 3-0.**

## Opinion of Marchmont (for the Council)

I would draw the line by function, not by subject-matter. A measure belongs to the court CONSTITUTION when it performs a constitutive operation: it changes who may sit, the size or parity of a deciding bench, the jurisdiction or tier of a court, or the assent rule. Those are the four things 2026-VJS-COURTS-CONSTITUTION-001 fixes and that Framework Act s.25 entrenches. Apply that test here and the answer is plain. The schema fields add no judge, shrink no bench, move no jurisdiction, and touch no assent source. They record what a constituted bench already did. Recording is downstream of constituting, never identical to it. The "AGAINST" limb proves too much: if the form of a court's output were part of what the court IS, then page layout and file naming would be entrenched. The order's force has never flowed from its shape; it flows from the bench. I affirm the County Court.

## Opinion of Tindale (concurring)

I join Marchmont and add the guard that keeps the test honest. The source-of-force reasoning of [2026] VJS-PC 7 controls: a thing is machinery only while nothing constitutive is satisfiable by the machinery alone. The decisive features of REG-COURT-RECORD-001 are that the fields are optional, serde-default, prospective, and validity-neutral. No field is a precondition of a valid or loadable order; the 22 legacy orders load unchanged; an absent digest invalidates nothing. That is what disarms s.7's anti-Henry-VIII bar. The boundary I would mark is sharp: the day any record field becomes constitutive of validity, the measure crosses from machinery into constitution and needs primary law or the Supreme Court (s.10, s.11). Were REG-COURT-RECORD-001 to read "an order without a digest is void," I would reverse without hesitation. It does not. I concur.

## Opinion of Rowanne (concurring)

I concur in the affirmance and write only to lash the doctrine to the entrenchment scheme, lest the test be mistaken for permission. Framework Act s.25 entrenches the assent floor, apex-singleness, and the protective floor; s.7 forbids a statutory instrument amending the Act, the constitution, any primary Act, or the assent rule. The constitutive-operation test does not loosen one fibre of that. It explains why a recording duty escapes the bar: because it operates on none of the entrenched objects. A measure that did touch them would remain void to that extent however it were captioned, and a "machinery" label could not save it. The converse is equally true and equally important: an auditable court record advances the protective floor's due-process limb rather than weakening it. The County Court read the s.7 power correctly. The appeal is dismissed.
