## Ratio (binding, realm-wide)

**Q3 - the coordination/sovereignty line.** Within the entrenched single apex (s.15), a coordinating or "super-repo" root may federate peer local jurisdictions under one canonical lawpack and one continued apex (REALM-SC 6, REALM-PC 15 persuasive), but may never be sovereign over them. The lawful-coordination test has two layers:
- **Bright-line (kernel-checkable, fail-closed `must_not`):** a root act is void if it (i) asserts or claims an apex or final-court function; (ii) binds, writes, or gates a peer's local law or `.vjs/config.toml` without that peer's recorded adoption (ACT-007 s.4); (iii) overrides the canonical lawpack without the s.3 route; or (iv) technically forecloses a peer's capacity to amend, pin/decline a version (s.7), fork with declared lineage (s.6), or exit (REALM-PC 17). These are formal, determinate predicates the clerk-kernel evaluates (s.11: no model, no similarity).
- **Standard (court-applied, NOT a kernel gate):** below the bright-line, a root that leverages an infrastructural chokepoint to make a peer's compliance practically unavoidable, or conditions a peer's essential access or Gazette publication on accepting central terms beyond the canonical lawpack such that the retained right to amend/fork/exit is paper-only, has crossed into unlawful sovereignty. This practical-subjection question is justiciable on a pleaded record; it is reserved to a court and is not delegated to the kernel (s.11). The four-part formula is the operative consent-and-capacity test; default subscription (ACT-007 s.2) is lawful coordination, not subjection, **because it is genuinely revocable** (decline/pin/fork/exit must remain real, not sham).

**Q4 - holes and mechanism.** Each hole takes exactly one mechanism. Decided here (SC): the coordination/sovereignty line. To the **Privy Council**: the Gazette publication-governance reference (routing/public-boundary), and the role of **transition court** that classifies pending V1 matters (continue / reframe / moot per VJS-PC 1), to be named by instrument - no new tier is created (s.15; REALM-PC 15). To the **V2 lawmaking route**: the directed regulations and deterministic citation-series minting. To the **V1 Court**, narrowly: the perfection-and-Gazette-publication of a matter *already fully heard in V1 before commencement* (no new doctrine, no runtime force, s.16; imports nothing) - and an authoritative validity finding on a *genuinely contested* V1 source for incorporation (the Q5<->Q4 tie); anything wider is V2's, because s.9 forbids reviving V1 machinery by silence. **No hole:** the Court of Appeal persists and convenes on need (courts-constitution order D4); abolition would need express amendment of s.10 by number.

**Q5 - extraction of valid general V1 law into the substrate.** Valid *general* (realm-wide) V1 law crosses only by the **central lawmaking route**: a void first draft (REALM-SC 8) stating all s.8 fields (`v1_source`, `v2_destination`, `operative_rule`, `kernel_effect`, `supersession_or_variation`) **plus a mandatory `validity_of_original` finding**; validated by a **fail-closed incorporation-validity invariant** (any missing element, rank-floor breach, missing ledger row, or protective-floor dilution is rejected); adopted by the organ (Committee + Sovereign assent for primary rank; parent authority for subordinate); and entered in both the canonical lawpack and the **migration ledger** (Charter s.11), the ledger being a deterministic, append-only, provenance-scoped record that is the single source of truth and is **not loaded into runtime agent context**. *Repo-specific* local law uses the **continuity election** of [2026] VJS-PC 1 (local-scope, `.vjs/config.toml`). The test is the scope of the operative rule (realm-wide -> substrate; repo-local -> election); doubt resolves to general. **Guardrails:** validity is found entry-by-entry (REALM-SC 7) - only V1 law itself validly adopted and not spent/overruled/superseded/void/per-incuriam is extractable; **rank-floor** (a lower-rank record may not incorporate higher-rank V1 material); **no bulk import** (one record per crossing); **propose-by-any, adopt-by-organ-only**; and **no incorporation may weaken the protective floor** (the real-world-law boundary, rights/due process, the public/private boundary) - a floor-preservation check fails closed.

**Q6 - canonicalisation.** A transitional ordinal that distinguished a *pending* successor from the incumbent is **spent on commencement**: the live Realm is the unqualified canon (VJS / the Realm / the lawpack / the kernel), and "V1" survives **only** as the Archive/Gazette estate label (so cross-estate matters still read). De-naming is **prospective and nominal and strictly non-disturbing**: it alters no enacted record, citation, digest, record ID, or assented text. The assented Bill 32 bytes and their digest `sha256:8e1d3f51...` are immutable (touching them would be machine self-authorisation barred by s.14); the `[YEAR] VJS-ACT N` series and existing IDs stand; protective and void/per-incuriam status records are never silently rewritten. The mechanism is a short canonicalisation instrument plus a **governed engineering rename that preserves digests, citations, and the lawpack lock** - **an unstaged mass edit is prohibited**, because the lawpack lock `sha256:4d2639cc...` is a tree digest and numerous invariants embed literal `lawpack/v2/` paths in their `scope.paths`, so a blind sweep would break the lock (COMMENCEMENT condition B) and silently un-scope invariants fail-open. Settle the principle now; stage the rename; keep "V1" as the estate label while the V1 estate is actively referenced.

**Method.** The Court rules the principles and **directs the Standing Committee** to reduce them to assented instruments; it does not legislate the text from the bench (REALM-SC 8). The binding form is the assented instrument; the resulting drafts carry no force until they clear the s.14 gate and resolve an `assent_source` under s.23.

## The opinions, in full

### Ashworth CJ (presiding; leading judgment)
Picks up where the Privy Council left off and treats the apex reservation as sound: coordination yes, second apex never. Q3: adopts the four-part formula as the structural test, with a *disciplined* practical-subjection rider anchored to s.16's "substance, not caption" - a paper exit nobody can use is no exit; the rider is anti-circumvention, not a roving licence, and ordinary infrastructural goods remain lawful. Q4: adopts the seven-way allocation; the V1 Court has a residual tightly-fenced role (perfection of already-heard matters, publication-only); the transition-court classifier is constitutional routing and belongs to the PC, named by instrument. Q5: confirms the general/repo-specific split by scope-of-rule; pipeline = void first draft with s.8 fields + validity finding -> fail-closed invariant -> lawpack + migration ledger -> organ adoption; rank-floor; contested validity -> V1 Court. Q6: ordinal spent on commencement; non-disturbing; governed rename, not a mass edit. Directs a five-instrument programme. **Vote: 9-0 on each disposition.**

### Bellamy J (textualist / entrenchment)
Will not adopt the formula bare: Case B's "practical subjection" is *textually unmoored* - ACT-007 fixes the line by legal capacity, not market reality. Holds the four conjunctive limbs anchored verbatim to enacted text, entrenched against any reading that relaxes the apex (s.15) or is machine-amendable (s.14); adds only a *textual* anti-sham rider (a root may not purport to extinguish a peer's s.6/s.7/s.4 rights and call it coordination). Q6 is his operative focus: **non-disturbance is the holding, not a footnote** - assented bytes and the `8e1d3f51...` digest immutable; citations and IDs not retro-edited; dropping "V2" cannot by implication amend or reopen any entrenched provision on pain of voidness. Q5: incorporation express (s.8), never by implication (s.6/s.9), entry-by-entry, rank-matched, fail-closed. **Vote: with the Court on disposition; separate reasoning on Q3 (anti-sham, not practical-subjection).**

### Cardoso J (federation architecture; Q3 lead)
Settles Q3 as a **two-tier rule**: a bright-line of kernel-checkable `must_not` predicates (no apex; no writing/gating a peer's lawpack or config without adoption; no foreclosing amend/pin/fork/exit) plus a court-applied **standard** for the grey zone (leveraging an infrastructural chokepoint to make subjection practically unavoidable), challenger bearing the burden; ordinary cost-of-leaving is not subjection. Adds a fifth limb: non-coercion on the protected exits (tying *optional* services to terms is fine; tying the *baseline subscription* to forfeiture of sovereignty is not). Holds s.2 default subscription lawful, glossed only that decline/pin/exit remain operative and unconditioned. Concurs on Q4/Q5/Q6. **Vote: 9-0 on disposition.**

### Driscoll J (restraint / local sovereignty; partial dissent on Q3 reasoning)
Presses Case B hardest: the formula is **necessary but not sufficient**; a test that bites only on formal apex-claims is one the centre always passes while accreting substance. Would make a practical-subjection rider the controlling fifth limb (substance over form; burden shifts to the root once a peer shows material foreclosure), and would write a *practical-revocability guarantee* into s.2. Concurs in the disposition on Q4 (narrow V1-Court line, PC as transition court), Q5 (anti-bulk-import as a holding, entry-by-entry, rank-floor), and Q6 (non-disturbing, no stealth re-centralisation). **Vote: concurs in disposition; partial dissent on Q3 reasoning (would let the rider bite harder).**

### Ellory J (delegated authority / lawmaking; Q5 lead)
Settles the Q5 pipeline operationally: propose/draft (void first draft, any agent) -> validate (fail-closed invariant on any missing s.8 element or missing validity finding or rank breach) -> classify general/repo-specific by scope (doubt -> general) -> adopt through the central route at matching rank (Sovereign assent for primary; parent authority for subordinate; constitutional needs a Sovereign-assented Act; lower may never incorporate higher) -> enter in lawpack AND ledger (Charter s.11). Contested-source validity is the one V1-Court referral (narrow). Q3: four-part test + practical-subjection as a *standard*, not a second bright line. Q6: settle principle, stage rename, non-disturbing. **Vote: 9-0 on disposition.**

### Fenwick J (continuity / holes; Q4 lead)
The V1 Court is a *tombstone-keeper, not a live forum*: it acts iff (a) the matter was fully heard in V1 pre-commencement, (b) only perfection-in-V1-form + Gazette publication remains, (c) no new doctrine/question, (d) it imports nothing into V2 - fail any limb and it is a V2 matter. The transition-court classifier is the **Privy Council** (routing = D2), named by the Transition Regulation; no new tier (s.15, REALM-PC 15). Court of Appeal: **no hole** (D4, convene-on-need). Q3: four-part + practical-subjection rider. Q5: two-route split, entry-by-entry, rank-floor, fail-closed invariant. Q6: prospective, non-disturbing, governed rename. **Vote: 9-0 on disposition; relief option (i).**

### Goswell J (rights / guardrails; the validity gate and the floor)
The compact substrate is a deliberate scarcity rule; the vice is the bulk-import hazard. Makes the **`validity_of_original` finding a mandatory, fail-closed s.8 field**; uncontested sources certified by the adopting organ on the record, **genuinely contested** sources requiring a **V1-Court finding** (the Q5<->Q4 tie). Adds a **floor-preservation check** to the incorporation invariant: no incorporation may weaken the real-world-law floor, rights/due process, or the public/private boundary - and Q3's sovereignty line is breached where a root conditions a peer's access/publication/membership on a standard *below* the canonical floor (coordination may standardise *upward*, never bargain a peer *below*). Q6: de-naming touches no protective or void-status record. **Vote: 9-0 on disposition.**

### Harlan J (operability; will it run deterministically?)
A rule the clerk-kernel cannot check is exhortation, not law (s.11). Q3: adopts the four formal limbs as fail-closed predicates; **rejects practical-subjection as a kernel gate** (it needs a model; s.11 forbids), reserving it as a bench standard on referral. Q5: the migration ledger is a deterministic append-only **provenance** record, *not loaded into runtime context*; `INV-INCORPORATION-VALIDITY-001` is fatal/fail-closed (allow-list, like INV-ASSENT-SOURCE-001); general/repo-specific decided by a declared `scope` field, not by reading text. Q6: the operability crux - the lawpack lock `4d2639cc...` is a tree digest and 11+ YAML files embed literal `lawpack/v2/` paths in `scope.paths`; **an unstaged mass `sed` is prohibited** (it would break the lock and un-scope invariants fail-open); directs a governed rename with compatibility/redirect and, for any path move, a freshly-recorded lock under a commencement addendum (old lock stays pinned). **Vote: 9-0 on disposition.**

### Pelham J (canon / citation / Gazette; Q6 lead)
A version ordinal is a distinguishing device; on commencement the distinguishing work is done, so "V2" is spent and the canon is unqualified, "V1" surviving only as the estate label (the symmetrical truth of s.16's two estates). The **non-disturbance rule** is stated firmly: assented bytes and the `8e1d3f51...` digest immutable; the `VJS-ACT N` series and IDs unedited; estate boundary operative by substance not caption (s.16). Settle the principle now, stage the rename behind a digest/citation-preservation guarantee; reject both "sweep everything" and indefinite deferral. On Q4, the unheard Gazette reference is a **PC** matter cured by a directed Gazette-Continuity Regulation (the split: V2 governs content/force/estate-label; V1 governs the act on its own repo via SI 7 / PC 19, not barred by s.9), with the estate-label rule captured once in the canonicalisation instrument and cited by the Gazette regulation. Q3: four-part + practical-subjection rider. Q5: split + pipeline + contested-validity V1-Court finding + rank-floor. **Vote: 9-0 on disposition.**

## The division, recorded

The Court is unanimous (9-0) on every disposition and on Q4, Q5, and Q6. On the *mechanism* of Q3 it divides on reasoning, not result: the majority (Cardoso, Ashworth, Ellory, Harlan, with Bellamy concurring on a textual anti-sham basis, and Fenwick, Goswell, Pelham aligning) holds the **four-part formula as the kernel-checkable bright-line and reserves practical-subjection to a court as a standard, not a kernel gate**; Driscoll J would let the practical-subjection rider bite harder as a controlling limb. The binding holding is the majority's: bright-line for the clerk, standard for the bench.
