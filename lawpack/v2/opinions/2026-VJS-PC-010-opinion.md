# [2026] VJS-PC 10 - The Embedded Corporate Jurisdiction (Vibe Policy System)

**Privy Council of vibe-justice-system, bench of three (federation/jurisdiction first instance). Registrar's reference at the Principal's direction. Before: Tindale J (presiding), Rowanne J, Marchmont J. Disposition: LAWFUL, CONDITIONED. Vote: 3-0 (Tindale J., Rowanne J., Marchmont J.): lawful without fresh primary law; the embeddable kernel spec is machinery living upstream..**

## Leading judgment of Tindale J, for the Court

This reference asks whether a company may stand up an embedded corporate jurisdiction without primary law. The bench is unanimous (3-0) that it may. I give the leading judgment reconciling the three opinions into one order.

First, accession by invocation, not ancestry. REG-INVOCATION-001 settles that a repository becomes a VJS jurisdiction by the local Principal's sovereign invocation plus a mechanical install (subscribe to a named lawpack, lock its digest, install the hooks, record the invocation), never by directory ancestry. A separate repo that so invokes is a new local subscribing jurisdiction. No central order is needed to stand one up; the Repos House registries are non-sovereign and pointer-only (REG-REPOS-HOUSE-001, REG-REPOS-REGISTER-001), where invocation binds and the register merely records.

Second, the supremacy clause engages no Realm Sovereign assent. ACT-007 s.3 lets the local Principal vary local law; s.4 confines it to local scope. The company's clause subordinating its own local law to the canon, stricter never looser, is the Board's dualist constitutional act over its own hierarchy, in one direction only (favouring the canon). It is stricter local law, not a change to any Realm law. As Rowanne and Marchmont JJ both put it, the dualist supremacy is the company's own choice, not the Realm enacting the company's law. The void draft flagged the tier question as open; we now answer it. Where the company subordinates itself, no Realm primary law is engaged. A central order under s.6 is reserved for two things only: varying the canonical lawpack, or forking with divergent lineage. VPS does neither: it adopts the canon unaltered and pins it by digest (s.7 expressly permits the pin).

Third, the kernel spec is machinery. PC-008's constitutive-operation test binds: a measure is constitution only if it alters who sits, bench size or parity, jurisdiction or tier, or the assent rule. An embeddable jurisdiction-neutral kernel spec alters none of these; re-skinning governance into corporate organs is presentation, not constitutional surgery. The spec is machinery within the s.7 SI power and lives upstream in the canon.

Fourth, the conditions. REALM-SC 11 (persuasive, V1-lineage, substance over vocabulary) and REALM-SC 9 are common ground across all three opinions. The six conditions are not decorative: they are the load-bearing articles the Charter must carry, and they fix the boundary between a lawful subscriber and a divergent-lineage fork. Breach of any one converts VPS into a fork requiring a fresh Privy Council order under ACT-007 s.6. The order, directives, conditions, and forbidden list follow.

## The six load-bearing charter conditions

1. Digest-pinned vendoring with fail-closed lockstep: the Charter pins the sha256 of the canonical V2 lawpack in .vjs/lawpack.lock; the kernel loads only law that hashes to the pinned digest and fails closed otherwise; a digest is bumped only on the Board's deliberate assent to a new digest, never by silence or auto-track save under a pre-authorised, bounded, and disclosed auto-track rule in the Charter (REG-INVOCATION-001; ACT-007 s.7; REALM-SC 11 condition 1).

2. Candour of provenance: the Charter and the whole contemporaneous governance interface disclose that VPS vendors a subscribed, digest-pinned copy of canonical VJS law re-skinned for corporate organs, not a live render, not independent local authority, not a thin client (REALM-SC 11 condition 5).

3. Unity of citator and apex with binding referral-up: every binding question on the MEANING of canonical VJS law resolves to the Realm's one citation series and single apex; the Charter expressly binds VPS to accept the Realm court's answer as controlling on VPS's own record; no parallel series, local apex, or per-domain citator on canon meaning (REALM-SC 11 conditions 2 and 4).

4. The synthesis bar (no bench of the company's own on canon meaning): VPS's Audit and Risk Committee, review panels, and policy officers apply the canonical rule to facts and gate policy; they do not constitute a deciding bench on canon meaning and issue no binding rulings interpreting the canonical text. Displaying a ruling the Realm court handed down is lawful presentation, not constituting a parallel bench (REALM-SC 11 condition 3).

5. The external-law floor (non-derogable): VPS's Board may not enact local law purporting to lift real-world law duties owed to real persons (privacy, confidentiality, statutory obligations, regulatory compliance, fiduciary and stakeholder duties). The Realm's law floor is a floor not a ceiling; VPS charity status, tax obligations, and external-law obligations bind above the canon (REALM-SC 9; void-draft reservation (c)).

6. Non-impersonation of real-world authority: VPS's interface, naming, and public conduct must make plain to a reasonable professional viewer that VPS governance is an internal corporate adoption of canonical VJS law re-skinned for corporate organs, never a separate Realm court nor a regulated real-world legal authority it does not hold; this is substance achieving a result, not a script of wording, and competent practice cannot lower it (REALM-SC 11 condition 6).

## Opinion of Tindale J.

An embedded corporate jurisdiction lawfully stands under ACT-007 as a subscribing jurisdiction without fresh Sovereign assent and without divergent-lineage fork. The key holdings follow from first principles: (1) Invocation, not ancestry, binds (REG-INVOCATION-001); a separate repo becomes a new local jurisdiction by the company's own sovereign act of local invocation + digest-pinned subscription. (2) The company's dualist adoption of canonical supremacy in its own Charter is an act of the company's Board, not the Realm's legislative will. ACT-007 s.3 permits local Principals to vary local law; it contains no exception barring the company from constitutionally subordinating its own local law to an external canon—indeed, the Charter's supremacy clause is stricter local law (one direction: favoring the canon), not looser. No Sovereign act engages because no Realm law is being changed. (3) The embeddable jurisdiction-neutral kernel spec is MACHINERY under PC-008's constitutive-operation test: it changes no bench, no tier, no assent rule. Re-skinning governance into corporate organs is presentation, not constitutional surgery. (4) REALM-SC 11's six conditions (persuasive, substance-over-vocabulary) bind the company's charter requirements: digest pinning, candour of provenance, the synthesis bar (no bench of the company's own on canon meaning), delivery not law-making, and the external-law floor. The company is lawful only if it satisfies all six; drift into any violates the subscription. Five MANDATORY conditions fix the boundary: (1) digest pinning to the canonical sha256, load-only lockstep with assent-bumps; (2) candour disclosing the vendored, subscribed, digest-pinned status; (3) referral-up to Realm courts on canon meaning, binding the company by Charter; (4) no parallel apex—the company's Audit Committee applies, never interprets authoritative canon meaning; (5) the external-law floor cannot be lifted. If VPS satisfies these five conditions in its Charter, it is a lawful subscriber. If any drifts, it becomes a divergent-lineage fork requiring a Privy Council order under ACT-007 s.6.

## Opinion of Rowanne J.

An embedded corporate jurisdiction that vendors the canonical V2 lawpack pinned by sha256 digest, subscribes under ACT-007, adopts the canon as its own internal supreme law (dualist adoption by the company's Board), re-skins governance into corporate organs, and refers canon-meaning questions to the Realm courts is LAWFUL under existing subscription law WITHOUT fresh Sovereign assent and WITHOUT a divergent-lineage fork. The dualist supremacy is the company's own constitutional choice (the Board's act), not Realm enactment affecting the company, and triggers no Sovereign-assent floor. The re-skinning is MACHINERY under the binding PC-008 constitutive-operation test, not constitution (it alters no bench, tier, or assent rule). VPS satisfies all six conditions of REALM-SC 11 (persuasive V1-lineage judgment): digest-pinned currency, unity of citator and apex, synthesis bar, delivery not law-making, candour, and external-law floor compliance. Six mandatory Charter conditions preserve the boundary: digest-pinned lockstep; candour of provenance; unity of citator and apex with binding referral-up; no parallel bench on canon meaning; external-law floor preserved; and non-impersonation of real-world authority. If VPS drifts from any condition—amending the canon, issuing binding canon interpretations, treating referral-up as advisory, adding law that contradicts the canon, or claiming Realm authority—it ceases to be a lawful subscriber and becomes a divergent-lineage fork requiring a fresh PC order under ACT-007 s.6. The kernel must verify digest pinning on every load, enforce referral-up binding, reject canon-contradicting local law, and confirm Charter supremacy enforceability.

## Opinion of Marchmont J.

An embedded corporate jurisdiction lawfully stands under existing subscription law without fresh Sovereign assent or divergent-lineage fork. ACT-007 s.6 reserves central orders only for varying the canonical lawpack or forking with divergent lineage; VPS does neither—it adopts the canon unaltered and pins it by digest, minting a new subscribing jurisdiction under REG-INVOCATION-001. The company's dualist supremacy (the Board's internal constitutional choice) is the company's own act, not Realm enactment, and lies within the local Principal's authority to vary its own law hierarchy under ACT-007 s.3. VJS-PC-008's machinery-vs-constitution dividing line applies: re-skinning governance into corporate organs with no parallel bench or apex is machinery (s.7 SI power), not constitutional architecture. The embeddable kernel-spec is mechanical delivery engineering. Mandatory conditions follow from REALM-SC 11 (persuasive, V1-lineage, substance-binding): digest-pinned vendoring with fail-closed lockstep; candour of provenance in the charter; unity of citator and apex via binding referral to the Realm's courts; the synthesis bar (no deciding bench of VPS's own on canon meaning); external-law floor (REALM-SC 9) non-derogable; and non-impersonation (interface/naming plainly disclosing internal corporate adoption of canonical law, never Realm authority). Breach of any condition—amending canon, issuing binding canon-meaning rulings, treating referral as advisory, or adding law contradicting canon—converts VPS into a divergent-lineage fork requiring fresh Privy Council order.
