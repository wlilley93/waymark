# [2026] VJS-PC 12 - The s.5(a) kernel-effect gate, on appeal (the entrenchment condition)

**Privy Council of the Vibe Justice System, odd bench of 3 per 2026-VJS-COURTS-CONSTITUTION-001 D2. On appeal from [2026] VJS-CC 15 (Marrowby CCJ), permission granted. Before: Carrow PC, Lindwall PC, Strathorn PC. Result: APPEAL ALLOWED IN PART. Point 1 (dividing line): County AFFIRMED unanimously - the gate is concordance-machinery. Point 2 (assent floor): County VARIED unanimously - the never-void / never-block-an-assented-record character must be ENTRENCHED, amendable only by Sovereign-assented primary law expressly citing s.5. Vote: 3-0.**

Each justice delivers in full. The judgment of the Council is the entrenchment condition in the order; each opinion below is authored by a counted member of the bench.

---

## Carrow PC

I am Carrow PC. I take the two points in turn, confronting the hard edge of each rather than the comfortable middle.

On Point 1, the appellant's strongest argument is that the giving-effect/defining distinction is illusory: to enforce "no kernel effect" the kernel must first decide which operations count as effect, and that boundary-drawing is constitutive of what binding law IS, a matter s.11 and [2026] VJS-CC-AGENT-UNIVERSE-V2 14 reserve from the clerk. I have weighed this seriously, because the ratio of that precedent is exactly that machinery becomes reserved where it re-defines what the constitutive thing is. But the distinction holds here, and the case file's own clarification is decisive. The "registry" the kernel applies is the KernelEffect operation schema, a structural form (is any recognised operation field populated), not the 37 PredicateExpr arms the original submission feared, and not a semantic judgment about meaning. The kernel does not decide what law means or whether a section deserves force; it reads whether a record carries any operative kernel effect at all, the precise predicate s.5 wrote. That is form-checking, the loading/validating function s.11 expressly permits, not the create-force-by-computation or adjudicate-as-court it forbids. The boundary of the runtime authority graph was drawn by the Sovereign in s.5's grammar; the kernel patrols it, it does not survey it. Defining the operation schema is administration "wholly within" s.7's subordinate space; it neither amends s.5 nor expands competence. So the line is real for THIS gate, and I affirm.

On Point 2 I cannot affirm cleanly, and here the County erred by treating a contingent disposition as a sufficient safeguard. The strongest contrary argument is that no harm is shown: the gate as built is a non-blocking Warning, an assented record with an inert kernel_effect validates OK, there are zero false positives on the real lawpack, and unimplemented verbs are cured by registry extension, not removal. All true, and all the reason I do not reserve. But s.14 and s.23 are entrenched and non-derogable BY CONSTRUCTION; that is their whole character. A floor that can be lowered by a one-line change from Warning to Error is not a floor, it is a courtesy. The assent rule fails closed (s.23) and is non-delegable (s.14); a gate that could, by mere configuration, block or effectively void an assented record would let the computational legislature's clerk-layer expand its own competence against assent, the precise Henry-VIII move s.7 voids. The cure must therefore share the character of the thing it protects. It is not enough that the gate happens to be benign today; its benign character must itself be beyond the clerk's reach.

I therefore impose entrenchment as a condition of validity: the gate may never void and never block an assented record, and that property is amendable only by Sovereign-assented primary law citing s.5. Limb (a) need not retreat to the agent rung, because once entrenched the gate is pure concordance with no derogable surface. The gate as built passes; the disposition that makes it safe must be carved into primary law, not left to a contingent line a future hand could change. So I affirm with entrenchment.

## Lindwall PC

I am Lindwall PC. I take the two points in turn, confronting at each the strongest case against my conclusion.

On the dividing line, the appellant's best argument is that to exclude a record "with no kernel effect" the kernel must first decide which operations count as effect, and that choice draws the boundary of the runtime authority graph, which is constitutive of what binding law is and so reserved by s.11 and s.15. I have weighed this seriously, because the line between giving-effect and defining can indeed be illusory. But it is not illusory here, and the gate as actually built tells me why. The implemented test is mechanical: a kernel_effect with every recognized operation field empty is inert; a bare top-level force_source is inert; the same token inside a populated defines block is real effect and is not flagged. That is not the kernel ruling on the meaning of binding law; it is the kernel reading a schema and detecting emptiness. The defining work was done by the Sovereign in s.5, which by its own grammar excludes a record with no kernel effect. The kernel is clerk applying that exclusion (s.11), and on the record it produces zero false positives on the real lawpack across 93 tests. Critically, the implemented registry is the KernelEffect operation schema, not the 37 PredicateExpr arms the original submission feared, so the constitutive over-reach that animated the reservation argument is, on these facts, absent. Per [2026] VJS-CC-AGENT-UNIVERSE-V2 14, machinery administers and does not redefine the constitutive thing; this gate administers. I affirm Point 1.

On the assent floor, the County is right that the gate as built cures the immediate risk: an assented record with an inert kernel_effect validates OK, is flagged not removed, and an unimplemented verb is cured by registry extension, never by voiding law. That answers s.14 and s.23 on today's facts. But the appellant's contrary point is the stronger one in principle, and I accept it: a Warning becomes a blocking Error by a one-line change. s.14 and s.23 are non-derogable by construction, fail-closed, entrenched. A floor that is held up only by a contingent disposition of today's clerk is not entrenched; it is merely well-behaved. The CONSOLIDATION-FRAMEWORK s.7 anti-Henry-VIII principle reinforces this: subordinate machinery may not, by quiet hardening, come to disapply or void an assented record, which a blocking gate on an assented section would do in substance. The safeguard must therefore be the same character as the floor it guards.

I decline reversal-reserve. Reservation would throw away a clean, tested concordance gate to push limb (a) to the agent rung, trading a deterministic clerk for a model-touching adjudication the s.11 prohibitions disfavour. The proportionate remedy is to entrench the disposition, not to abandon the machinery. I therefore affirm with the condition that the never-void, never-block-an-assented-record character be entrenched, amendable only by primary law citing s.5. So ordered.

## Strathorn PC

I am Strathorn PC.

Point 1 turns on whether, for this gate, giving-effect and defining collapse into one act. The reserved-side argument is real and I take it at its strongest: to exclude "a record with no kernel effect," something must first fix what counts as kernel effect, and if that fixing decides which operations are constitutive of binding law, the kernel has crossed from clerk to court (s.11), re-defining what the constitutive thing IS within the meaning of VJS-CC-AGENT-UNIVERSE-V2 14. Were the gate keyed to the 37 PredicateExpr arms the original submission named, I would likely reserve, because choosing which predicates "count" as force is a contestable boundary judgment about the authority graph.

But that is not the gate before me. The implemented registry is the KernelEffect operation schema, and "inert" means a kernel_effect node present with every recognized operation field empty. That is a structural, syntactic test: does the record carry any operative effect at all, or is it a bare ceremonial shell. A record that declares force_source inside a populated defines is real effect and passes; a bare top-level force_source is inert and is flagged. The kernel is not weighing whether a given operation is the right kind of law; it is observing whether the record does anything the kernel can operate. That is precisely what s.5 already commands at primary-law level: a record with no kernel effect is excluded. The Sovereign drew that line at assent. The clerk reports concordance with it. On these facts the giving-effect/defining distinction is not illusory; it is preserved by the modesty of the implementation. I therefore affirm Point 1, but my affirmance is fact-bound: it does not license a future kernel to enrich "kernel effect" into a semantic or predicate-selection judgment. Cross that line and the matter returns reserved.

Point 2 is where I part from the County's comfort. The County is right that, as built, an assented record with an inert effect validates with a Warning, is routed for correction under s.5's own grammar, and is never voided; zero false positives, 93 tests pass. That defuses the assent-floor objection for the system as it stands today. But s.14 and s.23 are entrenched and non-derogable BY CONSTRUCTION, and the contrary point is decisive: a Warning becomes a blocking Error by a one-line change. The County's cure rests on a contingent disposition, not a guarantee. An entrenched floor protected only by present good behaviour is not protected at all; under ACT-CONSOLIDATION-FRAMEWORK s.7, subordinate machinery may not reach a reserved matter, and a gate that could, by ordinary edit, begin blocking an assented record would reach exactly the s.14 constitutive gate it must never touch. The safeguard must share the rank of what it safeguards.

The strongest answer against me is that I am entrenching a mere disposition and ossifying ordinary engineering. I reject it: I do not freeze the operation schema, the tests, or the Warning's wording. I entrench one narrow invariant: this gate may never void or block an assented record, amendable only by Sovereign-assented primary law expressly citing s.5. That keeps limb (a)'s correction logic free to live at the agent rung while the floor itself is put beyond a one-line erosion. On that condition, the gate is good machinery.
