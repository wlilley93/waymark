**TINDALE J., Privy Council**

*In the matter of SUBMISSION-2026-06-24-vjs-pc-kernel-enforcement-gaps (case file digest sha256:8f2fa1810249782f1a2a5798ed838ac92cac4d44f4ed800c1646c0f52aa4a92e)*

**Constitution and standard of review.** I sit on the odd bench of three constituted by [2026] VJS-SC 2 D2 over jurisdiction, routing, constitutional machinery, and the public/private boundary at the realm level. I have read the pinned case file in full and verified its digest. I have not taken the parties' law on trust: I have read ACT-002, ACT-004, ACT-005, ACT-007, ACT-008, ACT-009 (the Consolidation Framework, with s.7 and the s.25 entrenchments), ACT-010 (the assented-record floor), and the cited regulations (REG-INVOCATION-001, REG-HOOKS-001, REG-KERNEL-001, REG-BUNDLE-001, REG-COURT-RECORD-001, REG-FEDERATION-COORDINATION-001), together with [2026] VJS-SC 2 and [2026] VJS-PC 6. The audit recorded in the case file is, on the law as I find it, accurate in every load-bearing particular. My task is not to re-audit but to judge: for each question, to find the diagnosis, name the instrument the fix gives teeth to, and fix the form.

**The two hard constraints I carry through every directive.** First, the anti-Henry-VIII limit (ACT-009:s7, reinforced by the s.25 entrenchments): a statutory instrument or kernel change made under the s.7 power is subordinate law and "may not amend, disapply, or expand this Act, the constitution, any primary Act, or the assent rule." It may not alter a bench size, a tier, a jurisdiction, or an assent rule. Second, the assented-record floor (ACT-010:s1): a record declaring a valid assent_source "may never be voided, excluded, or blocked by any subordinate validation, gate, invariant, regulation, or kernel operation"; its defects are "ALWAYS routed for correction." Crucially, that section's own last sentence reads: "This forbids only voiding and blocking; it does not prevent a defect being surfaced, flagged, or routed for correction." Those two constraints are not obstacles to the relief sought here; properly read, they are the design grammar of it. Almost every gate below operates on the *write* path, before assent attaches, where neither constraint bites; and the one gate that could touch an assented record (Q5) is disposed expressly as route-for-correction, never void.

**The unifying finding.** I accept the central diagnosis. The breach that occasioned this matter (eleven subscriber-scoped files self-asserting [2026] VJS-DEC 15-22 into canon, removed by e446abd) did not occur because the law permitted it. ACT-005:s1 forbade the content; ACT-004:s8 forbade the self-asserted citation; ACT-007:s4 and REG-FEDERATION-COORDINATION-001 forbade the cross-scope intrusion. The law was sound and silent at once: sound in substance, silent at the chokepoint, because no kernel gate stood on the canon-write path to make the existing prohibition fire. This is an enforcement-wiring failure, not a legislative one. The correct response to it is therefore overwhelmingly machinery under ACT-009:s7 and REG-KERNEL-001, not new primary law. To meet a wiring failure with a fresh raft of statutes would itself be a category error, and (as I develop at Q9) would reproduce the very vice this matter prosecutes: making law to outrun the evidence.

I now take the nine questions.

**Question 1 (Canonicity gate).** I accept the FACT-1 diagnosis. The prohibition is fully enacted (ACT-005:s1 must_not publish_private_repo_paths / publish_client_facts; s5 contributor-fact bar; s3 the agent's redaction duty; s7 the mandatory deterministic, no-LLM boundary scan on public changes). The defect is purely that the scanner does not run on lawpack/v2 writes and does not inspect structured fields, while the kernel reserves BoundaryFindingKind::PrivateRepoPath and ::UnredactedEvidence unused. The directed fix is machinery under ACT-009:s7 giving teeth to ACT-005:s1/s5/s7. It is the right form and it is sufficient, with one refinement I make express: the gate must be a *write-path* gate (pre_write and validate --staged on any lawpack/v2 path), it must remain deterministic and conservative (ACT-005:s7 forbids LLM judgement), and it must emit a Block verdict citing ACT-005:s1 by section (REG-KERNEL-001: every denial names the instrument). Because canon writes precede any assent, ACT-010 is not engaged on this path; the gate blocks lawfully. I direct it (D1).

**Question 2 (Citation allocation).** I accept FACT-2. ACT-004:s8 already enacts deterministic, unique citations, fixed format, collisions fatal, with must check_citation_uniqueness and must_not allow_duplicate_citations. The CitationRegistry exists but register() is never called in production, cmd_next_citation builds an empty registry each call, and a self-asserted citation string is accepted as authored. The directed fix is machinery under ACT-009:s7 giving teeth to ACT-004:s8: bind allocation to route/record so the kernel allocates-and-stamps the citation from the live, persisted register (register() called, collisions fatal), and treats a self-asserted citation: field as a fatal mismatch unless it equals the kernel-allocated value (reconcile-at-write). This is the right form. I add the determinative timing point the submission already identifies: allocation runs *before* assent, at authoring, so it never operates on an assented record and ACT-010 is not engaged. A self-asserted citation on the canon-write path is not yet an assented record; refusing to stamp it as-authored is a write-time integrity check, not a void of anything assented. I direct it (D2).

**Question 3 (Jurisdiction-scope twin).** I accept FACT-3 and I hold expressly that Q3 merges into the Q1 canon-write gate. The boundary law is fully enacted (ACT-007:s1/s3/s4; REG-FEDERATION-COORDINATION-001 pins apex_court_jurisdiction: vjs and the apex_routing_decision bright-line). That bright-line today catches one direction only: a subscribing jurisdiction recording an apex order, and it correctly excludes lawpack/ as the read-only mirror. The uncovered, inverse direction (the apex seat recording subscriber-*scoped* content into canon) is the same defect as FACT-1 seen from the federation side. The disposition fork the submission puts to me, one consolidated canon-write gate versus two distinct gates, I answer in favour of **one consolidated gate**. The two would be redundant and risk divergent verdicts on a single write event; the disease is one (subscriber-scoped content in canon) and the chokepoint is one (the canon write). I direct, as part of D1, that the canon-write gate also test the structured scope (scope.paths, identity, repo_code) for a named-subscriber origin and Block on it, citing ACT-007:s4 / REG-FEDERATION-COORDINATION-001 alongside ACT-005. I direct separately (D3) the thin working-root / jurisdiction_id check for the rare true cross-repo permit, which is the narrow lawful exception (ACT-007:s3, Privy order or assent) and must be the only path through.

**Question 4 (Install completeness; atomic manifest; PATH shim).** I take the three limbs in turn.

*(a) Install-completeness invariant.* I accept the diagnosis. REG-INVOCATION-001 already makes subscribe + lock-digest + install_enforcement_hooks + record-invocation a *must*; ACT-007:s1 makes create_config_toml_on_install a must; [2026] VJS-PC 6 already *held* a hooks-missing install legally incomplete and ordered it perfected; ACT-008:s5 declares a record with no kernel effect excluded from runtime authority. Nothing verifies the mandated surface is present and active at a governed write. The directed fix is machinery under ACT-009:s7 giving teeth to REG-INVOCATION-001, ACT-007:s1, and the ratio of [2026] VJS-PC 6: an install-completeness invariant fired at pre_write and validate --staged, fail-closed unless the REG-INVOCATION-001 surface is present and active. This is right form and within vires (it constitutes nothing; it verifies a state already mandated). I direct it (D4).

*(b) Atomic signed/locked manifest [first impression].* On the law as I find it, REG-BUNDLE-001's atomic, fail-closed manifest-verify is by its own terms scoped to *cross-repo deployment bundles* (assembling the governance house from separate repositories), not to the per-repo install. There is therefore a genuine gap of *form*, and it is a first-impression form question properly before this bench. I hold that the install *should* be an atomic, locked manifest of the required surface, mirroring the REG-BUNDLE-001 / REG-ACCESSION-001 lock pattern but repo-scoped. My reasons: the breach class this matter prosecutes is precisely partial, drifted, or silently-incomplete governance state; a manifest that names the required components and is verified atomically (all-present-and-active or fail-closed) is the minimal form that makes "incomplete" a *detectable, single* condition rather than a scatter of independent absences. I fix the "required surface" as the union of what existing law already mandates, so that I legislate nothing new in substance: (1) .vjs/config.toml present (ACT-007:s1); (2) .vjs/lawpack.lock pinning the canon digest (REG-INVOCATION-001, REG-ACCESSION-001); (3) the enforcement hooks installed and the agent-runtime adapters present (REG-INVOCATION-001 install_enforcement_hooks, REG-HOOKS-001 event surface; see Q6/Q7); (4) the recorded local_sovereign_invocation (REG-INVOCATION-001). The manifest is a lock file enumerating these with their digests; the verify is fail-closed and cites the instrument behind any missing limb. This is machinery under ACT-009:s7 (it adds no statute, bench, tier, or assent rule; it gives *form* to duties already enacted), and I so direct (D5). I do **not** direct a new *signature* scheme requiring external key material in this order; a sha256 lock mirroring REG-ACCESSION-001 is sufficient to the harm, and a cryptographic signing ceremony would import a Principal-held key dependency I decline to manufacture without need.

*(c) PATH shim [first impression; Principal-dependency].* The PATH shim is wholly unlegislated, and it is materially different from everything else in this order because it mutates host state *outside the governed repo* (a ~/.local/bin entry on the operator's machine). That places it outside pure machinery and into Principal-dependency territory: the kernel cannot lawfully or practically reach out and rewrite a human's shell environment as a self-executing gate, and to mandate it as a *must* enforced by the kernel would be to direct an act the governed system does not own. I therefore dispose it as follows. The bypass-proof *backstop* is, and must remain, the absolute-path hook invocation already in place: that is what makes enforcement real regardless of PATH, and it satisfies the duty. The PATH shim is a *convenience and discoverability* improvement, not an enforcement primitive; it is **recommended but not mandated**, and it is flagged as a Principal-dependency: its installation is offered by the invoke ceremony and may be declined, and its absence is **not** a fail-closed condition (the absolute-path backstop carries the duty). I direct accordingly (D6), and I record expressly that nothing in this order requires the Principal to alter their host environment.

**Question 5 (Court-tier floor; block-vs-warn under the assented-record floor).** I accept that ACT-002:s2/s3/s4/s9 already mandate routing each matter to its tier, and that choose_court() computes it deterministically but advisory-only, never gated. The directed fix is machinery under ACT-009:s7 giving teeth to ACT-002:s2/s3/s4: a tier-floor check at order validate/record that refuses to *record* an order whose court: is below the required tier (and flags too-high per s4), citing the section in the denial (REG-KERNEL-001). On the design fork (hard-block vs warn-and-route) I rule with care, because this is the one gate in the order that can encounter an *assented* record, and ACT-010 is a hard constraint. My disposition is bifurcated and follows the floor exactly:

(i) For an order that is **not** an assented record (the ordinary case: an order at the point of recording, before any Sovereign assent attaches), the tier-floor gate **hard-blocks**: the order is refused at record-time, citing ACT-002. This is lawful because ACT-010 protects only records declaring a valid assent_source, and a draft order at write-time is not one.

(ii) For an order that **does** declare a valid assent_source, the gate **must not block or void**: under ACT-010:s1 the tier defect is **routed for correction** (surfaced, flagged, registered as a correction-route condition), never excluded or voided. This is not a weakening of the tier floor; it is the floor doing precisely what its own text reserves ("it does not prevent a defect being surfaced, flagged, or routed for correction"). I would **refuse** any directive that purported to void or block an assented record for a tier defect; such a directive would be void to the extent of the inconsistency with ACT-010 (and with ACT-009:s25 entrenchment). I therefore direct the bifurcated gate: hard-block pre-assent, route-for-correction post-assent (D7). The defect code for the post-assent path must be registered in the kernel's ROUTE_FOR_CORRECTION set, never as Error/Fatal, consistent with the constitutional floor.

**Question 6 (Front-door wiring).** I confirm FACT-6: this is settled law and a built kernel. REG-SELF-CONVENE-001 ("enforcement is functional, not by prompt ... fail closed"), REG-HOOKS-001 (the five-event surface and typed verdicts), REG-KERNEL-001 ("the only smart enforcement point"), DEC-AGENT-001, and DEC-AGENT-HARNESS-001 (must_not rely on role prose as enforcement) leave nothing to decide on the law. The residual is pure install-wiring: vjs invoke --install-hooks installs only git pre-commit/pre-push and wires only Claude. The submission asks whether this needs an express direction or is a decisive engineering call under existing authority. I hold it is **a decisive engineering call needing no fresh ruling** as to *its substance*: REG-INVOCATION-001's install_enforcement_hooks and REG-HOOKS-001's thin-adapter rule already mandate it. However, because the install-completeness invariant (D4/D5) will now *verify* the adapter surface, and because this order is the natural vehicle, I do direct it expressly for the avoidance of doubt and to bind it to the manifest (D8): vjs invoke --install-hooks must emit the agent-runtime PreWrite/SessionStart/PostAction adapters for each supported runtime, with a testkit eval asserting their presence and firing. I add the boundary the submission correctly flags: the git names "prepare-commit-msg" and "post-checkout" are *not* VJS events and must not be legislated as such (REG-HOOKS-001 fixes the five-event surface exhaustively).

**Question 7 (Hook completeness).** I confirm the surface point: REG-HOOKS-001 and hook.rs already define all five events; install wires only pre_commit/pre_push. I direct wiring of the existing pre_write (the priority, because it catches an unpermitted governed write *before* it lands, which is exactly the chokepoint the canon-write gate of D1 needs), session_start, and post_action events (D9). I affirm, as a holding, that "prepare-commit-msg" and "post-checkout" are non-VJS git names and are **not** to be added; the five-event surface is closed. This is machinery under ACT-009:s7 giving teeth to REG-HOOKS-001 and REG-INVOCATION-001; it adds no event, no verdict type, and no new authority.

**Question 8 (Bench-integrity verification) [first impression on form].** I accept FACT-5, and I accept it with particular gravity because it failed *live* in the preparation of this very matter: an out-of-kernel deliberation lost a justice-seat to a transient model-capacity error and nothing detected the incomplete bench; it was caught only by manual transcript inspection. A court that can silently lose a justice is the disease this matter prosecutes, turned inward. The constitution is fully enacted: [2026] VJS-SC 2 fixes the benches (county 1, privy 3, supreme 5/9), forbids any even bench and any order before constitution; REG-COURT-RECORD-001 makes recording the deciding bench and pinning the case_file_digest a duty before the order issues; ACT-002:s10 validates an order's fields. None *verifies* bench integrity: validate_order_format checks fields, and REG-COURT-RECORD-001 is by its own terms "a recording duty only ... not constitution." The directed fix is machinery under ACT-009:s7 that gives *teeth* to that extant constitution without touching it: I am explicit that this adds no bench, no tier, no size, and no assent rule (it would be ultra vires the s.7 power and void under s.25 if it did); it merely refuses to record an order that does not *match* the constitution VJS-SC 2 already fixed. On the first-impression *form* question (is a verification gate sufficient, or is a fuller convene-first state machine needed?), I hold the verification gate is the **right and sufficient** floor for this order, and I direct it (D10): vjs court record / validate_order_format must fail-closed and refuse an order unless (i) the recorded bench size equals the constituted size for the order's court tier per VJS-SC 2 (and is odd), **and** (ii) every counted seat in bench: is matched by a present, non-empty opinion attributable to that seat in the recorded source_opinion. The denial cites VJS-SC 2 and REG-COURT-RECORD-001. I add one integrity refinement the live failure demands: the verification must not be satisfiable by a *named* seat with no opinion (the bench:[A,B,C]-where-C-never-opined failure mode); seat and opinion must be matched, not merely counted. I decline, in *this* order, to mandate a full convene-first state machine: that is a larger build whose precise shape should be informed by the conformance audit I order at Q9 (the convene/record/issue lifecycle is exactly the kind of thing the audit will map), and the verification gate already closes the demonstrated live hole. I reserve the convene-first state machine to the same follow-on track, without prejudice.

I note for completeness that the order under preparation is being *re-run through the kernel mechanism* rather than salvaged from the defective out-of-kernel convening; that is the correct restorative remedy (ACT-009:s21 limb four, restorative-not-punitive) and I record Lexby's self-file of the out-of-kernel convening as a falling-below remedied by re-running through the gate, with no punitive consequence.

**Question 9 (Full-spectrum audit; reserving the consolidating instrument; the unifying principle).** This is the question on which I have reasoned hardest, because it is where the temptation to over-reach is greatest and where over-reaching would betray the very wrong we are correcting.

The eight gaps above are one disease: an existing substantive duty with no deterministic gate at the chokepoint. The unifying principle the submission articulates is sound and I adopt it: **no governed record (law, ruling, citation, order) may come into being except through the front door; any other entry is structurally a breach.** "Single front door, no other entry." That principle is already *latent* across the lawpack: REG-KERNEL-001 says the kernel is the only smart enforcement point; REG-SELF-CONVENE-001 says enforcement is functional and fail-closed; DEC-AGENT-001 routes all load-bearing work through vjs route; ACT-008:s5 excludes a record with no kernel effect from the runtime authority graph. What is missing is its *general, enforced* statement as a conformance rule: every substantive duty must be bound to a gate, on pain of being unwired-and-void at the chokepoint.

The question is whether this bench should **enact** that consolidating rule now (Position B), or **mandate a full-spectrum audit now and reserve** the consolidating instrument to a follow-on grounded in that audit (Position A). I hold firmly for the reserved approach, and I reject Position B, for reasons of both principle and competence:

First, *principle*. The wrong this matter prosecutes is law (and citations, and orders) brought into being ahead of the evidence and outside the front door, asserted rather than earned. To answer it by *bashing out* a consolidating "wired-or-void" regulation now, without a complete map of which duties are wired and which are not, would be to commit the same sin in the legislature that the breach committed in the canon: a load-bearing instrument asserted ahead of the ground truth that alone can make it safe. A wired-or-void rule enacted *before* the conformance map is known is a live hazard: it could, on its own terms, render currently-unwired-but-valid duties void at the chokepoint the instant it commenced, with no inventory of what would fall. That is not discipline; it is the opposite. The remedy for bashing-law-out-of-thin-air must not itself be bashed out of thin air.

Second, *competence and vires*. A "wired-or-void" conformance rule is not obviously mere machinery. If it operates to *void* a duty for want of a gate, it brushes against the entrenched guarantees (ACT-009:s25; the assent floor) and against ACT-010 wherever an assented duty is implicated; its precise reach must be drafted to *route-for-correction* unwired-but-assented duties rather than void them, exactly as I held at Q5. That drafting cannot be done responsibly blind. It requires the conformance map first.

Third, *sufficiency of the staged approach*. Position A loses nothing of value. The teeth that close the live breach vectors (D1-D10) land *now*; the realm is protected today. The audit lands now and produces the inventory. Only the *consolidating instrument* waits, and it waits precisely so it can be grounded and safe. Position C (minimal: Q1/Q2/Q8 only) I reject as under-reaching: it leaves Q4-Q7 duties unwired and orders no audit, so the next side-door is unguarded; it treats the symptom and ignores the pattern.

I therefore confirm the sequencing the submission proposes: **teeth + audit now; the consolidating instrument reserved.** I direct (a) a full-spectrum conformance audit enumerating *every* substantive prohibition/duty in the lawpack and recording, for each, whether it is bound to a deterministic kernel gate, producing a conformance map of unwired duties (D11); and (b) I **reserve** to a follow-on instrument, grounded in that map and brought back to the appropriate court for enactment (the consolidating rule, being constitutional machinery at the realm level, returns to this Privy Council or higher), the "single front door, no other entry" rule, with its disposition of unwired duties to be drafted as **route-for-correction, never void**, so as to sit within ACT-010 and ACT-009:s25 from the outset (D12, reservation only, enacting nothing today). I explicitly do **not** enact the consolidating rule in this order.

**On vires, summarised.** Every gate I direct (D1-D11) is machinery under ACT-009:s7 and REG-KERNEL-001. None amends a statute, a bench, a tier, or the assent rule; each *gives teeth to* a duty already enacted, and each denial names the instrument it serves (REG-KERNEL-001). None voids an assented record: the write-path gates (D1-D6, D8-D11) operate before assent attaches; the one gate that can meet an assented record (D7, tier-floor) and the reserved consolidating rule (D12) are bound to route-for-correction, never void, in faithful obedience to ACT-010 and the s.25 entrenchments. Where the relief would have required the kernel to mutate host state it does not own (the PATH shim), I have declined to mandate it and flagged the Principal-dependency (D6).

**DIRECTIVES.**

- **D1.** Direct, as machinery under ACT-009:s7 (teeth to ACT-005:s1/s5/s7 and, per Q3, ACT-007:s4 / REG-FEDERATION-COORDINATION-001): extend the deterministic, no-LLM boundary scanner to run on every lawpack/v2 write (pre_write and validate --staged) and to inspect structured fields (scope.paths, id, title, decision, citation, repo_code) for a subscriber repo_code / subscriber path / named-subscriber origin, emitting BoundaryFindingKind::PrivateRepoPath or ::UnredactedEvidence and a **Block** verdict citing the section. One consolidated canon-write gate serves both Q1 and Q3.
- **D2.** Direct, as machinery under ACT-009:s7 (teeth to ACT-004:s8): bind citation allocation to route/record; the kernel allocates-and-stamps from the live, persisted CitationRegistry (register() called; collisions fatal) at authoring, *before assent*; a self-asserted citation: field is a fatal mismatch unless equal to the kernel-allocated value (reconcile-at-write).
- **D3.** Direct a thin working-root / jurisdiction_id check as the sole lawful path for a true cross-repo permit (ACT-007:s3 exception; Privy order or Principal assent), fail-closed otherwise.
- **D4.** Direct, as machinery (teeth to REG-INVOCATION-001, ACT-007:s1, the ratio of [2026] VJS-PC 6, ACT-008:s5): an install-completeness invariant fired at pre_write and validate --staged, fail-closed unless the REG-INVOCATION-001 surface is present and active.
- **D5.** Direct (first-impression form, disposed): the install be recorded as an **atomic, locked manifest** of the required surface, repo-scoped, mirroring the REG-ACCESSION-001 / REG-BUNDLE-001 lock pattern; required surface = config.toml (ACT-007:s1) + lawpack.lock digest (REG-ACCESSION-001/REG-INVOCATION-001) + installed hooks and runtime adapters (REG-HOOKS-001/REG-INVOCATION-001) + recorded invocation; verify is fail-closed and cites the instrument behind any missing limb. **No external signing-key dependency is mandated** (sha256 lock suffices).
- **D6.** Dispose (first-impression; **Principal-dependency, declined as a mandate**): the absolute-path hook invocation remains the bypass-proof enforcement backstop and satisfies the duty; the PATH-visible vjs shim is **recommended, offered by the invoke ceremony, and may be declined**; its absence is **not** a fail-closed condition. Nothing in this order requires the Principal to alter their host environment.
- **D7.** Direct, as machinery (teeth to ACT-002:s2/s3/s4): a tier-floor gate at order validate/record citing the section. **Bifurcated under ACT-010:** for a non-assented order, **hard-block** at record-time; for a record declaring a valid assent_source, **route-for-correction only** (surface/flag, never void or block), the defect code registered in the kernel's ROUTE_FOR_CORRECTION set. A directive to void or block an assented record for a tier defect would be **refused** as void against ACT-010 / ACT-009:s25.
- **D8.** Direct (engineering call under REG-INVOCATION-001/REG-HOOKS-001, bound here to the manifest): vjs invoke --install-hooks must emit the agent-runtime PreWrite/SessionStart/PostAction adapters for each supported runtime, with a testkit eval asserting their presence and firing.
- **D9.** Direct wiring of the existing pre_write (priority), session_start, and post_action events (teeth to REG-HOOKS-001/REG-INVOCATION-001). **Affirm** that "prepare-commit-msg" and "post-checkout" are non-VJS git names and are not to be added; the five-event surface is closed.

Denial cites VJS-SC 2 and REG-COURT-RECORD-001. The fuller convene-first state machine is reserved to the D11 audit track, without prejudice.

**Disposition.** I would adopt **Position A** in full. I dispose the first-impression points as above (Q4 manifest: yes, sha256-locked, no signing-key dependency, D5; Q4 PATH shim: recommended-not-mandated, Principal-dependency flagged, D6; Q5 block-vs-warn: bifurcated, hard-block pre-assent / route-for-correction post-assent, D7; Q8 bench-integrity: verification gate sufficient now, convene-first machine reserved, D10; Q9 sequencing: teeth + audit now, consolidating instrument reserved, D11/D12). ### I. The nature of the matter, and the discipline I bring to it

I have read the case file in full and I have read, for myself, every instrument on which the diagnosis rests: ACT-002, ACT-004 (s8), ACT-005 (s1, s3, s5, s7), ACT-007 (s1, s3, s4), ACT-CONSOLIDATION-FRAMEWORK (s4, s7, s10, s21, s25), ACT-ASSENTED-RECORD-PROTECTION (s1, s2); REG-COURT-RECORD-001, REG-FEDERATION-COORDINATION-001, REG-INVOCATION-001, REG-HOOKS-001, REG-KERNEL-001, REG-BUNDLE-001, REG-ACCESSION-001, REG-SELF-CONVENE-001; and the orders [2026] VJS-SC 2 (the courts-constitution order) and [2026] VJS-PC 6. A justice does not take the parties' law on trust, and I have not. On the law as I have verified it, the recorded audit is correct: the substantive prohibitions and duties this matter is concerned with are already enacted, in force, and (in the federation and front-door instances) already built into the kernel. What failed was not the law. What failed was the wiring of that law to the one chokepoint the Realm has chosen as its single smart enforcement point: the kernel, on the canon-write path.

I want to name, at the outset, the governing insight, because it disciplines everything below. The breach that occasioned this matter (the eleven subscriber-scoped files that entered canon self-asserting citations [2026] VJS-DEC 15–22, removed by commit e446abd) did not occur because anyone was permitted to do it. Every one of those acts violated a prohibition already on the statute book: ACT-005:s1 forbids private repo paths and client facts in the public record; ACT-005:s5 forbids contributor private facts entering canon; ACT-004:s8 makes citations deterministic, unique, and collision-fatal. The acts succeeded not by permission but by **the absence of a gate**. A prohibition with no deterministic check at the chokepoint is, at runtime, indistinguishable from no prohibition at all. That is the disease. The remedy is overwhelmingly machinery, not legislation.

This shapes my central refusal. **I will not re-legislate a settled duty.** To enact a fresh statute restating ACT-005:s1 or ACT-004:s8 would be worse than redundant: it would imply, by negative inference, that the existing prohibition lacked force until re-enacted, which is false and which would corrode the very statutes it purported to reinforce. ACT-CONSOLIDATION-FRAMEWORK:s4 is explicit that a restated principle acquires no runtime force by restatement alone, only through a live invariant, regulation, or s.7 instrument. The duties here already have runtime *meaning*; what they lack is runtime *teeth*. Teeth are machinery. Machinery is s.7's business.

Two hard constraints bound every gate I would direct, and I hold them in mind throughout rather than only at Q5:

1. **The anti-Henry-VIII limit (ACT-CONSOLIDATION-FRAMEWORK:s7).** A statutory instrument made under s.7 is subordinate law; it "may not amend, disapply, or expand this Act, the constitution, any primary Act, or the assent rule." No gate I direct may, in substance or by side-effect, change a bench size, a tier, a jurisdiction, or the assent rule. Each gate must be a *check that the existing rule holds*, never a *new rule about who may rule*. Where a directed gate even arguably touches the constitution of a court, I require it to draw its content **by reference** from the constituting instrument ([2026] VJS-SC 2), never to restate or vary it.

2. **The assented-record floor (ACT-ASSENTED-RECORD-PROTECTION:s1, entrenched by its s.2 and by ACT-CONSOLIDATION-FRAMEWORK:s25).** A record declaring a valid Sovereign assent_source "may never be voided, excluded, or blocked by any subordinate validation, gate, invariant, regulation, or kernel operation." Its defects are *always* routed for correction. Critically, s.1 forbids **only voiding and blocking**; it expressly "does not prevent a defect being surfaced, flagged, or routed for correction." That sentence is the design key for every block-vs-route question below. A gate that would Block an assented record is void to that extent. A gate that *Warns and routes-for-correction* an assented record is not merely permitted — it is the floor's *prescribed* disposition.

With those constraints fixed, I turn to the nine questions. I accept the diagnosis on each; my work is the disposition.

### II. The eight enforcement gaps

**Question 1 — The canonicity gate.** I ACCEPT the FACT-1 diagnosis. The prohibition is enacted (ACT-005:s1, s5; the duty to act on flags, s3; the deterministic-scan mandate, s7, "no LLM"). The kernel even reserves `BoundaryFindingKind::PrivateRepoPath` and `::UnredactedEvidence` and leaves them unused, and the RedactScanner does not run on `lawpack/v2` writes at all. This is the paradigm enforcement gap: a complete duty with the gate disconnected from the path it must guard.

The proposed form is correct and I adopt it, **as machinery under ACT-CONSOLIDATION-FRAMEWORK:s7, the operative rule being ACT-005:s1/s5/s7** — no fresh statute. I DIRECT that the deterministic scanner be extended (i) to run on `lawpack/v2` writes, not only `.vjs/` writes, and (ii) to inspect structured fields (`scope.paths`, `id`, `title`, `decision`, `citation`, and the like) for a subscriber `repo_code` or a subscriber path, emitting the already-reserved `BoundaryFindingKind::PrivateRepoPath` / `::UnredactedEvidence` with a **Block** verdict at `pre_write`. This is sufficient for the ordinary (non-assented) canon-write, which is where the breach occurred and where Block is lawful. I add one refinement the case file leaves implicit but the floor compels: because a canon record *may* in principle carry an assent_source, the gate's verdict must be **conditioned on assent status** — Block where there is no valid assent_source; Warn-and-route-for-correction where there is one (the ACT-010 carve-out, developed at Q5). The duty to *redact before publication* (s3) is the agent's; the gate's job is to make the unredacted write impossible to land silently, which Block (or, for assented records, a routed Warning) achieves.

**Question 2 — Citation allocation.** I ACCEPT FACT-2. ACT-004:s8 already enacts deterministic, unique, collision-fatal citations. A `CitationRegistry` exists but `register()` is never called in production; `cmd_next_citation` rebuilds an empty registry each call and thus returns a perpetual genesis n=1; `cmd_route` never allocates or stamps. The allocator is built and disconnected. Again: a complete duty, no gate.

The proposed form is correct, **as machinery under s.7, the rule being ACT-004:s8.** I DIRECT that allocation be bound to route/record: load the `CitationRegistry` from the live persisted register, allocate-and-stamp the citation at authoring, and persist via `register()` so collisions are detected against real state and remain fatal. A self-asserted `citation:` field must be treated as a **fatal mismatch unless it equals the kernel-allocated value** — reconcile-at-write, which is exactly what would have caught the self-asserted [2026] VJS-DEC 15–22.

I make one express finding the case file invites at line 104–105 and which the floor requires me to confirm rather than wave through: **allocation must run *before* the assent gate, never after.** A citation is an identity-stamp, not a merits judgment; stamping it at authoring time means a later Sovereign-assented record arrives already-stamped and consistent, so the collision check never has occasion to fire *against* an assented record. This is the correct sequencing and it is the only sequencing consistent with ACT-010: the fatal collision policy of s8 operates on the *authoring* path, comfortably upstream of the assent floor, so the two never collide. I so direct.

**Question 3 — The jurisdiction-scope twin.** I ACCEPT FACT-3, and I make the structural finding the question presses for. The federation bright-line (`apex_routing_decision`, enforced per REG-FEDERATION-COORDINATION-001 and [2026] VJS-SC 4 D3) already catches one direction — a *subscribing* jurisdiction recording an *apex order* — and expressly excludes `lawpack/` as the read-only mirror. The uncovered inverse is the apex seat itself recording *subscriber-scoped content* (a decision/spec/invariant whose `scope.paths` or identity belongs to a named subscriber) into canon. On the law, this is the **same defect as FACT-1 seen from the federation side**: in both, the wrong is "subscriber-scoped content in canon," detected by inspecting the same structured fields against the same subscriber-identity predicate.

I therefore hold, on the merits of the fork the question poses, that **one consolidated canon-write gate (Q1+Q3) is the right structure, not two.** Two gates would duplicate the predicate, drift apart over time, and invite the precise side-door this matter prosecutes (a write that satisfies one gate's spelling of "subscriber path" but not the other's). The unifying rule — *no subscriber-scoped content enters canon, from any direction* — is one rule and deserves one gate. The thin working-root / `jurisdiction_id` check the case file describes, for the rare *true* cross-repo permit (an apex order legitimately reaching down under REG-FEDERATION-COORDINATION-001), is a narrow *exception path* on that single gate, not a second gate: where a valid Privy/Supreme cross-repo permit is present and the `jurisdiction_id` matches, the gate admits the write; absent that, subscriber-scoped content is Blocked (or, if assented, Warned-and-routed). I direct the consolidation accordingly, **as machinery under s.7, generalising `apex_routing_decision`'s bright-line to the inverse direction** — no new federation statute.

**Question 4 — Install completeness, the atomic manifest, and the PATH shim.** This question has three limbs and I take them in turn; the second and third are the genuine first-impression points on which I am asked to reason hardest in this section.

*4(a) — the install-completeness invariant (machinery, accept).* I ACCEPT the diagnosis. The install MUSTs are enacted (REG-INVOCATION-001: subscribe + lock-digest + `install_enforcement_hooks` + record an invocation; ACT-007:s1: `create_config_toml_on_install`; REG-ACCESSION-001: the fail-closed digest loader), and [2026] VJS-PC 6 has *already held* a hooks-missing install legally incomplete and ordered it perfected. What is missing is a gate that *verifies presence-and-activeness* of that surface at a governed write and at `validate --staged`. I DIRECT it, **as machinery under s.7, the rule being REG-INVOCATION-001, ACT-007:s1, and the ratio of [2026] VJS-PC 6**: a fail-closed install-completeness invariant fired at `pre_write` and at `validate --staged`, refusing to treat the jurisdiction as governed unless the REG-INVOCATION-001 surface is present and active. No fresh statute; PC 6 already supplies the binding ratio that an incomplete install is a defect to be perfected, and this gate is merely the deterministic detector PC 6 assumed but did not wire.

*4(b) — the atomic, signed/locked install MANIFEST (FIRST-IMPRESSION).* I find this a genuine first-impression point: REG-BUNDLE-001's atomic, fail-closed `verify` is, by its own terms, scoped to *cross-repo deployment bundles* assembled from separate repositories ("assemble from separate repos by sha256, never relocate"); it does not reach the *per-repo install surface*. There is therefore no instrument requiring the per-repo install to be an atomic, locked manifest of its required surface, and the question is open.

I dispose of it thus. The *pattern* is right and I adopt it; the *instrument* is not REG-BUNDLE-001 but a sibling of it. I DIRECT, **as machinery under s.7 (the form question, like REG-BUNDLE-001 itself, is machinery — it prescribes a FORM, enacts no new duty)**, that the install record the required surface as an atomic, digest-locked manifest, mirroring the lock pattern REG-ACCESSION-001 and REG-BUNDLE-001 already establish (`.vjs/lawpack.lock` and `bundle.lock` are its cousins; this is, naturally, an *install manifest* — call it what the registrar will, but it is one file, fail-closed, verified as a whole). On **which mechanisms are "required,"** I hold the manifest must enumerate exactly the surface the existing law already mandates, and no more, so that the manifest cannot become a back-door for adding obligations no statute imposes: (i) `.vjs/config.toml` (ACT-007:s1, the fixed discovery anchor); (ii) `.vjs/lawpack.lock` pinning the canon digest (REG-INVOCATION-001, REG-ACCESSION-001); (iii) the enforcement hooks, present and active (REG-INVOCATION-001 `install_enforcement_hooks`, perfected per [2026] VJS-PC 6); and (iv) the recorded `local_sovereign_invocation` (REG-INVOCATION-001). The manifest's value is *atomicity*: a half-installed surface (the exact failure mode PC 6 found) becomes a single fail-closed verify failure, not a silent partial state. The verify reuses REG-ACCESSION-001's fail-closed-on-first-violation discipline. I emphasise the s.7 limit: this manifest binds the *install ceremony's form*; it does not and may not enlarge the substantive duties, touch a bench, tier, jurisdiction, or the assent rule.

*4(c) — the PATH shim (FIRST-IMPRESSION + PRINCIPAL-DEPENDENCY).* This is the one point where I must flag a dependency outside the Realm's own machinery, and I do so squarely. The hooks deliberately invoke the kernel by **absolute path** and set `core.hooksPath`, which is bypass-proof *as to git operations* but means the `vjs` binary is invoke-by-full-path and not generally on `PATH`. A `~/.local/bin` shim would make `vjs` PATH-visible — but it **mutates host state outside the governed repo** (the user's home directory / shell environment), and the Realm's writ runs to the repo, not to the operator's machine.

I dispose of it as follows, and I am deliberately conservative. First, on the law: nothing in the lawpack legislates a PATH shim, and I will not invent a duty to mutate host state. ACT-007:s1 fixes `.vjs/config.toml` as the *in-repo* discovery point; REG-INVOCATION-001 is emphatic that binding flows from the in-repo invocation surface, "NOT by directory ancestry" and not by any host-layout assumption. A mandated host-environment mutation would sit uneasily against that in-repo-anchored philosophy. Second, on the engineering: the absolute-path hooks are, and must remain, **the bypass-proof backstop** — they are what actually enforces, and they do not depend on `PATH` at all. The shim is therefore a *convenience and discoverability* affordance, not an enforcement primitive; enforcement does not weaken by its absence.

I therefore DIRECT: (i) the absolute-path hooks remain the enforcement backstop and are **not** to be made contingent on any PATH shim — this is a decisive engineering call requiring no ruling beyond this confirmation; and (ii) the install ceremony **may offer** to place a PATH-visible `vjs` shim as a discoverability convenience, but because that step mutates host state outside the repo it is a **Principal-dependency**: it must be opt-in, surfaced to the Principal for authorisation, and never performed silently as a precondition of governed operation. I REFUSE to mandate the shim. Mandating it would (a) legislate a host-environment mutation the lawpack does not contemplate, and (b) risk creating an implied dependency of enforcement on `PATH`, which would *weaken* the bypass-proof backstop the absolute-path design exists to provide. The shim is a nicety the Principal may grant; the backstop is the law.

**Question 5 — The court-tier floor, and the block-vs-warn fork.** I ACCEPT the diagnosis: ACT-002:s2/s3/s4/s9 mandate routing each matter to its tier, and `choose_court()` already computes the required tier deterministically but only advisorily, never gated. A duty with no teeth. I DIRECT a tier-floor gate at order validate/record, **as machinery under s.7, the rule being ACT-002:s2/s3/s4**, that refuses an order whose `court:` is below the required tier and (per s4) flags one pitched too high, **citing the section in the denial** as REG-KERNEL-001 independently requires ("every denial names the instrument").

Now the fork I am specifically asked to decide: **HARD-block vs WARN-and-route, under the ACT-010 floor.** I hold the answer is **neither uniformly — it is conditioned on assent status, and the floor dictates the split**:

- **Where the order does *not* declare a valid Sovereign assent_source** (the ordinary case — a court order issuing from a bench, not a primary/constitutional Act): **HARD-block.** ACT-010 protects only *Sovereign-assented* records; an ordinary court order is outside its scope (its own s2 confirms it "confers no force on any record that lacks a valid assent_source"). Blocking an under-tiered ordinary order is lawful and correct: an order that issues from a court below its constitutionally required tier is *not validly constituted* in the first place, and refusing to record it is the kernel enforcing [2026] VJS-SC 2's jurisdictional fixity, not voiding a good record.

- **Where the order *does* declare a valid Sovereign assent_source:** **WARN-and-route-for-correction, never block.** This is the floor's command, not a discretion. ACT-010:s1 is absolute: such a record "may never be voided, excluded, or blocked," and its defects are "ALWAYS routed for correction." A tier-floor gate that *blocked* an assented record would be **void to that extent** under ACT-010 and ACT-CONSOLIDATION-FRAMEWORK:s25 (entrenchment), and I would strike it. So the gate must, on detecting an assented record below tier, emit a Warning, register the defect in the route-for-correction codes (per the assented-record-floor build discipline), and **route it for correction — surfacing the defect loudly while leaving the record standing.** ACT-010:s1's closing sentence is the authority: the prohibition "forbids only voiding and blocking; it does not prevent a defect being surfaced, flagged, or routed for correction."

I make the general finding, because it recurs at Q1, Q3, and Q8: **every gate this court directs must carry the same assent-conditioned verdict split.** Block (or its kin) for non-assented records; Warn-and-route for assented ones. A defect code must exist in the route-for-correction set for each, and **never** an Error/Fatal disposition on an assented record. This is not a softening of enforcement; it is the constitutionally mandated *form* enforcement must take when it meets the Sovereign's own assent. I so direct, and I would REFUSE any directive, here or elsewhere in this order, that would Block, void, or exclude a Sovereign-assented record for any defect whatever.

**Question 6 — Front-door wiring of the agent-runtime adapters.** I ACCEPT FACT-6: this is settled law (REG-SELF-CONVENE-001, REG-HOOKS-001, REG-KERNEL-001, DEC-AGENT-001, DEC-AGENT-HARNESS-001) and a built kernel (all five events implemented in `hook.rs`; `cmd_hook` exits non-zero on verdict). I direct it as machinery under s.7 / existing authority, with the express recording being for audit-traceability, not because a fresh rule is needed.

**Question 7 — Hook completeness.** I ACCEPT FACT-6's surface point. REG-HOOKS-001 and `hook.rs` already define all five events `[session_start, pre_write, post_action, pre_commit, pre_push]` with typed verdicts; install wires only `pre_commit`/`pre_push`. **Question 8 — Bench-integrity verification (FIRST-IMPRESSION on form).** I ACCEPT FACT-5, and I note with appropriate gravity that it failed *live* in the preparation of this very matter: an out-of-kernel deliberation silently lost a justice-seat to a transient model-capacity error and nothing detected the incomplete bench until manual transcript inspection. I add the now-familiar floor refinement: where (improbably) such an order carries a valid Sovereign assent_source, the verdict is Warn-and-route, not Block (ACT-010). For ordinary court orders — which is every order this gate will meet — Block is correct and lawful, because an order from an unconstituted or incomplete bench is *not a valid order* and refusing it is the kernel enforcing [2026] VJS-SC 2, not voiding a good record.

### III. Question 9 — the full-spectrum audit, and the reserved consolidating instrument

This is the question on which I am asked to reason hardest, and it is the one that most tests the discipline of this court. The eight gaps above are, as the case file rightly frames it, **one disease**: an existing substantive duty with no deterministic gate at the chokepoint. The tempting move — Position B — is to enact, now, a single omnibus "single front door, no other entry" regulation that declares no governed record may come into being except through the front door and that every substantive duty must be wired to a gate. I have weighed it carefully and I REJECT it, for reasons that go to the heart of what this matter is about.

**Position B would commit the very wrong it prosecutes.** The breach this court is remedying is *law (a citation, a record) brought into being without first passing through the discipline that should have produced it*. A consolidating constitutional rule — "no governed record except through the front door; wired-or-void" — is itself a governed record of the highest order. To bash it out *now*, before the Realm possesses a complete map of *which* substantive duties are wired and which are not, would be to legislate a sweeping conformance rule on an incomplete factual record: to assert "every duty is wired" (or must be, on pain of voidness) without knowing whether that is true or even achievable. That is bashing-law-out-of-thin-air dressed as its own cure. The remedy for ungrounded lawmaking must not itself be ungrounded. I hold that the **anti-bash-it-out discipline is itself a substantive value of this Realm**, traceable to the route-first/symmetric-case-file philosophy of REG-SELF-CONVENE-001 and the consolidation framework's insistence (s.4, s.20) that nothing acquires force by restatement or by silence, only by a deliberate, grounded, recorded act. A consolidating instrument grounded in a *complete conformance map* honours that discipline; one bashed out today betrays it.

There is a second, independent ground. A "wired-or-void" conformance rule, if enacted as a *primary or constitutional* rule, would collide head-on with two entrenched floors. **It cannot, consistent with ACT-010, render "void" any Sovereign-assented record that happens to declare a duty not yet wired** — the floor forbids voiding such records absolutely, and ACT-CONSOLIDATION-FRAMEWORK:s25 entrenches that against amendment by anything short of a Sovereign-assented Act citing s.10/s.21 by number. And **it cannot be enacted as machinery under s.7 at all if it purports to be constitutional**, because s.7 may not amend or expand the Act, the constitution, or the assent rule. So even on its own terms, the consolidating rule is a *primary/constitutional* instrument requiring Sovereign assent (ACT-CONSOLIDATION-FRAMEWORK:s10), carefully drafted to route-not-void assented records — which is all the more reason it must be *drafted with care on a complete record*, not improvised in this order. Position B cannot lawfully be done in the form it imagines, today.

I likewise REJECT **Position C** (fix only Q1/Q2/Q8, defer the rest). It under-reaches. The whole lesson of this matter is that *any* unwired duty is a side-door, and the next breach will come through whichever door we left unguarded. Fixing the three breach-vectors that happened to fire this time, while leaving Q4/Q5/Q6/Q7 unwired and ordering no audit, simply relocates the vulnerability and guarantees a sequel. C mistakes the symptom for the disease.

I therefore ADOPT **Position A**, and I confirm its sequencing as the correct discipline:

**Now (this order):** declare each of the eight matters an *enforcement of existing law* (not a new duty); direct the machinery/wiring fixes of Q1–Q8, each citing the duty it gives teeth to and each carrying the assent-conditioned Block-or-route verdict the ACT-010 floor requires; dispose the Q4 first-impression points (atomic install manifest: yes, as s.7 form-machinery, surface enumerated and bounded; PATH shim: not mandated, opt-in Principal-dependency, absolute-path backstop preserved) and the Q5 block-vs-warn design (assent-conditioned, as above); and **mandate a full-spectrum conformance audit** enumerating *every* substantive prohibition/duty in the lawpack and, for each, whether it is bound to a deterministic kernel gate — producing a conformance map of unwired duties. That audit is the factual record on which the consolidating instrument must be built.

**Reserved (a follow-on, grounded in the audit):** a consolidating **"single front door, no other entry"** instrument establishing that no governed record may come into being except through the front door, and that every substantive duty must be wired to a gate (a wired-or-void conformance rule). I reserve, rather than enact, it — *and I make three findings about its eventual form so the registrar is not later free to bash it out either*:

1. **It is a primary/constitutional instrument and requires Sovereign assent** (ACT-CONSOLIDATION-FRAMEWORK:s10). It may not be enacted as bare s.7 machinery, because a realm-wide "wired-or-void" rule reaching all governed records is constitutional in character.
2. **Its "void" limb is subordinate to ACT-010.** It may render unwired/improper records void *only where they lack a valid Sovereign assent_source*; an assented record is always routed-for-correction, never voided — drafted to that effect on its face, or it is void to that extent under s.25.

**Question 9 — Full-spectrum search and the consolidating front-door instrument.** This is the question I have reasoned hardest about, because it is where the court could itself commit the very sin it is prosecuting. The temptation of Position B is real: one omnibus regulation, done today, "single front door, no other entry," and the matter closed. I REFUSE Position B, and I want the reasons on the record because they are the ratio I would have this court adopt.

The breach that opened this matter was *law asserted into being without passing through the front door* - "[2026] VJS-DEC 15-22" minted from thin air. The structural remedy is "no governed record may come into being except through the front door." If this court were to *enact that consolidating rule now*, before the full-spectrum audit exists, it would be writing the master wired-or-void conformance rule *from the same thin air* - on the eight-gap sample in front of it, not on a complete enumeration of every substantive duty and its gate-status. A "single front door, no other entry" rule is only as good as its inventory of doors. Enact it on a partial inventory and one of two failures is guaranteed: either the rule is drafted narrowly to the eight gaps and silently leaves other unwired duties as undefended side-doors (Position C's defect, dressed up), or it is drafted broadly as "every duty must be wired or the record is void" and *that* is the more dangerous outcome, because a *void* conformance rule, switched on before the gates exist, would begin voiding records that the gates have not yet been built to clear - and if any such record carried a valid assent_source, the rule would collide head-on with the ACT-010 floor and be void *pro tanto* under ACT-009:s21. A consolidating rule that can void an assented record for want of a gate the kernel has not yet grown is not a front door; it is a trap. The discipline the case file proposes - **teeth and audit now, the consolidating instrument reserved and grounded in the completed audit** - is therefore not timidity. It is the only sequence that does not repeat the original sin. I ACCEPT Position A.

I also reject Position C (minimal: fix only Q1/Q2/Q8). It under-reaches on the law: Q4, Q5, Q6, Q7 each rest on an *already-enacted* duty with a missing gate, and to leave them unwired is to leave known side-doors open for the next breach, which is indefensible once the audit has named them. The court does not get to see a duty unwired and look away.

So, on Q9: (a) I MANDATE a full-spectrum conformance audit enumerating *every* substantive prohibition/duty in the lawpack (every must / must_not / prohibits in every in-force instrument) and recording, deterministically, whether each is bound to a kernel gate, producing a conformance map of unwired duties. This audit is itself a governed deliverable and must be produced *through* the kernel, not as an out-of-kernel workflow (the Q8 lesson applies to the audit too). (b) I RESERVE the consolidating "single front door, no other entry" instrument to a follow-on grounded in that audit. When it comes, I record now the two constitutional rails it must run between, so the registrar does not have to re-litigate them: it must be made under the s.7 power as subordinate law and may not amend any statute, bench, tier, or the assent rule (anti-Henry-VIII, ACT-009:s7/s25); and its wired-or-void conformance rule must be drafted so that "void" never reaches a record carrying a valid assent_source - for assented records the only disposition is route-for-correction (ACT-010:s1), which means in practice the consolidating rule bites at the *write/route* chokepoint (pre-assent) and degrades to routing post-assent. A front door that cannot void what the Sovereign has assented to is not a weaker front door; it is a constitutional one. (See D10, D11.)

**The unifying principle, stated for the record.** The eight gaps are one disease: an enacted substantive duty with no deterministic gate at the chokepoint. The cure is not more law - the law is largely complete and, in its protective-floor limbs, entrenched. The cure is to bind every duty to the single smart enforcement point (REG-KERNEL-001) so that no governed record - law, ruling, citation, order - can come into being except through the front door. The court's task today is to grow the teeth the law already grants, to take the complete census of doors, and to *withhold* the master lock until it can be cut to fit every door at once. That withholding is the discipline, not a delay.
