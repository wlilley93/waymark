I add one verified fact the parties did not foreground, because it is decisive for scope. The operative fields of a real order are not authored in a single form. The holding of [2026] VJS-PC 16 cites in canonical prose ("ACT-COMPUTER-FIRST-REALM s.23", "INV-ASSENT-SOURCE-001", and citation-form authorities), but its directives encode citations as lossy snake_case tokens: "act_computer_first_realm_s23", "act_010_s2", "inv_assent_source_001", "reg_kernel_001". Q1 ADOPT. Disposition: ADOPT, as machinery, not a new duty.

A deterministic, model-free gate that requires every authority, section, and citation an order leans on in its operative parts to resolve to a real, defined object in canon is giving teeth to an existing doctrine, not minting a new one. The Vibe Procedure Rules already hold that only the ratio binds and that a ruling made in ignorance of binding law is per incuriam and void. A ratio cannot rest on an authority that does not exist; the doctrine presumes the cited law is real. The gate adds nothing to what an order must do. It does not require an order to cite more, to cite better on the merits, or to reason differently. It requires only that the authorities the order has itself chosen to cite in its operative parts actually exist and are defined. That is structural enforcement of a precondition the doctrine already assumes, and it is machinery under ACT-CONSOLIDATION-FRAMEWORK s.7: it changes no word of any Act, no bench, no tier, and no assent rule, in the same posture REG-KERNEL-001 describes for itself ("additive machinery; it amends no statute, no bench, no tier, and no assent rule").

But I fix the reach of the teeth now, so they are not over-claimed in the build. Per incuriam has three limbs: the cited authority must exist, the order must state it faithfully, and the ignored or mis-stated authority must be material to the result. A deterministic clerk can answer only the first. "Does ACT-COMPUTER-FIRST-REALM:s23 exist and is it defined" is a table lookup. "Does s.23 say what the bench claims" is reading, and "would the error have changed the holding" is judging the reasoning. Those last two are merits, reserved to the bench's duty of care and to a court on appeal, and forbidden to the clerk by REG-KERNEL-001. The hand-verification the integrating writer performed for [2026] VJS-PC 16 spanned all three limbs; the gate I adopt reaches only the first. That is real teeth against the hallucinated and the never-existed citation, and it must be sold as nothing more.

Q2 DISPOSITION. Disposition: CORRECTABLE, not constitutive. I adopt Position B by name.

This is the question that decides the matter, and the answer turns on a single line: the clerk may find that a cited section does not EXIST, but the clerk may not conclude that the order is therefore VOID. Existence is structural. Voidness for per incuriam is a merits conclusion.

Why the constitutive route (Position A) is refused. Every member of the constitutive family shares a property that citation-grounding does not. A bench defect, a citation collision, an apex order in the wrong jurisdiction: each is a defect whose consequence is structural and automatic. A bench-less order simply is not an order; you need read no reasoning to know it. There is no materiality inquiry, no inspection of the ratio. Per incuriam is different in kind. A missing or hallucinated citation does not void an order automatically; it voids the order only if the bad authority was binding and material, and materiality cannot be found without judging whether the holding stands or falls on it. For the clerk to brand the order "void ab initio" on the bare fact that a section does not resolve is for the clerk to presume the very merits conclusion REG-KERNEL-001 forbids it to reach. The doctrine itself, recited in the submission, locates the voidness in a holding: "a ruling made in ignorance of binding law is per incuriam and void." That holding is for a court. The kernel is the clerk, not the court (REG-KERNEL-001; ACT-002:s9 reserves variation and overruling to a new and a Supreme order respectively). The clerk finds the fact; the court draws the conclusion.

There is a second, independent reason A fails, and it is the assent floor. The constitutive family is the only class that overrides Sovereign assent: those codes are never softened even for a resolving assented record, because assent to a non-thing is incoherent ("a false signature is no signature", per [2026] VJS-PC 16). The test for membership is therefore: could the Sovereign coherently have assented to THIS as an order of its kind? For a bench-less order, no: the signature would be on a phantom court. For a real, convened, odd-numbered bench that issued a real opinion and then cited a hallucinated s.99 in a directive, yes: the Sovereign can perfectly well assent to a genuine court's genuine order that contains a citation error, and the error is then corrected. The defect lives in the order's content and reasoning, which is exactly the domain VJS-ACT 10 s.1 was entrenched to protect by routing for correction and never voiding. To fold citation-grounding into the constitutive family would let the machine block an assented order over a content defect, which is the machine enlarging its own override power beyond identity defects: ultra vires the s.7 anti-Henry-VIII limit and a breach of the entrenched floor.

The disposition, therefore. An unresolved operative citation is a Fatal but CORRECTABLE defect, bifurcated by assent exactly as the ordinary downgradeable Fatals are. A non-assented order, or one whose declared assent does not resolve under [2026] VJS-PC 16, fails closed: it does not enter canon until the citation is corrected. An order whose assent resolves (and note that the ordinary binding order carries standing_bounded_assent, which resolves once the Realm's Sovereign foundation exists) is routed for correction, never voided or blocked: the defect is surfaced loudly, a correction obligation attaches, the citation is fixed and the order re-recorded. The clerk's verdict reads "this operative part cites X, which resolves to no defined instrument in canon; correct the citation and re-record", and never "this order is void." That branding is for a court on appeal. This is the maximal teeth the constitution permits against an assented record and strictly more than canon has today, while it fails closed on the genuinely dangerous case, the hand-asserted draft order with a phantom authority and no real assent, which is the citation-grounding analogue of the forgery [2026] VJS-PC 16 killed.

Q3 SCOPE. Disposition: operative parts at section granularity, with three mandatory carve-outs and one hard distinction.

Operative parts are holding, directives, and forbidden clauses (all of which bind). The explanatory fields, issue, question, vote, runtime_summary, the source_opinion path, and case_file_digest, are out of scope, because only the ratio binds and a stray reference in a narrative recital is not load-bearing. Granularity is section level: normalize the forms ":sN", " s.N", and " s N" to one canonical section key and require BOTH the instrument AND the named section to be defined, so a real Act cited at a section it does not contain is unresolved. That is precisely the defect [2026] VJS-PC 16's writer guarded by hand.

Carve-outs that must not trip the gate:

1. A negated mention. "no DEC-X", "no longer DEC-X", "not DEC-X", "without DEC-X" is a statement about absence, not a reliance the ratio rests on. The existing check's ends_with("no") test is too thin; detect negation robustly on the token boundary.

2. The order's own forward self-reference. At validate time the staged order is not yet in committed canon, so a naive lookup would false-positive on an order that cites its own id or citation (as D6-style directives routinely do). The order's own id and own citation always resolve; compare against the staged record's own fields.

3. A reference to a genuinely superseded-but-historical instrument. Here is the hard distinction the submission asks for. "Cites a section that never existed" resolves to nothing in canon: that is the hallucination, and it fails closed (for a non-assented order) or routes for correction (for an assented one). "Cites a real section that was later repealed or superseded" resolves to a defined object whose status is superseded or repealed: it EXISTED, and citing the authority that governed at the material time, or that the order is distinguishing, is ordinary and legitimate legal work. It must NOT fail closed. The clerk may note structurally that the instrument is not in force (a non-blocking advisory), but whether reliance on historical law is apt is a merits question reserved to the bench. So the fail-close trigger is "resolves to nothing", never "resolves to something no longer in force". This is why Q1's "in-force" requirement is, in the disposition, split: existence fails closed, in-force-ness is advisory only.

Q4 SEVERITY. Disposition: Fatal-and-assent-bifurcated for orders, reconciled with the lawpack-wide Warning so each defect is reported once, precisely.

Two checks coexist without collision. The lawpack-wide DANGLING_REFERENCE stays a non-blocking corpus drift Warning, but its line-wrap false positive is fixed at the root: scan parsed scalar values (or join continuations) rather than physical content.lines(), and match ids on token boundaries (the boundary discipline already proven in assent.rs::names_token), so a soft-wrapped id can never present a head fragment as a phantom. The new order gate emits a distinct code, ORDER_CITATION_UNRESOLVED, at Fatal severity, scoped to a staged order's operative parts, registered as NOT constitutive so the assent floor downgrades it for a resolving order. De-duplication is mandatory: compute the order-gate findings first and suppress any DANGLING_REFERENCE Warning for the same (order-file, id) pair, so a real defect is reported exactly once, at the order gate's higher precision, and never twice.

The honest limit, recorded as [2026] VJS-PC 16 recorded its remainder. The gate reliably grounds canonical-form references, which is the holding's idiom. It cannot reliably ground the lossy snake_case tokens in which directives are presently authored ("act_010_s2" does not mechanically resolve to ACT-ASSENTED-RECORD-PROTECTION:s2). The cure is to express operative citations in a machine-resolvable form. The recommended mechanism, which I direct as the preferred path because it is additive, reversible, and already proven in the schema, is an optional structured cites/authorities list of AuthorityId on the order, exactly as supersedes is already a resolvable AuthorityId list checked by ORPHAN_SUPERSESSION, with prose kept for humans. Until that lands, the gate's teeth reach the holding and any canonically-cited operative text, and the snake_case directive bodies are a known, recorded gap, not a silent one.

Positions. Position A (build it, constitutive) is REJECTED: it has the clerk presume the per-incuriam voidness conclusion, which is merits, and it would let the machine override Sovereign assent on a content defect, enlarging the constitutive family beyond identity defects and offending s.7 and VJS-ACT 10 s.1. Position C (do not build) is REJECTED on the merits but its concerns are adopted as binding conditions: a non-blocking Warning does not stop a per-incuriam order entering canon, which is the very gap the hand-recording of [2026] VJS-PC 16 exposed, and the false-positive and over-reach risks C names are answered by the carve-outs of Q3 and the precision and de-dup mandate of Q4 rather than by declining the gate. Position B (build it, correctable) is ADOPTED.

Coke J., Privy Council

This is my independent opinion on the Reference re Citation Grounding. I have read the symmetric case file in full and verified the cited law and code for myself rather than on the parties' word. I write as one seat of the constituted bench of three; the other seats deliver their own opinions and the order records our collective vote.

The matter is a real hole, and the parties describe it accurately. The bench-integrity gate verifies that an order was issued by a real court of the constituted size, each seat carrying a real opinion. It does not read a single word of the law the order cites. So an order whose holding or directive names an authority that resolves to no defined instrument, a hallucinated "s.99" or a mis-cited Act, passes validate and enters canon, even though our settled doctrine is that a ruling made in ignorance of binding law is per incuriam and void. The integrating writer caught exactly this by hand when recording VJS-PC 16, manually confirming that ACT-COMPUTER-FIRST-REALM s.23 existed and said what the bench claimed. A duty discharged by hand on alertness is a duty the kernel should discharge deterministically. I would adopt a gate. The entire weight of the case, however, lies in HOW it sits with the two hard limits, and on that I part company with the constitutive position.

Q1, ADOPT. I hold that the kernel should ground an order's operative citations, and that doing so is machinery, not a new substantive duty. The gate I would build asks one structural question of each authority and case-citation token appearing in an order's operative parts: does this token resolve to an object the corpus actually defines? That is a pure table lookup over `defined_ids` and the live citator register, of exactly the kind the kernel already performs for bench size, citation uniqueness, and (per VJS-PC 16) assent resolution. It reads structure, not meaning. It gives teeth to a duty that already exists, the requirement that the law an order rests on be real law, which is implicit in the per-incuriam doctrine and in REG-KERNEL-001's command that every denial name a real instrument. It amends no statute, no bench, no tier, and no assent rule, so it falls within the statutory-instrument power of ACT-CONSOLIDATION-FRAMEWORK s.7. What it must NOT become is a duty to reason WELL. Whether a holding is correct, whether the cited authority truly supports the proposition, whether the bench weighed it rightly, all of that is merits, and REG-KERNEL-001 forbids the clerk-kernel to deliberate, draft, or judge merits. The gate I adopt checks existence, full stop. It does not check soundness.

I draw one line within Q1 that the submission's framing ("real, defined, in-force") blurs and that the hard limits force me to sharpen. EXISTENCE and IN-FORCE are not the same test. Whether a cited id is DEFINED anywhere in the corpus is structural and clerk-safe. Whether it is currently IN FORCE is a different matter, because an order may legitimately cite a superseded enactment, to overrule it, to distinguish it, or to narrate why it no longer binds. A gate that fails closed on "you cited a repealed section" would block a valid order for doing exactly what a court is supposed to do, and policing whether reliance on historical law was appropriate is merits. So the constitutive limb of the gate is EXISTENCE only. In-force status, at most, drives an advisory note, never a block.

Q2, DISPOSITION. This is where I reason hardest, and I reject the constitutive characterisation. The disposition is route-for-correction, not void ab initio. My reasons are two, and each is independently sufficient.

First, the clerk limit. There is a difference between detecting that a cited id does not resolve, which is structural, and concluding that the order is therefore per incuriam and void, which is not. Per incuriam voidness depends on whether the holding DEPENDS on the bad citation. A bench may cite five authorities, four sound and one mistyped, with the ratio standing firmly on the four; or the phantom citation may sit in an explanatory clause carrying no weight; or "s.99" may be a slip for "s.9". The clerk cannot tell load-bearing from surplusage without reading and weighing the reasoning, and weighing the reasoning is precisely the merits judgment REG-KERNEL-001 reserves to the bench and to a court on appeal. To presume voidness from the bare structural signal is to have the clerk decide the very question, did the error infect the result, that defines an appellate function. The clerk grounds the citation; the court judges the consequence. That separation keeps the kernel in its lane.

Second, and decisively, the entrenched floor and the lawful route. VJS-ACT 10 s.1 commands that a Sovereign-assented record may NEVER be voided or blocked by subordinate validation; its defects are ALWAYS routed for correction. VJS-PC 16 carved the only exception, the constitutive family, and was scrupulous that every member of it, bench size, silent seat, missing opinion, tier, apex-singleness, citation collision, goes to whether the thing IS an order at all, never to the quality of its reasoning. A hallucinated internal citation does not go to record-hood. The order issued from a real bench, carries a real opinion, bears every required field; it is an order, with a bad citation in it. That is a defect in the content of the reasoning, not in the record's standing as a record. The instructive contrast is citation COLLISION, which IS constitutive: two records claiming "[2026] VJS-PC 16" is a defect in the record's IDENTITY as a canonical object, incoherent independent of any reasoning. A hallucinated reference INSIDE the holding is a defect in the reasoning's content. Identity is constitutive; reliance is merits.

It follows that treating a hallucinated citation as constitutive-void cannot be done by machinery at all, and this is the point I most want on the record. To add such a code to the never-shelter family would let the kernel BLOCK a Sovereign-assented order for a defect that does not go to its record-hood. That carves a new exception into the entrenched VJS-ACT 10 floor, and ACT-CONSOLIDATION-FRAMEWORK s.7 (anti-Henry-VIII) and s.25 (entrenchment of the assent floor) reserve any such narrowing to a Sovereign-assented Act citing the provision by number. A statutory instrument that narrowed the floor would be ultra vires and void to that extent. So Position A is not merely wrong on principle; it is beyond the power of the machinery this reference may authorise. The most the kernel may lawfully build is the correctable gate. If the Realm ever wishes to make a phantom citation a record-killing defect immune to assent, that is the Sovereign's prerogative, on notice, by express Act; the cure I direct is effective now and is in no way conditional on it.

The interaction with an assented order therefore resolves cleanly. A non-assented order that cites a phantom authority in its operative parts fails closed, the ordinary write-gate holding the draft out of canon until the citation is fixed; that is correction with teeth, not a declaration of voidness. An assented order that resolves under VJS-PC 16 and carries the same defect has the finding SURFACED and is ROUTED FOR CORRECTION, and commits; the floor forbids blocking it. In neither case does the clerk pronounce the order per incuriam. That pronouncement is for a court on appeal, on the full reasoning, which is where our doctrine has always placed it.

Q3, SCOPE. The gate runs on the operative parts, the holding and the directives, which are what bind under ACT-002:s7 ("orders bind; opinions explain"). The issue line, the vote, and the runtime summary are explanatory or descriptive and are out of the fail-closed scope. Granularity is section level: the token must match at "ID:sN" precision, so that an order citing ACT-002:s99 where ACT-002 exists but has no s99 is caught, the section being the unresolved object even though the parent Act resolves. `defined_ids` already inserts both the statute id and each section id, so the resolution is available. The gate must read the PARSED operative strings, not raw YAML lines; once the parser has reassembled a folded scalar, "REG-FEDERATION-\nCOORDINATION-001" is a single logical token, and the line-wrap partial that troubles the existing check cannot arise. Three carve-outs must not trip the gate:

A negated mention. "No DEC-X", "there is no such s.99", a forbidden-style prohibition, is a statement that something does not or should not apply, not reliance on it. The existing check already attempts this with an "ends with no" heuristic; for a fail-closed gate the safe direction on ambiguity is to treat a plausible negation as a carve-out, because the dangerous error here is a false positive that blocks a valid order.

A genuinely superseded-but-historical instrument. A citation to a real enactment whose status is Superseded, Overruled, Revoked, or Spent is still a citation to a DEFINED object; it is in `defined_ids` and so passes the existence gate and is never blocked. This is the concrete payoff of confining the constitutive test to existence rather than in-force status, and it is exactly how the gate distinguishes "cites a section that never existed", absent from `defined_ids`, which is the defect, from "cites a real section later repealed", present in `defined_ids` with a non-live status, which is not. At most the latter earns an advisory Warning flagging a possible per-incuriam point for human or appellate attention, in the manner of the existing `subject_tier_advisory`, and never a block.

The order's own forward self-reference. An order that names its own id or its own allocated citation, including a directive that speaks of "the resulting order", must resolve against itself. The gate's resolved set must include the staged order's own id and its allocated citation, because at write time the record being added is not yet in the loaded corpus. A self-reference always resolves.

I add that the grounding must cover case-citation tokens ("[YYYY] VJS-SERIES N" and the subscriber form) as well as instrument ids, because the phantom-precedent risk ("[2026] VJS-PC 99", a ruling that was never made) is as real as the phantom-section risk, and the existing referential check covers neither order ids nor citations. For a case citation, "resolves" means it is a citation actually claimed by a defined order in canon, or the staged order's own allocated citation, a pure lookup over the citator register.

Q4, SEVERITY. The new gate and the existing Warning are reconciled by scope, severity, and dedup, so that they are precise and do not double-report. The existing `DANGLING_REFERENCE` stays a non-blocking Warning over the whole corpus; its remedy is lawmaking, and it must be hardened so the line-wrap partial no longer fires, by resolving over reassembled scalars rather than physical lines (and, as a fallback, suppressing a match that is an exact prefix of a defined id continued on the next physical line). The new gate is narrower and sharper: it runs on a staged order's operative parts, parsed structurally, section-granular, with the three carve-outs. Its severity bifurcates exactly as the assent architecture requires. A non-assented order with an unresolved operative citation produces a Fatal finding (a new code, ORDER_CITATION_UNRESOLVED) that fails the write closed pending correction. An assented order that resolves under VJS-PC 16 has the same finding surfaced and ROUTED FOR CORRECTION, and commits, never blocked. **Q1 (ADOPT). Yes, but cut precisely to the existence of cited law, and no further.**

The per incuriam doctrine is settled and recited across our law (F3): a ruling made in ignorance of binding law is void. The doctrine already exists. The only live question is whether a model-free gate that checks whether the authorities an order cites in its operative parts actually RESOLVE gives that doctrine teeth (machinery, amending nothing) or invents a new substantive duty.

It gives teeth, provided it is cut at the right joint. Per incuriam has two failure modes, and they are not alike. The first is structural: the order cites an authority that does not exist, or mis-numbers a section that was never enacted (a hallucinated s.99, a mis-cited Act). Whether a cited section EXISTS in the canon, and in what status, is a pure table lookup over digest-pinned law: no model, no reading, no weighing. The second is substantive: the order cites a real, in-force section that does not SAY what the bench claims, or overlooks contrary binding law. To find that, one must read the law and the reasoning and decide whether the bench got it right and whether the overlooked law was binding and outcome-determinative. That is the per incuriam MERITS, and it belongs to a bench on appeal.

The gate may do the first and must never do the second. I therefore adopt the gate ONLY as an EXISTENCE-and-status check, and I refuse, as forbidden by REG-KERNEL-001, any limb that would have the clerk judge whether the cited authority supports the holding. Cut that way, the gate creates no new duty: the duty to rest a binding order on real, binding law already lives in the per incuriam rule and in ACT-002:s7 (orders bind at runtime; the kernel applies them). It amends no statute, no bench, no tier, no assent rule. It is the same character as the CITATION_COLLISION teeth already built under ACT-004:s8, and the DANGLING_REFERENCE check already standing. It is machinery under ACT-CONSOLIDATION-FRAMEWORK:s7, within the anti-Henry-VIII limit, and routes nothing to the Sovereign.

**Q2 (DISPOSITION). CORRECTABLE, not constitutive. Position B. This is the hardest point and I reason it to the floor.**

Position A says an unresolved operative citation makes the order per incuriam and void ab initio, Fatal, never softened by assent, in the bench-integrity family. It is wrong on both hard limits, and the reason is a single distinction the submission elides.

Bench-integrity is constitutive because its voidness is automatic and merits-free: if no constituted bench sat, there was NO COURT, and a non-court cannot issue any order at all. The consequence follows mechanically from the absence, with zero weighing. "No assent makes a bench-less order a real order" because a false signature is no signature. The defect is in the record's OWN identity and standing.

A citation defect is different in kind. A real, constituted bench DID sit and DID decide. The thing IS an order issued by a court, with its bench, its opinions, its digest, its own citation. The defect is not in whether a court spoke; it is in the CONTENT of what the court pointed at. And here is the decisive line: "therefore the order is per incuriam and VOID" is not a structural fact like counting seats. It is a CONCLUSION OF LAW that a court reaches on appeal after deciding the overlooked or mis-cited authority was binding and would have changed the result. A hallucinated section in one directive may be a clerical slip that never touched the ratio (ACT-002:s9 calls clerical citation errors expressly correctable), or it may be the load-bearing authority for the whole holding. To presume voidness from the bare fact of non-resolution is to have the CLERK reach the per incuriam conclusion. That is the merits judgment REG-KERNEL-001 forbids. The clerk may detect the existence defect; it may not adjudge the consequence.

So the clean boundary is this. Constitutive defects go to the record's OWN identity, standing, or composition: its bench, its tier, its apex-singleness, its own unique citation number (CITATION_COLLISION destroys the record's name in the register, mechanically, with no merits weighing). Correctable defects go to the record's CONTENT: what it says, what it claims the law is, what it cites. A hallucinated authority is a content defect. The collision is a defect in the order's name; the dangling citation is a defect in what the order names. That line is principled and it is the line PC-16 itself drew between the assent SIGNATURE (the floor's entry condition) and the order's other defects (routed for correction).

The assent interaction confirms it, and this is where Position A breaks the entrenched floor outright. If citation-grounding were constitutive, a citation defect in a Sovereign-ASSENTED order, an order issued by a real bench under real assent, would be Fatal and block the record. But VJS-ACT 10 s.1 forbids exactly that: an assented record's NON-constitutive defects must ALWAYS route for correction, never void or block. An assented order's standing is intact (real bench, real assent); only its content is defective. To block it would void a Sovereign-assented record for a non-constitutive defect, breaching the entrenched floor that may be amended only by the Sovereign citing the Act by number. Position A would have the machine do what only the Sovereign may authorise. I refuse it on that ground as well as on the merits-judgment ground.

The disposition that honours BOTH limits is Position B, refined:

- For a NON-assented order, an operative citation that resolves to no defined instrument at all is Fatal and fails the write closed, routed for correction, exactly as ORDER_MALFORMED already is. This is not the clerk pronouncing the order void; it is the clerk declining to FILE a record that cites an authority the canon does not contain, the same ordinary gatekeeping the lawpack-wide check already performs, only sharpened and fail-closed for an order's binding ratio. The per incuriam VOIDNESS, if anyone presses it, is for a court on appeal.
- For an ASSENTED order, the same finding routes for correction and is never blocked or voided, because the code is NON-constitutive and the existing VJS-ACT 10 downgrade path at lib.rs ~241 bites automatically. The floor is preserved by construction.

Does fail-closing a non-assented order judge merits? No. Refusing to record a citation to ACT-FOO:s99 when there is no such section is a referential check, not a semantic one. The clerk says "the canon contains no such section; resolve the reference before this is recorded," never "your reasoning is wrong." That is squarely the clerk's role.

**Q3 (SCOPE). Operative parts, section granularity, three carve-outs, and one sharp distinction.**

Scope is the operative ratio that binds at runtime under ACT-002:s7: the holding, the directives (each directive's must text), and forbidden (the binding prohibitions). It is NOT the issue tag, the runtime_summary recital, the vote, or the source_opinion, all of which are recital or explanation, where historical and negated mentions naturally live and where a gate would over-reach. Granularity is the section: "ACT-002:s99" must resolve to a DEFINED section id, not merely to ACT-002 existing, and defined_ids already contains section ids, so the lookup supports it.

The carve-outs that must NOT trip the gate:

1. Negated mention. An id governed by a negation or absence operator ("no DEC-X", "must_not create", "never", "without", "absent", "no such") is a statement ABOUT absence, not reliance on authority. Generalise the existing ends_with("no") carve-out.

2. Superseded-but-historical. A superseded, overruled, revoked, or spent instrument is still a real enactment: it is DEFINED in the corpus, merely not in force. An order legitimately cites it historically ("supersedes X", "overruling Y", "carried forward from V1 source W"). Such a citation resolves on EXISTENCE alone and is never put to the in-force test.

3. Forward self-reference. At the write gate the staged order is not yet in committed canon, so an order that cites its own id or own citation in a directive ("record this order as [2026] VJS-PC NN") would false-flag as dangling. Add the staged order's own id and own citation to the resolved set before checking, so it resolves to itself.

The sharp distinction the case file asks for, between "cites a section that never existed" and "cites a real section later repealed," is the hinge that keeps the clerk out of the merits. Non-existence is structural and fail-closed (it is the hallucination the gate exists to stop). Not-in-force, of a section that genuinely exists, is NOT a fail-closed defect: it may be a legitimate historical reference, and to decide that a not-in-force citation was relied on as LIVE authority is precisely the per incuriam merits read the clerk may not perform. Therefore a citation that resolves to a defined-but-not-in-force instrument earns at most an advisory Warning surfacing it for confirmation, never a block, for assented and non-assented orders alike. This is how the "in-force" requirement in Q1 is honoured: in-force is an advisory refinement, not a hard gate, because the only hard, clerk-safe fact is existence.

**Q4 (SEVERITY). Fail-closed for orders on non-existence; the lawpack-wide DANGLING_REFERENCE stays a Warning; reconcile by one shared matcher and de-duplication; cure the partial-id class.**

The two checks have different jobs and must keep them. DANGLING_REFERENCE is a lawpack-wide drift survey over everything, including commentary and recital, where a dangling id is often legitimate drift and the remedy is lawmaking, not a blocked commit: it stays a Warning. The new gate is narrow: an order's operative parts only, at that order's write, fail-closed Fatal for a non-existent citation in a non-assented order, route-for-correction for an assented one.

To prevent the same defect being reported twice at two severities with two contradictory remedies, the two must share ONE id-extraction and whole-token matcher (single source of truth), and the order-operative gate OWNS the operative region of order files: DANGLING_REFERENCE de-duplicates by suppressing any finding whose id and order-operative-region the new gate already adjudicates, and continues to survey everything else unchanged.

The partial-id false positive (the REG-FEDERATION line-wrap) is cured deterministically and in BOTH checks by the shared matcher: match against the PARSED field VALUES (folded scalars reassembled by serde), not raw physical lines; and match whole, maximal id tokens using the boundary discipline already implemented in assent.rs names_token, where '-' counts as an id character, so a prefix of a longer hyphenated id can never match. No model, no edge-case guesswork.
