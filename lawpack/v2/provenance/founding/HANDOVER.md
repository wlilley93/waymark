## The constitutional relay

V1 supplies the lawful migration process. V2 receives the settlement as its founding authority. After commencement, V2 governs itself through its computer-first lawpack, kernel, courts, orders, specs, invariants, permits, proofs, and logs.

The V1 process is migration provenance, not daily runtime law.

> V1 completes the lawful handover. V2 receives the handover as founding provenance. The single Gazette preserves the thread. The V2 lawpack governs runtime.

## Part 1 - The three migration layers

Everything that crosses from V1 to V2 lands in exactly one of three layers:

### Layer 1: Provenance

The proof of lawful birth. Records the V1 route: void first draft, Committee referral, Privy Council reference, Committee drafting, Privy Council ruling, Supreme Court settlement (if escalated), Sovereign consultation, Sovereign Assent, commencement.

This layer is **not runtime law**. It is the chain of authority showing that V2 was lawfully founded.

### Layer 2: Gazette continuity

The public memory of the Realm. One Gazette, two estates. V1 is the Archive estate. V2 is the Current computer-first estate. The Gazette shows the continuous thread from V1 to V2. Publication does not create runtime force.

### Layer 3: Runtime law

The compact, schema-valid, machine-checkable records that the V2 kernel loads, validates, and applies. Only the final settled outcomes enter this layer, expressed as V2 statutes, regulations, rules, specs, decisions, invariants, orders, permits, proofs, and logs.

### 2.3 V1 findings that the Committee must resolve

The Committee referral identified these issues that must be resolved before enactment:

**A. Bootstrapping.** The Act cannot commence without V2 infrastructure. Resolution: the Act is enacted first as a declaratory instrument; commencement is deferred until the V2 lawpack validates and the founding lock is recorded.

**B. Citation scheme.** The V2 `VJS-ACT` scheme is novel. The Act itself establishes it (as Bill 16 established `REALM-*`). V1 citations remain valid historical identifiers.

**C. Bill 27 conflict.** Section 24 of the Act states V2 does not carry forward the four-branch directory. Bill 27 mandates it. Resolution: the Act expressly supersedes Bill 27 as runtime machinery while preserving it as V1 archive law.

**D. Court continuity.** Supersession of V1 legal system does not automatically dissolve the courts. The V2 authority hierarchy references Supreme Court, Privy Council, and County Court orders. Resolution: courts continue by constitutional carry-forward; separate V2 court-establishment provisions may follow.

**E. Companion records.** The void first draft's companion YAML records (decision, invariant, court order) are premature. They reference an unenacted Act. The purported court order cites a non-existent V2 jurisdiction and is void ab initio. Resolution: companion records are created in the V2 lawpack after commencement.

**F. Single Gazette.** One Gazette, two estates. V1 is Archive; V2 is Current. Publication does not create runtime force. V2 force derives from the lawpack, not the Gazette.

**G. Constitutional relay.** V1 supplies the migration process; V2 receives the settlement. After commencement, V2 uses V2-native lawmaking, not V1's full machinery.

**H. Supreme Court handover.** The Supreme Court should issue an express handover order upon commencement.
